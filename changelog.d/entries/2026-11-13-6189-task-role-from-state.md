- **The bare `local run-task --assume-task-role` assumes the deployed task role under `--from-state` / `--from-cfn-stack` (issue [#6189](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6189))** -- `src/local/ecs-task-resolver.ts`, `src/local/ecs-task-resolver/{role-arn,needs,target,types}.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-run-task/{command,image-context,stack-account}.ts`, unit tests and docs. Before, a `TaskRoleArn` that references a same-stack `AWS::IAM::Role` always became `arn:aws:iam::<account>:role/<RoleLogicalId>`, even with a state source. A role CDK names for you is deployed as `<Stack>-<LogicalId>-<suffix>`, so AssumeRole asked for a role that does not exist and failed. Now, with a state source, cdkd reads the role's deployed ARN from the stack's record: the recorded `Arn` under `--from-state`, and the role name CloudFormation reports (plus the template's literal `Path`) under `--from-cfn-stack`. The state record is read for this only when the bare flag is passed. In AWS Emulator mixed mode, where the role is assumed in real AWS, the record is used only when IAM is a `"real-aws"` exception under `Parameters`. Otherwise cdkd keeps the logical-ID ARN and prints a warning that names the cause and the remedy. The logical-ID ARN now takes its partition from your identity, as it already took the account, instead of always `aws`.
