- **The bare `local run-task --assume-task-role` assumes the deployed task role under `--from-state` / `--from-cfn-stack` (issue [#6189](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6189))** -- `src/local/ecs-task-resolver/{role-arn,needs,target,types}.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-run-task/{command,image-context,stack-account}.ts`, unit tests and docs. Before, a `TaskRoleArn` that references a same-stack `AWS::IAM::Role` always became `arn:aws:iam::<account>:role/<RoleLogicalId>`, even with a state source. A role CDK names for you is deployed as `<Stack>-<LogicalId>-<suffix>`, so AssumeRole asked for a role that does not exist and failed. Now, with a state source, cdkd reads the role's deployed ARN from the stack's record: the recorded `Arn` under `--from-state`, and the role name CloudFormation reports under `--from-cfn-stack`. The state record is read for this only when the bare flag is passed. Without a state source, or when the record has no entry for the role, cdkd keeps the logical-ID ARN and prints a warning naming the remedy: a state source, or the ARN passed explicitly.
