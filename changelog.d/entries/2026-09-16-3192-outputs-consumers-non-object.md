- **`cdkd orphan`, `import` and `scrub` refuse a state record whose `outputs` bag is not an object instead of saving a rebuilt one over it; `cdkd scrub` exits 2 when a producer it imports from is unreadable; and the shared export-set predicate fails closed (issue [#3192](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3192))** -- `src/types/state.ts`, `src/state/{malformed-resources-bag,export-index-store}.ts`, `src/cli/commands/{orphan,import,scrub,diff-recursive}.ts`, four `docs/` pages, `docs/design/3192-outputs-consumers.md`, cases across 10 test suites plus a new `tests/unit/state/importable-outputs-malformed.test.ts`. Sibling sweep of [#3189](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3189), which guarded `cdkd diff` alone. Two WRITE paths, confirmed by execution: `cdkd orphan` rebuilt `outputs: 'abcdef'` into `{"0":"a",..}` and SAVED it, destroying the only signal the record was damaged; and a string bag published one fabricated export per character into the shared `exports.json`. Such loads now REFUSE (`STATE_RESOURCES_MALFORMED`), naming the broken container. **Exit-code change**: a `cdkd scrub` whose CONSUMER is healthy but whose PRODUCER record cannot be read now raises `SCRUB_PRODUCER_RECORD_UNREADABLE` and exits **2** — with or without `--fail`, `--dry-run` included — rather than exiting 0 over a stack whose imported plaintext it could not verify (`docs/cli-scrub.md` carries the table). The `exportNames` FIELD is a further site: a hand-edited non-array killed `cdkd diff` with a raw `TypeError`, and now reads as an EMPTY export set, never the pre-v9 every-key rule re-opening [#2193](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2193)'s shadowing; `cdkd diff` WARNS, naming stack and region. The same predicate covers the deploy resolver and `cdkd local`. An ABSENT `outputs` field stays exempt; a healthy or empty bag is untouched and silent. Sites left unguarded are in the design doc and [#3207](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3207).
