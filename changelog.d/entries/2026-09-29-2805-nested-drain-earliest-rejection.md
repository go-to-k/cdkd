- **A failed intrinsic resolution reports its EARLIEST failure again when the failures sit at different nesting depths (issue [#2805](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2805))** -- `src/deployment/intrinsic-function-resolver.ts` (`allSettledKeepingFirstRejection` and its two call sites), `.claude/rules/layout-deployment-secrets.md` and unit cases. Since the drain of [#2563](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2563), a list nested inside an `Fn::Join` (or another list) held its own failure until its slower siblings settled, so the enclosing drain received a LATER, shallower failure first and reported that one. Which failure surfaces is what the retry classifier reads, so the swap could turn a retry into a stop or the reverse. A drain now starts its parts itself, inside a scope holding its own record of capture orders; a nested drain writes the order its pick was captured in into its PARENT's record, keeping the smaller one if a sibling already wrote it, and the parent ranks that error by it instead of by arrival. The resolution fails either way, as before. Bounds: two failures inside one event-loop turn are ordered by when they reach a drain, not by when their lookups failed; a drain the 60-second cap releases reports the earliest failure it has received; an error that is not an object (`undefined`, `null`) carries no order and is ranked by arrival, as is an error a layer wraps between two drains; an error object that reaches a drain from another resolution is ranked by arrival there.
