- **`cdkd diff` over a producer and its consumer now previews the consumer against the exports the producer's deploy publishes (issue [#6796](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6796))** -- `src/analyzer/cross-stack-preview.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/compute-stack-diff.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/cross-stack/import-value.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/stack-output.ts`, `tests/unit/analyzer/cross-stack-preview.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/diff-cross-stack-preview-6796.test.ts`, `tests/integration/cross-stack-export-in-use/`. `cdkd deploy` of both stacks reaches the consumer after the producer has saved its new outputs, but the diff resolved the consumer's `Fn::ImportValue` / `Fn::GetStackOutput` against the producer's old state, printed `No changes detected` for it, and let `--fail-on destructive` / `--fail-on broadening` pass a run whose deploy replaces a consumer resource or widens its IAM policy. The diff now runs a producer before its consumers, and a consumer reads each export the producer changes: the new value when it is a plain value, or `(known after deploy)` when it comes from a resource the producer creates or replaces. A diff that does not select the producer, and every deploy, are unchanged.
