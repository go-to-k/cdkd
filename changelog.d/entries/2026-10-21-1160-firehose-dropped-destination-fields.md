- **A Firehose delivery stream now applies every destination sub-field its template declares, on create and on an ExtendedS3 update (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/firehose-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/firehose-provider-removal-1160.test.ts`, `firehose-provider-readcurrentstate.test.ts`, and `tests/integration/stream-delete-redeploy/` (Phase 1 and Phase 3c). Before this change, `FileExtension`, `CustomTimeZone` and `DynamicPartitioningConfiguration` were dropped from ExtendedS3 creates and updates. A stream therefore came up without them, a change to them was never applied, and a dynamic-partitioning stream could be neither created nor updated: `UpdateDestination` refuses a call that omits that block. Creates also dropped `SecretsManagerConfiguration` on HttpEndpoint, Redshift and Splunk destinations, `RetryOptions` and the S3 backup settings on Redshift, `BufferingHints` on Splunk, and `VpcConfiguration` / `DocumentIdOptions` on the OpenSearch and Elasticsearch destinations. `cdkd drift` now reads `FileExtension` / `CustomTimeZone` too. A stream deployed earlier still lacks these fields after the upgrade, because an unchanged template sends nothing. To apply them, change the destination or redeploy with `--replace`. `VpcConfiguration` is create-only on the OpenSearch and Elasticsearch destinations, so it takes effect only through `--replace`. A destination sub-field REMOVED from the template still stays at its live value. A live CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation keeps it as well (`UpdateDestination` merges), so the unit suite pins that no reset value is sent.
