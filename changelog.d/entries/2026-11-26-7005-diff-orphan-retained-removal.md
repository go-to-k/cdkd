- **`cdkd diff` / `cdkd deploy`: a removed resource that its `DeletionPolicy` keeps is shown as orphaned, not deleted (issue [#7005](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7005))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/render.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow/change-summary.ts`, `src/deployment/deployment-approval.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/run.ts`. Removing a resource with `RemovalPolicy.RETAIN` or `RETAIN_ON_UPDATE_OR_DELETE` printed it in `cdkd diff` like a resource about to be destroyed and counted it under `to delete`, as did the deploy's `Changes:` line and the `--require-approval` prompt, although the deploy keeps it in AWS. The row now ends `[orphan: its DeletionPolicy keeps it in AWS]` (the AWS CDK CLI marks it `orphan`), and the totals count it under a separate `to orphan` term. `cdkd destroy` listed such a resource under `Resources to be deleted` and counted it in its confirmation question; it is now listed under `Resources kept in AWS by their DeletionPolicy`, and the question counts only what it deletes. `--json` already reported it as `WILL_ORPHAN` in `destructiveChanges`. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/diff-orphan-removal-7005.test.ts`; integ `diff-orphan-retained-removal`.
