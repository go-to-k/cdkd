- **`AWS::CloudWatch::Alarm` keeps the SDK route for `WarmUpConfiguration` and `ActionConfiguration` (issue [#2762](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2762)).** An alarm declaring either used to go through Cloud Control. Both now ride on `PutMetricAlarm` as declared, and both are read back for `cdkd drift` when AWS reports them. `PutMetricAlarm` replaces the whole alarm configuration, so removing either from the template clears it on AWS. `EvaluationCriteria` and `EvaluationInterval` no longer carry the claim that the SDK and the CDK L1 lack them: both have them now, so the two are back on the backfill list beside `EvaluationWindow`. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/cloudwatch-alarm-provider.ts`.
