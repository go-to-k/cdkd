- **`cdkd drift --revert` records the physical id and attributes the revert's update returned (issue [#4476](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4476))** -- `src/cli/commands/drift.ts`, `docs/cli-drift.md`. The revert persisted only the narrowed `effectiveProperties` delta, so a provider that re-creates on update left the record naming what it removed: reverting a drifted `AWS::EC2::SecurityGroupIngress` revokes and re-authorizes the rule under a new `sgr-` id, while the record kept the revoked one for `cdkd export` and a later `Fn::GetAtt <rule>.Id`. The record now takes the returned identity exactly as the rollback replay does (go-to-k/cdkd#4434): attributes replaced wholesale when the update replaced the resource, merged key-wise when it updated in place, untouched when none were returned, and the physical id followed on a replacement. A `NoEcho` attribute is masked before the write, and the attributes are scrubbed of the revert's resolved secrets. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift.test.ts`; integ arm in `tests/integration/drift-revert-vpc/verify.sh`.
