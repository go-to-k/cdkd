- **`AWS::Cognito::UserPool`: removing `UserPoolTags`, `MfaConfiguration`, `AccountRecoverySetting`, `SmsConfiguration`, `UserPoolTier` or `DeletionProtection` from the template now does what CloudFormation does (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cognito-provider.ts`. `UpdateUserPool` keeps each of these when the call leaves it out. Before, removing one from the template sent nothing, so AWS kept the old value while the deploy reported success. A CloudFormation A/B on the issue (us-east-1, 2026-10-27) removed them from a live pool. Now a `UserPoolTags` key removed from the template is untagged with `UntagResource`; `aws:` keys are skipped. The type's schema makes `UserPoolTags` its updatable tag property, and its update handler has `cognito-idp:UntagResource`, so the deploy role needs that permission too. Removing `MfaConfiguration` along with every MFA factor property (`EnabledMfas` and the rest) now turns MFA OFF. cdkd sends `SetUserPoolMfaConfig` with `MfaConfiguration: OFF` and warns. Before, the pool stayed `OPTIONAL` or `ON`. CloudFormation keeps `AccountRecoverySetting`, `SmsConfiguration`, `UserPoolTier` and `DeletionProtection` when they are removed, and so does cdkd. It now warns, naming the property, instead of keeping them silently. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/cognito-provider-tag-removal-1160.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/cognito-provider-member-removal-1160.test.ts`. The `cognito-custom-attribute-add` integ's update phase also removes the pool's tag, its MFA and its account recovery setting.
