- **`cdkd drift --revert` on a Route 53 RecordSetGroup no longer warns that the group names no hosted zone (issue [#6681](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6681))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/route53/record-set-group.ts` (`readRecordSetGroup`). The group read returned only `RecordSets`, so the deploy's drift baseline and the revert's desired bag carried neither `HostedZoneId` nor `HostedZoneName`, and every revert printed "exactly one of HostedZoneId and HostedZoneName must be specified (the group names neither)" although the template names the zone. The revert itself was correct. The read now echoes the zone key the group declares. A baseline captured before this change keeps not comparing the key.
