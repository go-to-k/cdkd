- **A nested stack's failed or interrupted deploy no longer suggests a stack-less `cdkd rollback` that targets the top-level stack (issue [#3864](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3864))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts` (`recoveryHint`), with unit tests in `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-type-change-routing.test.ts`. A nested child engine printed "Run 'cdkd deploy' to resume, 'cdkd rollback' to revert, or destroy to clean up." on a `--no-rollback` failure. With no stack argument, `cdkd rollback` resolves to the top-level stack, not the nested one. The child now says it is a nested stack and to recover it through its top-level stack, named, whose own message follows; the top-level stack's message is unchanged. The clean-rollback settle warning no longer names a command ("a later rollback replay is idempotent"). The interrupted path's hint is unchanged; its parent-side handling is go-to-k/cdkd#3875.
