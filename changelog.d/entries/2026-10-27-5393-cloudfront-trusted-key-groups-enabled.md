- **`AWS::CloudFront::Distribution` cache behaviors with `TrustedKeyGroups` or `TrustedSigners` now deploy (issue [#5393](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5393))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cloudfront-distribution-provider.ts`. A declared list was sent as `{ Quantity, Items }` without the `Enabled` member CloudFront requires, so a distribution restricting viewer access to signed URLs or cookies (CDK `trustedKeyGroups`) was refused with `MalformedXML` on create and update. `Enabled` is now always derived from the list, `true` for a non-empty one and `false` for an empty one, in `DefaultCacheBehavior` and every `CacheBehaviors` entry. `cdkd drift` also reports a listed restriction whose `Enabled` was switched off outside cdkd, which it previously read back as the template's bare list.
