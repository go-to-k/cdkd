- **Deleting an `AWS::Events::EventBus` no longer deletes rules that are not the stack's (issue [#6479](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6479))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/eventbridge-bus-provider.ts`. Before deleting a bus, cdkd removed the targets of every rule on it and deleted the rules, including rules from other stacks or added by hand. AWS refuses to delete a bus that still has rules, and so does CloudFormation. cdkd now does the same: the delete refuses, naming the rules still on the bus, and the bus, the rules and the bus's state record stay. The stack's own rules are deleted before the bus as before. Rules an AWS service manages (an archive's or a schema discoverer's) are waited out for up to two minutes, since their owner is deleted first and AWS removes them after it.
