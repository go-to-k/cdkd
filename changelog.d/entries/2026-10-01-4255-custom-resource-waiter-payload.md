- **A backing Lambda that never becomes ready no longer puts its `GetFunction` response into the custom resource's error (issue [#4255](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4255))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/custom-resource-provider.ts`. Before, a readiness waiter that TIMED OUT was relayed verbatim into `Lambda backing custom resource ... did not reach a ready state for Invoke: ...`, and `@smithy/util-waiter` keys its `observedResponses` by the serialized response whenever one carries no HTTP status, or by the raw body on a deserialization failure: the function's `Environment.Variables` in plaintext and its presigned `Code.Location` could reach the default-verbosity error line and the persisted `deployments/*.jsonl` store. The caught waiter error stayed in the cause chain with the payload, even on the FAILURE arm whose message already withheld it (a chain-walking render such as the CLI's fatal `util.inspect` prints it), and the IAM-propagation recycle path debug-logged a failed update-wait's whole response. Now every waiter arm is described from an allowlist -- the waiter state and fixed reason, the function's `State*` / `LastUpdateStatus*` fields, `<status>: OK` keys, and each rejected poll as `describeAwsFailure` summarizes it (`403: AccessDeniedException` in the error, AWS's account / role / session sentence at `--verbose`; a transport failure such as `socket hang up` keeps its wording, a credential failure is summarized by class; a deserialization failure, whose parse message quotes the body, by status and name only) -- the caught waiter error is rewritten in place to the same text, and the recycle line uses it as well. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/custom-resource-provider-waiter-payload.test.ts`, driving the real waiter.
