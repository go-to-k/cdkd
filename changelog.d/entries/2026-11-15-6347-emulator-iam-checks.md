- **`--aws-emulator`: role flags no longer promise IAM errors the emulator does not raise (issue [#6347](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6347))** -- `src/local/role-permission-emulation.ts`, `docs/aws-emulator.md`, `docs/local-emulation.md`. AWS Emulator evaluates IAM policies only on IAM and STS calls, so a role run with `--assume-role` (and its siblings) under the emulator can call S3, DynamoDB or SSM whatever its policies say. The docs said the emulator checks the role's policies, and the warning on a refused AssumeRole said a federated session would be "allowed nothing"; both now say the emulator checks who can assume the role, and that a missing permission shows up only on real AWS.
