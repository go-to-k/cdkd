- **Every `cdkd state orphan` remedy cdkd prints as a template now carries `--stack-region <region>` and says it drops every record the stack has in that region (issue [#3996](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3996))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/{cloudwatch-anomaly-detector,custom-resource,iam-policy,iam-user-group,s3-bucket,lambda-permission}-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/composite-id.ts`, `src/cli/commands/{destroy,state,import}.ts`, with pins in `tests/unit/provisioning/*` and `tests/unit/cli/{destroy,state-destroy,import,import-resolver-error-masking}.test.ts`. The provider skip warnings and refusals, the composite-id skip message and the destroy / state destroy summaries told the operator to clear one resource's record with `cdkd state orphan <stack>`: without `--stack-region` that removes the stack name's record in every region, and in any region it removes every record the stack has there, not one. The templates now carry the region and say so, and the S3 region-mismatch refusal says the region is the stack's, not the bucket's. `cdkd import`'s unresolved-intrinsic warning now names the per-resource `cdkd orphan <StackPath>/<Path/To/Resource>` instead. The three custom-resource `ServiceToken` refusals quote the commands instead of wrapping them in backticks, which ran them when pasted.
