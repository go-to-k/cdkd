- **A Lambda network connector created right after its operator role is retried instead of failing the deploy (issue [#6775](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6775))** -- `src/deployment/retryable-errors/patterns.ts`. The connector create rejects a seconds-old operator role first with `The operator role is invalid ...` and then, as the rejection moves, with `Encountered unauthorized operation while calling EC2 due to invalid ConnectorOperatorRole permissions`, which no pattern matched, so the create was single-shot on the second wording. The new entry is anchored on the handler's `due to invalid ConnectorOperatorRole permissions` clause only.
