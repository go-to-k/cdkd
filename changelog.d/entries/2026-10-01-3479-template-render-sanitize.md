- **`cdkd import`, the context-provider lookups and the CDK annotation display no longer print template- or assembly-derived values raw (issue [#3479](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3479))** -- `src/cli/commands/import.ts`, every `src/synthesis/context-providers/*-provider.ts`, `src/synthesis/stack-messages.ts` and `src/utils/display-safe.ts`. A value carrying a newline, a C1 CSI, `U+0085`, `U+2028` or a bidi override could forge or erase a cdkd line. `cdkd import` now renders stack names and logical ids through `displayStackName` / `displayIdent` (a quoted boundary for a non-plain value, no `'...'` a planted quote can close) on its progress lines, its confirmation prompt, the nested-stack shape refusal, the nested child walk and its template read / parse refusals; the intrinsic resolver's echo on the unresolved-intrinsics warning and the parameter / condition failure texts take `displayAwsMessage`, which also bounds their length. The context providers build every lookup-argument line with `safeMsg`. `[Warning|Info|Error at <path>] <message>` sanitizes both halves through the new `displaySafeMultiline`, which keeps the newlines a multi-line CDK warning carries while stripping the forging class, so `cdkd deploy -a <dir>` over someone else's `cdk.out` can no longer forge a line from an annotation. A legitimate value renders as before, except that the `'...'` cdkd used to put around a stack name, logical id or KMS alias in these messages is gone.
