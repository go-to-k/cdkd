- **An `AWS::EC2::Volume` is now deleted with EC2 `DeleteVolume` instead of through Cloud Control (issue [#3455](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3455))** -- `src/provisioning/ec2-volume-delete.ts` (new), `src/provisioning/cloud-control-provider.ts`. The Cloud Control delete handler for this type can take a snapshot of its own, and it did so on 2 of the 35 Cloud Control volume deletes in this project's test account's CloudTrail for the 90 days to 2026-09-24, both right after cdkd's `DeletionPolicy: Snapshot` final snapshot. Both times the handler deleted the volume while its snapshot was still pending, the volume stayed in `deleting` for more than 15 minutes, and cdkd's delete gave up after its 900-second wait (on 2026-09-19 that failed the rollback of a failed deploy). The extra snapshot carried no cdkd tag, so nothing recorded it and it kept billing. Every volume delete that goes through `CloudControlProvider` now calls `DeleteVolume` and waits until the volume is gone, with the same 15-minute limit. A volume that is already `deleting` is waited on rather than failed. `InvalidVolume.NotFound` goes through the same region-checked already-deleted handling as before. The delete is refused, before any call, if the EC2 client and the Cloud Control client are set to different regions. The `rollback-deletion-policy-snapshot` and `deletion-policy-snapshot` integration fixtures now assert that no Cloud Control `DeleteResource` request exists for their volumes, and the rollback fixture's cleanup now deletes every snapshot of its volume, tagged or not.
