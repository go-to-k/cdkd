- **An `AWS::ECS::Service` recorded `provisionedBy: 'cc-api'` now returns to its SDK provider on its next mutating deploy, and its recorded physical id moves to the SDK form (issue [#5654](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5654))** -- `src/provisioning/provider-registry.ts`, `src/provisioning/sticky-exempt-physical-id.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-in-place.ts`, `src/deployment/read-only-attribute-healer.ts`, and the new `cc-to-sdk-reroute-ecs-service` integ. Cloud Control records a service as `<serviceArn>|<cluster>` while the SDK provider records the bare ARN, so the sticky-CC exemption gains a declared, strict physical-id normalizer: on the flip the service is updated in place by its ARN and the record is written with the ARN and `provisionedBy: 'sdk'`. An id the normalizer does not recognize is refused before any AWS call, naming `--pin-cc-api <LogicalId>` to stay on Cloud Control, and so is a replacement on the deploy that rewrites the id. `cdkd diff` annotates the move `[returning to SDK provider]`.
