- **A replaced SQS queue or SNS topic that has a policy keeps its old copy until the deploy succeeds (issue [#6712](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6712))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/replacement/delete-replaced.ts`, `docs/cli-rollback-limitations.md`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety-replacement.md`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety-internals.md`. A create-first replacement whose old physical id another record of the stack also held was deleted right after the new copy was created. `AWS::SQS::QueuePolicy` and `AWS::SNS::TopicPolicy` are recorded under the queue URL and topic ARN, so every queue with a queue policy and every topic with a topic policy took that path: when a later resource failed the deploy, the rollback re-created the queue empty and the topic with no subscriptions, while state still recorded the stack's own subscription. The old copy now waits for the cleanup phase in memory only (never written to state, since a later run would delete the other record's resource), as CloudFormation deletes replaced resources only after the update succeeds, and a failed deploy's rollback re-adopts it with its messages and subscriptions. A deploy killed outright before the cleanup phase leaves that old copy in AWS, untracked.
