- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports an `AWS::IAM::RolePolicy` / `UserPolicy` / `GroupPolicy`'s policy as `Policies` drift on its role, user or group (issue [#4821](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4821))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/iam-role-provider.ts` (`collectInlinePolicyNamesManagedBySiblings`), `src/deployment/deploy-engine/observed-capture.ts`. Only an `AWS::IAM::Policy` sibling's inline policy was excluded from the principal's `Policies`, so a `CfnRolePolicy` on a role that declares inline policies showed its name as drift on the role. A standalone policy naming the principal in `RoleName` / `UserName` / `GroupName` is now excluded the same way, in the drift read and in the deploy-time baseline.
