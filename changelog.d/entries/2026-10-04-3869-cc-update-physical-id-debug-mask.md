- **cdkd no longer prints a secret-derived Cloud Control identifier in a Cloud Control update's log lines (part of issue [#3869](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3869))** -- `src/provisioning/cloud-control-provider.ts` (`updateIdLogSink`, threaded through the sparse read-back merge, the `GetResource` failure line, the attribute-enrichment lines, the model-parse failure lines and the account-lookup WARN of `accountInfoForSynthesizedArn`) and a new unit file. A Cloud Control identifier can carry a name taken from a secret (`AWS::Logs::MetricFilter` is `<LogGroupName>|<FilterName>`); after a rotation under an unchanged reference that name is the pre-rotation value, which the masker never resolved, so it printed in plaintext on `cdkd deploy`, its rollback revert, and `cdkd drift --revert`. When the desired or previous properties hold a secret-derived leaf or key (a `{{resolve:` reference, `***`, or a value the masker recognises), every occurrence of the identifier and of each `|` part, raw or as the masker rewrote it, now prints as `***` in those lines, and the model-parse detail line, whose echo of the model is truncated, is dropped; every line goes through the masker first. An identifier whose name comes from ANOTHER resource's secret-derived value (neither bag holds the secret) still prints until the engine-level registry lands. **Residual**: that registry, the engine lines and other providers' lines naming a physical id, and this provider's `create()` / `delete()` lines and thrown update errors stay open under [#3869](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3869).
