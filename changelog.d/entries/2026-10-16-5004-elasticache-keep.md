- **Removing `SnapshotRetentionLimit`, `AutoMinorVersionUpgrade` or `NotificationTopicArn` from an `AWS::ElastiCache::CacheCluster` no longer resets it, matching CloudFormation (issue [#5004](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5004))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/elasticache-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/elasticache-provider-update-removal.test.ts`, `docs/provider-rules.md`. A real CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation keeps the live value of all three when they are removed from the template, while cdkd reset them (retention to 0, auto minor version upgrade to true, notifications set inactive). cdkd now sends nothing for a removed one and leaves the live value. `LogDeliveryConfigurations` keeps its reset (CloudFormation disables delivery).
