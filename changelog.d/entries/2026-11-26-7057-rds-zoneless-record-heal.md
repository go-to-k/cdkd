- **`cdkd deploy` / `cdkd diff`: an RDS DB instance whose state record holds no `AvailabilityZone` while the template declares one is compared with the instance's live zone instead of refused as a replacement (issue [#7057](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7057); follow-up to [#7044](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7044))** -- `src/deployment/zoneless-db-instance-heal.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/rds/live-zone.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow/diff.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/preview-record-heals.ts`. A record written before #5640 while `--prefer-sdk-route` kept the zone out read as a zone added since #7044, so `cdkd diff` showed a replacement and every deploy refused it (`--force-stateful-recreation`), although CloudFormation saw no change. Before its diff, a deploy now reads the instance's zone once (`DescribeDBInstances`, in the stack's region) for a record in exactly that shape, or takes it from the record's drift baseline: the declared zone equal to the instance's is no change and the record takes it on the deploy's save, so later deploys read nothing; another zone, or an unreadable one, keeps the replacement and its refusal. `cdkd diff` runs the same read and previews the same verdict. Any other record makes no call. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/zoneless-db-instance-heal-7057.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-zoneless-db-instance-7057.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/diff-zoneless-db-instance-7057.test.ts`.
