- **`cdkd rollback`, `cdkd deploy` and `cdkd orphan --force` name the `Fn::Base64` encoding of a secret as a cause of a `***` they cannot use (issue [#2881](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2881))** -- `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine-masking.ts`, `src/analyzer/orphan-rewriter.ts`, `docs/_contents/state-management.md`. cdkd records `***` where a value is the `Fn::Base64` encoding of a `{{resolve:...}}` secret (EC2 `UserData`, for example), but the rollback refusal on such a baseline and the deploy refusal on a resource reading it back from state (an `AWS::SSM::Parameter`'s `Value` attribute, for example) named only a `NoEcho` Custom Resource and a mask `cdkd import` or `cdkd orphan` wrote, so the remedies they printed could not apply. Each now names it, with its own remedy: the rollback refusal says a deploy that changes the resource restores the value, and that the refusal recurs until the secret is no longer encoded into the property; the deploy refusal says to have the reader build the value itself from the secret's own reference, and scopes its re-import command to the import-written mask it can clear. The `cdkd orphan --force` warning no longer offers a re-import for a mask a re-import cannot clear. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/rollback-executor-masked-baseline.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-base64-attribute-refusal.test.ts`, `tests/unit/analyzer/orphan-rewriter.test.ts`.
