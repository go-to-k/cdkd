- **A failure that cannot be converted to text no longer stops the teardown or dispatch of `cdkd local start-api`, `cdkd local run-task` or `cdkd local invoke-agentcore --watch`** (`src/local/{container-pool,websocket-server,ecs-task-runner,invoke-agentcore-watch-loop}.ts`, go-to-k/cdkd#3361). Handlers that log a failure and carry on built their message with `x instanceof Error ? x.message : String(x)`, and `String()` throws for a null-prototype object, so the handler itself threw: the container pool skipped `docker rm` after a failed log-stream stop, and a failed in-flight start made `dispose()` reject before tearing down any warm container; a WebSocket dispatch failure left the 1011 close or the error frame unsent and broke the connection's dispatch chain, so every later frame and its `$disconnect` were skipped; `run-task`'s cleanup stopped at a failed hook, in-flight removal, log-stream stop or drain, leaving the network and volumes behind (and, without `--keep-running`, the containers); and the `--watch` loop lost its rebuild fallback, kept pinging a torn-down container after a failed rebuild, stopped running later reloads, or rejected when closing its watcher failed. Each now goes through `describeAwsFailure(x).detail`, or `safeStringify(x)` beside a kept `err.stack` arm, byte-identical whenever the value converts and a placeholder only when it cannot. Tests: `tests/unit/local/*-string-outthrow-3361.test.ts`.
