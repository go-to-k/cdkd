- **`AWS::S3Tables::Table` and `AWS::S3Tables::TableBucket` keep the SDK route for their configuration properties (issue [#2762](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2762)).** A table declaring `IcebergMetadata`, `StorageClassConfiguration`, `Compaction`, `SnapshotManagement` or `WithoutMetadata`, and a table bucket declaring `EncryptionConfiguration`, `StorageClassConfiguration`, `MetricsConfiguration`, `UnreferencedFileRemoval` or `ReplicationConfiguration`, used to go through Cloud Control. They are now sent on `CreateTable` / `CreateTableBucket` or by their own `Put*` call. A change to the table's create-only `IcebergMetadata`, `StorageClassConfiguration` or `WithoutMetadata` replaces the table, as CloudFormation does; every other one is updated in place and read back for `cdkd drift`. Removing a bucket's `MetricsConfiguration` or `ReplicationConfiguration` turns it off; removing any of the others leaves the AWS value in place with a warning, so declare the value you want instead. A table bucket with replication configured outside cdkd that its template does not declare now reports `ReplicationConfiguration` as drift, and `cdkd drift --revert` removes that replication. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/s3-tables-provider.ts`.
