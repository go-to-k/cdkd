- **A `RetentionInDays` of `false`, `null`, `0` or `'0'` no longer silently removes a live log-group retention policy (issue [#2699](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2699))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/logs-loggroup-provider.ts` (`isAbsentRetention`, `assertUsableRetention`; `create()` / `update()` now take their contexts), `tests/unit/provisioning/logs-loggroup-provider-retention-coercion.test.ts` (`CREATE_MATRIX` / `UPDATE_MATRIX` re-pinned to the measurement), `tests/unit/provisioning/logs-loggroup-provider-roundtrip.test.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`. The live A/B settled the falsy family: an absent property, `''` and a whitespace-only string ARE CloudFormation's spelling of "no retention" (the policy is removed) and keep the delete arm -- whitespace-only was refused before and now matches CloudFormation -- while `false`, `0`, `'0'` (rejected by the Logs handler) and `null` (rejected at `update-stack`; cdkd's resolver omits the key for `AWS::NoValue`, so a `null` here is a template literal) are refused like `'abc'`. One cdkd-side exception: a numeric `0` on a `desiredFromAwsReadback` bag (`cdkd drift --revert`) is `readCurrentState`'s own never-expire and still deletes, so reverting a console-added retention keeps working; on a template bag `0` is refused. On a rollback replay (`CreateContext.replayingState`) every refusal downgrades to warn-and-skip so an old record stays restorable. **Behavior change:** those four template values now fail the deploy instead of deleting the retention.
