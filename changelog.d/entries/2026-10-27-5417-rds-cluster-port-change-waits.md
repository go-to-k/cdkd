- **`AWS::RDS::DBCluster`: a port change now waits out the member instances it reboots, records the sent port under `--no-wait`, and fails a wait at once on a terminal or vanished cluster (issue [#5417](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5417))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/rds-provider.ts`. A cluster update that changes `Port` (declared, or reset to the engine default on removal) already waited for the cluster to report the new port. It now also waits for each member instance the change moved to `modifying` / `rebooting`. Before, a same-deploy `AWS::RDS::DBInstance` modify could be refused with "Database instance is not in available state". Under `--no-wait` the recorded `Endpoint.Port` is now the port sent; before, it was the old port the describe could still read, so `Fn::GetAtt <cluster>.Endpoint.Port` consumers stayed stale. The port wait and the security-group wait now fail at once when the cluster enters a terminal status or `DescribeDBClusters` returns no cluster. Before, they waited out their 30-minute budget. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/rds-provider-cluster-port-change-5417.test.ts`.
