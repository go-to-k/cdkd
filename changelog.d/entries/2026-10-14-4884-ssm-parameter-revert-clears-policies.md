- **`cdkd drift --revert` now removes an out-of-band `AWS::SSM::Parameter` policy instead of reporting a revert that left it in place** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ssm-parameter-provider.ts`. When the template declares `Policies` and the recorded baseline holds none, a policy added out of band is drift. The revert sent no `Policies`, and `PutParameter` keeps a parameter's policies until it is sent new ones or an empty one, so the policy stayed and the next `cdkd drift` reported it again. A revert to no policies against a parameter that has some now sends the empty policy the Systems Manager guide documents for removal (`[{}]`), when the template declares no policies either; a baseline whose empty list stands for a failed read never removes a policy the template declares. A deploy still sends no `Policies` when the template has none, as CloudFormation does. Issue [#4884](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4884).
