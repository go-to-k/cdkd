- **`cdkd local run-task` keeps the output of a container that exits right away** (Refs #6364). The bundled `cdk-local` stopped the `docker logs -f` stream as soon as the container exited, so a task that printed and exited within a moment could lose some or all of its output. Since `cdk-local` 0.168.5 (bundled here at `^0.168.6`), it waits up to two seconds for the log stream to close before stopping it.
