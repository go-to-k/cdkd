- **`AWS::Route53::HostedZone`: removing `VPCs` from a private hosted zone now prints a warning that the zone keeps its VPC associations, as CloudFormation does (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/route53-provider.ts`. A CloudFormation A/B on the issue (us-east-1) showed CloudFormation keeps every association when `VPCs` is removed whole, from a zone with one VPC or two, or set to `[]`, and no update fails. cdkd already kept them, so no AWS call changes. The deploy used to say nothing; it now warns `<LogicalId> (AWS::Route53::HostedZone): VPCs was removed from the template, and the zone keeps its current VPC associations, as CloudFormation leaves them. ...`. A rollback revert gets its own wording, and `cdkd drift --revert` does not warn. Dropping some VPCs while keeping others still disassociates the dropped ones. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/route53-hostedzone-vpcs-removal-1160.test.ts`; the `ec2-vpc` integ fixture gains a private hosted zone whose `VPCs` its removal phase drops.
