- **Removing `Monitoring` from an `AWS::EC2::Instance` now turns detailed monitoring off, matching CloudFormation (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- A real CloudFormation A/B showed that CloudFormation turns monitoring off when `Monitoring` is removed. cdkd used to send nothing, so detailed monitoring stayed on after the template stopped declaring it. cdkd now calls `UnmonitorInstances` when monitoring was on. A removed `MetadataOptions` or `CreditSpecification` keeps its live value, as in CloudFormation. When `DisableApiTermination` is removed, CloudFormation fails the update and the value stays. cdkd also keeps termination protection as it is, and now warns that it was left in place and that setting `DisableApiTermination` to `false` turns it off. `cdkd drift --revert` writes no reset. Changed: `src/provisioning/providers/ec2-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/ec2-provider-instance-removal-1160.test.ts`, `tests/integration/ec2-instance/`.
