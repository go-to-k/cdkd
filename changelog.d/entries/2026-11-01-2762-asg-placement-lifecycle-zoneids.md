- **The SDK provider sends `PlacementGroup`, `InstanceLifecyclePolicy` and `AvailabilityZoneIds` of `AWS::AutoScaling::AutoScalingGroup` (issue [#2762](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2762))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/asg-provider.ts`, the regenerated property-coverage map, `tests/fixtures/cfn-schemas/_todo-backfill.json`, the ASG unit and wire cases, and new arms in the `launchtemplate-asg-inplace` integ. Before, a group declaring any of the three routed through Cloud Control. They now ride `CreateAutoScalingGroup` and `UpdateAutoScalingGroup`, and drift reads them back. A removal follows CloudFormation, measured on a real stack: a removed `PlacementGroup` is cleared, a removed `InstanceLifecyclePolicy` keeps its live value, and a switch between `AvailabilityZoneIds` and `AvailabilityZones` sends only the one the template now declares. `InstanceId` still routes through Cloud Control: `CreateAutoScalingGroup` derives a launch configuration from the instance, and launch configurations are end-of-life like `LaunchConfigurationName`.
