- **A role ARN is shape- and length-checked before cdkd sends it to STS (issue [#2348](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2348), following cdk-local#607)** -- `src/utils/role-arn.ts`, `src/cli/options.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-invoke.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-invoke-agentcore.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-start-api.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-run-task.ts`, `src/local/ecr-puller.ts`.
  - One predicate, `isIamRoleArn` (`arn:<partition>:iam::<account>:role/<printable ASCII>`, anchored at both ends, at most 2048 characters, the STS `RoleArn` maximum), is enforced immediately before every `AssumeRoleCommand` cdkd sends. A malformed `--role-arn` / `CDKD_ROLE_ARN`, `--ecr-role-arn`, `--assume-task-role` or task-definition `TaskRoleArn`, and a cross-account `Fn::GetStackOutput` `RoleArn`, now fail with "AssumeRole refused ... Nothing was sent to STS." instead of being sent. An empty `--role-arn ""`, `--ecr-role-arn ""` or `--layer-role-arn ""` is refused too; it used to run as the caller's own identity.
  - `--assume-role` on `cdkd local start-api` used a start-anchored pattern, so a value with a newline, an escape sequence or any length after `role/` passed. It is now rejected, as are an empty role name and a partition containing a space. The bare form is trimmed, as the `LogicalId=<arn>` form already was.
  - A malformed `--assume-role <arn>` on `cdkd local invoke` and `local invoke-agentcore` is now an error. It used to fail at STS and fall back to the developer's shell credentials. `--ecr-role-arn`, `--assume-task-role` and `--layer-role-arn` are checked at startup too. A bare `--assume-role` ARN containing `=` now parses.
  - A role ARN read from deployed state, a sibling role's cached `Arn`, or a template `Role` / `RoleArn` literal used to be accepted on `startsWith('arn:')`. A malformed one now stops `local start-api --assume-role-auto` at startup. `local invoke` and `invoke-agentcore` warn and fall back, as they did when STS rejected it.
