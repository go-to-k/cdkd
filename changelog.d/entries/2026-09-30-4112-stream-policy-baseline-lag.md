- **A DynamoDB stream `ResourcePolicy` or `Tags` that cdkd just applied is no longer left out of the drift baseline when AWS is slow to show it** (`src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/dynamodb-stream-members.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/types/resource.ts`, go-to-k/cdkd#4112). After a create or update, the deploy reads the table back to record its `observedProperties` baseline. When the stream's policy or tags were still not visible after the re-read schedule, or the read failed (a throttle, for example), the baseline recorded the member as absent. `cdkd drift` then reported `StreamSpecification.ResourcePolicy` or `.Tags` as drift until the next deploy. The capture after cdkd's own write now keeps the declared member in that case. A `cdkd drift` read still believes the live answer, so a policy or tag set removed out of band still reports as drift. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/dynamodb-table-provider-stream-members.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-iam-capture-siblings.test.ts`.
