- **`cdkd destroy --remove-protection` restores deletion protection on an ELBv2 load balancer or an Auto Scaling group whose delete then failed terminally (issue [#2204](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2204), partial)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/elbv2-provider.ts`, `asg-provider.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/elbv2-asg-remove-protection-compensate.test.ts` and a live arm in the `remove-protection` integ (step 4b: an out-of-band VPC endpoint service makes an NLB's `DeleteLoadBalancer` refuse). Before, the flip-off (`ModifyLoadBalancerAttributes deletion_protection.enabled=false` / `UpdateAutoScalingGroup DeletionProtection=none`) was never undone, so a failed destroy left the resource live with its guard silently stripped. Now the guard is turned back on before the failure is reported, under the rules the DynamoDB, RDS-family, log group and user pool sites follow: only a guard a pre-flip `DescribeLoadBalancerAttributes` / `DescribeAutoScalingGroups` saw on and this run turned off, only on a terminal failure, never once AWS has accepted the delete, and a failed re-enable is its own log line with a pasteable restore command. An Auto Scaling group gets back the level it had (`prevent-force-deletion` or `prevent-all-deletion`). The other `--remove-protection` types stay open under #2204.
