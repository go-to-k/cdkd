- **`cdkd export` refuses a stack record whose `resources` map it cannot read by name, root or nested child, instead of failing on a bare `TypeError` or reporting every resource as not deployed (issue [#3188](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3188))** -- `src/cli/commands/export.ts` (`exportCommand` at the state load, `runPerStackImportLoop` over every tree node before its pre-flight), with cases in `tests/unit/cli/export-malformed-child-bag.test.ts`. A record whose `resources` was a string, a number, a boolean, a list, `null` or absent was refused only by accident: `buildImportPlan` read every template row against it, so a `null` or absent bag threw `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading '<id>')` naming no record, and any other shape listed every row as "no entry in cdkd state". For a nested CHILD this mattered more, because the tree walk returns such a child with no children of its own, so the accidental refusal was the only thing keeping the export from migrating a truncated tree. Both paths now raise the shared `STATE_RESOURCES_MALFORMED` refusal (the one `cdkd import`, `cdkd orphan` and `cdkd rollback` raise, among others), naming the stack and region and ending on how to inspect the record. It fires before the lock on the root, before any changeset, template upload or state write on a child, and on `--dry-run` too. A readable record is unaffected.
