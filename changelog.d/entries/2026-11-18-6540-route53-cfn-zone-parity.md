- **Route 53 records accept only the hosted-zone spellings CloudFormation accepts (issue [#6540](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6540))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/route53/shared.ts` (`refuseCfnUnresolvableZoneName`), `route53/record-set.ts`, `route53/record-set-group.ts`, `route53/core.ts`, `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/predicates.ts`, unit tests and the `route53` integ fixture. Measured against CloudFormation, cdkd accepted templates the AWS CDK CLI then fails: an `AWS::Route53::RecordSet` or `AWS::Route53::RecordSetGroup` `HostedZoneName` without its trailing dot or in upper case (cdkd normalized it), a name shared by a public and a private zone (cdkd took the first; a delete already refused it), and, on a `RecordSetGroup`, a zone key on a `RecordSets` entry, a group naming neither or both zone keys, and an empty `RecordSets`. Each is now refused before any change on a create or a template-path update, with CloudFormation's reason; a rollback replay or `drift --revert` warns and proceeds. Correcting a zone name's spelling updates the record in place.
