- **An `AWS::ECS::Gateway` CREATE rejected because ECS could not assume its infrastructure role is now retried (issue [#5634](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5634))** -- `src/deployment/retryable-errors/patterns.ts` and its unit cases. A Gateway CREATE rejected with `Could not assume the infrastructure role` is now retried on the IAM-propagation schedule instead of failing on the first rejection; a role that is genuinely wrong still fails after the bounded retries. The rejection was seen when the role was created in the same deploy, right before the gateway.
