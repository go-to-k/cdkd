- **`cdkd deploy --concurrency` defaults to 20 instead of 10, and `CDKD_PHASE_TIMINGS=1` counts retries by error class (issue [#5242](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5242))** -- `src/cli/options.ts`, `src/deployment/retry.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy.md`, `docs/troubleshooting-slow-deploys.md`, `docs/architecture-deploy-pipeline.md`, `docs/provider-rules.md`, `tests/integration/wide-independent` (a Cloud Control mode), and a new unit file. Measured on 48 independent resources: on the Cloud Control route the resource DAG took 103 s at 10, 68 s at 20 and 72 s at 30, where Cloud Control began answering with retryable failures (17 retries, none at 20); on the SDK route it took 3.6 s at 10 and 2.1 s at 30. A stack whose resources depend on each other rarely has more than 10 ready at once, so it deploys as before. Pass `--concurrency 10` to keep the previous limit. The phase report now carries a `retry after <error class>` line per class, with the total backoff and the longest; the error's message is never shown.
