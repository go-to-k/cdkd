- **`cdkd deploy --concurrency` defaults to 20 instead of 10, and `CDKD_PHASE_TIMINGS=1` counts retries by error class (issue [#5242](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5242))** -- `src/cli/options.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/deployment/retry.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy.md`, `docs/troubleshooting-slow-deploys.md`, `docs/architecture-deploy-pipeline.md`, `docs/provider-rules.md`, `tests/integration/wide-independent` (a Cloud Control mode), the new `tests/integration/wide-web-functions`, and a new unit file. A stack with many independent resources provisions up to 2x faster: 24 Lambda web functions took 40 s at 10 and 20 s at 20; 48 independent Cloud Control resources 103 s at 10, 75 s at 15, 66 s at 20 and 72 s at 30, where Cloud Control began to throttle. A stack whose resources depend on each other rarely has more than 10 ready at once, so it deploys as before. A nested stack's child deploys at its parent's limit, so a nested level now allows up to 20 x 20 operations in flight. Pass `--concurrency 10` to keep the previous limit. The phase report now carries a `retry after <error class>` line per class, naming the AWS error a provider wrapped, with the scheduled backoff and the longest; an error's message is never shown.
