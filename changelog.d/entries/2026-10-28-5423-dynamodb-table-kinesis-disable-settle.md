- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports a Kinesis streaming destination just removed from a DynamoDB table (issue [#5423](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5423))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`. AWS kept reading a disabled destination as `ACTIVE` for about a second after `DisableKinesisStreamingDestination` answered `DISABLING`, so the drift baseline captured right after a deploy that removed (or changed) `KinesisStreamSpecification` recorded the old destination, and every later `cdkd drift` reported it. The deploy now waits, for up to 60 seconds, until the destination reads `DISABLED`; one still reported after that goes on with a warning that `cdkd drift` may report it until AWS finishes disabling it.
