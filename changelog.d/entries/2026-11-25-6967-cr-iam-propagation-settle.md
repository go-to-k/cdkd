- **A custom resource invoked right after its handler's role or policy is written no longer loses up to five minutes to IAM propagation (issue [#6967](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6967))** -- `src/provisioning/iam-propagation-settle.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-{policy,role,managed-policy}/{create,update}.ts`, `src/provisioning/deferred-readiness.ts`, `src/deployment/deferred-readiness-run.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy-waits.md`. cdkd invoked a custom resource's handler moments after writing its role and policy, before IAM had propagated them. A CDK `Provider` framework `onEvent` answers that denial by polling `waitUntilFunctionActiveV2` until its 300-second limit, so each such resource took 3 to 4 minutes before the transient-authz retry recycled the function. An `AWS::IAM::Role`, `AWS::IAM::Policy` or `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` create or update now reports an `iam-propagation` readiness through the #6940 mechanism, and a custom resource downstream of it, transitively, waits until 10 seconds after the write before its first invoke, only for what remains of that time. Nothing else waits: a plain dependent and a `Fn::GetAtt` of the role's `Arn` proceed at once, and a deploy that writes no IAM is unchanged. The wait prints one `Waiting for IAM to propagate` line per custom resource. An 18-stack app deployed in 3 min 47 s instead of 7 min 35 s.
