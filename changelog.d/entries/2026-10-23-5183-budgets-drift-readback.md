- **`cdkd drift` compares `AWS::Budgets::Budget` again, through the provider's own read-back (issue [#5183](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5183))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/budgets-budget-provider.ts` (`readCurrentState`, `getDriftUnorderedPaths`, `getDriftUnknownPaths`, `canonicalizeDriftProperties`), `src/analyzer/drift-cc-api-deny-list.ts`, `docs/cli-drift.md`, `docs/cli-drift-internals.md`, a new unit file, and the `budgets` integ. Since #5179 every budget was reported as drift unknown (`? CostBudget`), because the Cloud Control READ response diffed on an unchanged budget. The provider now reads the budget with `DescribeBudget`, `DescribeNotificationsForBudget`, one `DescribeSubscribersForNotification` per notification and `ListTagsForResource`, mapped to the template shape: amounts as numbers, dates as ISO strings, and members AWS changes on its own (`CalculatedSpend`, `LastUpdatedTime`, `HealthStatus`) left out. Notifications and their subscribers are compared as sets. An absent `ThresholdType` reads as AWS's default `PERCENTAGE` on both sides. `Budget.BudgetLimit` is not compared on a budget declaring `AutoAdjustData` or `PlannedBudgetLimits`, where AWS sets it. The deny-list entry is removed. Like every provider with a read-back, a deploy that creates or updates a budget now captures its `observedProperties` off the critical path; `--no-capture-observed-state` turns that off.
