- **The deploy engine's stateful-replacement refusals now say when `--force-stateful-recreation` cannot remove the resource because AWS protects it (issue [#2610](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2610), sites 9-11)** -- `src/provisioning/recorded-protection.ts` (new), `src/provisioning/protection-flags.ts` (new, the protection table moved out of `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`), `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-replace.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-in-place.ts`, `src/deployment/recreate-targets.ts`. The property-driven replacement refusal, both arms of the update-failure fallback refusal and the `--recreate-via-*` stateful refusal told the user to re-run with `--force-stateful-recreation` (plus `--replace` on one arm) even when cdkd's record of the resource carries a deletion / termination protection flag. `cdkd deploy` has no `--remove-protection`, so AWS refuses that delete: the deploy then failed at it, or completed and left the old resource and its data in AWS, untracked. When the recorded properties, or the AWS read-back cdkd stored, carry the flag, the refusal now names it and the bag it came from, says the flag alone does not remove the resource, points at "Deletion protection blocks a replacement" in `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`, and asks for protection to be turned off out of band first. The flag is read through the same per-type table the `cdkd destroy` confirm prompt counts with, and a target under `UpdateReplacePolicy: Retain` gets no note. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/recorded-protection.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/replacement-protection-note.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/recreate-targets.test.ts`.
