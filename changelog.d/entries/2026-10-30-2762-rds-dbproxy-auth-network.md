- **`AWS::RDS::DBProxy` keeps the SDK route for `DefaultAuthScheme`, `EndpointNetworkType` and `TargetConnectionNetworkType` (issue [#2762](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2762)).** A proxy declaring any of them used to go through Cloud Control. All three are now sent on `CreateDBProxy` and read back for `cdkd drift`. A `DefaultAuthScheme` change is sent on `ModifyDBProxy`, and a proxy with `DefaultAuthScheme: IAM_AUTH` may omit `Auth`, as on CloudFormation. A change to either network type replaces the proxy, as CloudFormation does for these create-only properties. A switch to `DefaultAuthScheme: NONE` whose template declares no `Auth` is refused before any call, since AWS requires `Auth` under `NONE`. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/rds-dbproxy-provider.ts`.
