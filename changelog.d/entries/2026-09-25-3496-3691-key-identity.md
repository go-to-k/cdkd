- **The cross-stack read union and the masked-output recovery store are keyed by what actually identifies an entry (issues [#3496](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3496), [#3691](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3691), [#3746](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3746))** -- `crossStackReadsForPartialSave` and the stale-attribute heal memo in `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `recordRecoverableMaskedOutput` / `recoverMaskedOutput` in `src/deployment/secret-redaction.ts` and their callers in the resolver and `NestedStackProvider`, `tests/unit/deployment/cross-stack-read-union-key.test.ts`, and cases in the recovery-store, resolver-identity and nested-stack suites. The union of a stack's recorded cross-stack reads keyed each entry by its source stack, region and name joined with a NUL; the previous record is an unchecked cast, so two distinct entries could share a key and the second was dropped from `state.imports`, the list `cdkd destroy` refuses a destroy on. The keys are now an injective encoding, as the other composite keys became in #3501. The in-run recovery store for a `NoEcho` output masked in the same process now carries the credential identity as well, so a library caller that switches `AwsClients` between accounts is not handed one account's plaintext for another account's same-named producer stack. `cdkd destroy`'s strong-reference guard (`scanActiveConsumers`) and the recreate prompt's consumer scan (`findDownstreamConsumers`) now compare a recorded region case-insensitively, as the deploy side does, so an import recorded in another case still blocks the producer's destroy.
