- **`AWS::ServiceDiscovery::Service` now warns when a `DnsConfig.NamespaceId` or `DnsConfig.RoutingPolicy` change is not applied (issue [#5373](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5373))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/servicediscovery-provider.ts`. `UpdateService` cannot change either member. CloudFormation finishes such an update without applying them and without replacing the service, and cdkd does the same, but the deploy used to report success without saying anything. It now prints a warning that names the members and says how to apply them: set the top-level `NamespaceId` to move the service, or replace the service.
