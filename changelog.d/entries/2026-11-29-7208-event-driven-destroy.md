- **`cdkd destroy` deletes each resource as soon as everything that depends on it is gone, instead of one dependency level at a time (issue [#7208](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7208))** -- `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/delete-dag.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/under-lock.ts`, `src/analyzer/implicit-delete-deps.ts`. A slow delete (a NAT gateway, a CloudFront distribution, a VPC Lambda's ENIs) no longer holds back every unrelated deletion below it. Destroy runs through the deploy's event-driven executor, bounded by the larger of 20 and the stack's widest level, so no stack starts fewer deletes at once than before. The SIGINT drain, a failed delete keeping its dependencies, and the superseded-copy order are unchanged. A custom resource is now also deleted before every IAM policy on its handler's role, in `cdkd destroy` and in a deploy removing both, so its handler's Delete keeps its permissions; that order held before only through the level barrier. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/destroy-delete-dag-7208.test.ts`, `tests/unit/analyzer/implicit-delete-deps-custom-resource-policy-7208.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/deletion-deps-custom-resource-policy-7208.test.ts`.
