- **A DynamoDB TimeToLive change made soon after the previous one now waits out AWS's rate limit instead of failing the deploy (issue [#5451](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5451))** -- `src/provisioning/dynamodb-ttl.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`, `dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/slow-cc-operation-timeouts.ts`. AWS refuses another `UpdateTimeToLive` for a while after each TTL change (`Time to live has been modified multiple times within a fixed interval`; about 31 minutes after an enable in a measured run, up to an hour per AWS). CloudFormation re-sends until AWS accepts, so a table updated to disable TTL right after it was enabled reached `UPDATE_COMPLETE`, where cdkd failed. Both `AWS::DynamoDB::Table` and `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` now announce the wait and re-send every minute until AWS accepts, within the resource timeout (a rollback or `cdkd drift --revert`, which have none, wait until AWS accepts); Ctrl-C ends the wait. Their update timeout has a 75-minute minimum so the wait fits, and a refusal still standing when the timeout runs out fails with the remedy (`--resource-timeout`, or redeploying later).
