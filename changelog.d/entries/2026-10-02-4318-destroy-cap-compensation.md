- **`cdkd destroy --remove-protection` puts a deletion guard back when the delete gives up at its attempt cap (issue [#4318](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4318))** -- `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/deletion-protection-compensation.ts`. The guard cdkd turned off was restored only when the delete failed terminally, so a refusal that kept reading as retryable (an IAM deny worded `not authorized to perform`) ran the destroy loop to its attempt cap and left the guard off on a live resource with no message. The loop now marks its last attempt (`runDeleteAttempt`), and any failure there is compensated: the guard is turned back on, or, on a Cloud Control delete that may already be running, the warning naming the check and restore commands is printed. The compensation also classifies a failure as the loop does, reading the cause chain: a Cloud Control delete wait abandoned over a withheld access denial was counted as final while the loop retried it, so a refusal on that retry left the guard off. A per-resource `--resource-timeout` still restores nothing. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-remove-protection-cap.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/deletion-protection-final-attempt.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/{cloud-control-remove-protection-compensate,providers/logs-cognito-remove-protection-compensate}.test.ts`.
