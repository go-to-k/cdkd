- **A fix-forward deploy now deletes the S3 bucket an earlier failed CREATE left behind (issue [#4606](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4606), S3 bucket slice)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/s3-bucket-provider.ts`. When a `cdkd deploy --no-rollback` made an `AWS::S3::Bucket`, failed configuring it and could not clean it up, and a later deploy kept the logical id under another bucket name, the successful deploy warned about the earlier bucket, left it in AWS and exited 2: it could not prove the two were different buckets, nor that the bucket under the old name was still the one the failed CREATE made. The S3 provider now answers both. Two different bucket names are two buckets (names are global and cannot be changed), once the record's bucket reads back in the stack's region. A bucket's identity is its name, region and the `CreationDate` this account's `ListBuckets` reports, recorded when the CREATE fails and compared before the delete: a bucket deleted by hand and re-created under the name reports another date and is kept, with a warning. A date S3 moved after a bucket change (a bucket policy edit) also keeps the bucket. The same identity serves `cdkd rollback` and `cdkd destroy` for such a journaled bucket. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/s3-bucket-identity-4606.test.ts`; integ: `s3-fix-forward-orphan`.
