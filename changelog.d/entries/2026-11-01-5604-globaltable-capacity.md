- **A `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` capacity change now reaches AWS with no billing flip (issue [#5604](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5604))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts` left a flat `ReadCapacityUnits` change on the deploy region's replica unsent, and a literal `WriteCapacityUnits` change too, so the deploy reported success and the old capacity stayed in AWS. Both now reach AWS: a side the update does not change goes as AWS holds it, so an auto-scaled side stays with its scaling policy. With a cross-region replica, a read change goes out as the local region's override, and a write change puts back each other region's read, which `ProvisionedThroughput` overwrites. A changed literal that does not resolve to a number is reported instead of passing in silence.
