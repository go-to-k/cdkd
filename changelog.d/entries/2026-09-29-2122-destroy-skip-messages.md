- **The skip messages of `cdkd destroy`, `cdkd state destroy` and a deploy-side skipped delete no longer say cdkd could not address the resource when a custom-resource Delete handler ran and reported FAILED (issue [#2122](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2122))** -- `src/deployment/delete-outcome.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy.ts`, `src/cli/commands/state.ts`, `docs/cli-reference.md`, `docs/troubleshooting.md`, `docs/cli-destroy.md` (which now also lists the handler causes). Since issue #2054 a handler that answers FAILED is reported as skipped, but every skip message was written for a record cdkd could not address: it said cdkd `could not address` the resource, that `no delete was issued`, and to fix the physicalId in state.json and re-run -- all false for a handler that received the delete and refused it, whose record is correct. The per-resource skip sentence now reads `cdkd did not confirm <id> (<physicalId>) was deleted ..., so it may still exist: <reason>`; the per-stack summaries and the exit-2 error say the delete was not confirmed, point at the `skipped (...)` lines and their warnings for each cause and for whether repairing the record in state.json helps, and otherwise, such as for a handler that reported FAILED, say to delete the resources by hand and drop the records with `cdkd state orphan`. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/delete-outcome-message.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-skipped.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/destroy.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/state-destroy.test.ts`.
