- **Two newly published nested members the SDK providers cannot send yet are refused instead of being dropped while the deploy reports success (PR [#5123](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/pull/5123))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-network-connector-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts`, and unit tests. `AWS::Lambda::NetworkConnector` `Configuration.VpcEgressConfiguration.ElasticIpConfiguration` is modelled by no `@aws-sdk/client-lambda-core` release, and `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` `Replicas[].DisassociationProtectionEnabled` is taken by DynamoDB only as a separate per-replica `UpdateTable`, which the provider does not make yet ([#5570](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5570)). A declared value is refused before any AWS call on a create, and on an update when it changed; an unchanged or removed value deploys as before, and a rollback replay or `cdkd drift --revert` warns and proceeds without it.
