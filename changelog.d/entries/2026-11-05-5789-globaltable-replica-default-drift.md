- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports `Replicas` drift on an `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` whose record has no observed baseline (issue [#5789](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5789))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts` and its unit tests. The read-back puts each replica's `TableClass` (`STANDARD` when AWS reports none), `DeletionProtectionEnabled` (both since #5397), contributor insights, point-in-time recovery and tags on its `Replicas` entry. A record whose comparison falls back to the template's properties never declared them, so the whole `Replicas` list differed and drift exited 1 on a healthy table. That happens to a record a rollback rebuilt and to a pre-v3 record. The comparison now fills these in from AWS only at their default (`STANDARD`, `false`, insights and recovery off, no tags), matched by region. A non-default value the template does not declare, such as another class, protection on, insights or recovery on, or a tag, still reports drift.
