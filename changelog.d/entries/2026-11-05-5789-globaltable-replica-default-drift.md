- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports `Replicas` drift on an `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` whose record has no observed baseline (issue [#5789](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5789))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts` and its unit tests. Since #5397 the read-back puts each replica's `TableClass` (`STANDARD` when AWS reports none) and `DeletionProtectionEnabled` on its `Replicas` entry. A record whose comparison falls back to the template's properties never declared them, so the whole `Replicas` list differed and drift exited 1 on a healthy table. That happens to a record a rollback rebuilt and to a pre-v3 record. The comparison now fills the two in from AWS only at their default (`STANDARD`, `false`), matched by region. A non-default class or an enabled protection the template does not declare still reports drift.
