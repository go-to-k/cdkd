- **A delete of an Auto Scaling group that still has instances scales it to 0 and waits, as CloudFormation does, instead of failing; every delete of a group checks the recorded ARN first (issues [#6466](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6466), [#6468](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6468))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/asg/delete.ts` (`scaleInThenDelete`, `assertRecordedGroup`, `ScaleInRefusal`), `src/deployment/superseded-resources.ts` (`retryWhileInUse`), `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/asg-delete-scale-in-6466.test.ts`, `docs/cli-destroy-protection.md`, `docs/cli-destroy.md`, the `asg-destroy-running-instances` integ fixture. AWS refuses a delete without `ForceDelete` while the group has instances, so `cdkd destroy` of a running group failed. Now cdkd sets MinSize / MaxSize / DesiredCapacity to 0, waits up to 15 minutes (cut to the per-resource timeout) for the instances to terminate, then deletes. Nothing is scaled under `prevent-force-deletion` / `prevent-all-deletion`, for an instance protected from scale in, in `Standby` or retained, or a live warm pool; a failure after the scale-in names the previous capacity. A recorded ARN that differs from the live group's refuses before any write, `--remove-protection` included; a record without one deletes by name. A non-retryable refusal is no longer retried by the superseded-resource in-use wait.
