- **An IoT thing type is now replaced when its `ThingTypeProperties` change its searchable attributes or description, and kept on a `Mqtt5Configuration` change, as CloudFormation does (issue [#7083](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7083))** -- `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/rules.ts`, `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/predicates.ts`, `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/evidence.ts`, `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/evidence-baseline.ts`, `src/provisioning/cloud-control/unapplied-update.ts`. Before, such a change was sent as an in-place update; Cloud Control refuses one for the description. A reorder of the searchable attributes alone does not replace the thing type, since IoT keeps them as an unordered set. An `AWS::EC2::TrafficMirrorSession` `NetworkInterfaceId` change that Cloud Control reports applied while the session keeps its interface now fails the update instead of only warning. Throwaway probes also measured more rows of the unmeasured spec-replacement baseline, each now with a recorded verdict.
