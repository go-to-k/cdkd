- **Changing an `AWS::EC2::Route`'s target (for example `GatewayId` to `TransitGatewayId`) now updates the route in place with one `ReplaceRoute` call, as CloudFormation does, instead of deleting and re-creating it (issue [#5068](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5068))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ec2-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/ec2-route-replace-5068.test.ts`, and the `ec2-route-targets` integ's Phase 2. The old `DeleteRoute` + `CreateRoute` left the route table without the route between the two calls, so traffic to that destination dropped for the window, and a refused create (a target not yet attached, a permission, a throttle) left the route deleted. The update now reports the route as updated in place rather than replaced, and a rollback of a target change replaces the target back. A change to the route table or destination is createOnly and still replaces the route.
