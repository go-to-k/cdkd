- **`AWS::KinesisFirehose::DeliveryStream` now warns when an `S3BackupMode` change on an Elasticsearch, OpenSearch Service or OpenSearch Serverless destination is not applied (issue [#5386](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5386))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/firehose-provider.ts`. Firehose cannot change the backup mode of an existing stream on these destinations. CloudFormation finishes such an update without applying it and without replacing the stream, and cdkd does the same, but the deploy used to report success without saying anything. It now prints a warning that names the destination's `S3BackupMode` and says how to apply it: replace the stream, for example by giving it a new `DeliveryStreamName` or a new logical id.
