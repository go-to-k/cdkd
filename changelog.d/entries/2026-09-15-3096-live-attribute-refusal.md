A settled instance with no public address still resolves to the known `''` (CloudFormation parity, the provider's own three-state rule). `AWS::RDS::DBProxy` / `DBProxyEndpoint` `VpcId` with no recorded value refuse without a live read (marked non-retryable; no RDS client in the resolver).
