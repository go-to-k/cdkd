- **A cluster update changing capacity providers and settings together no longer fails with `UpdateInProgressException` (issue [#6562](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6562))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/cluster-task-definition.ts`, the new `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/cluster-attachments.ts`, unit tests and the `ecs-cluster-capacity-settings` integ fixture. `PutClusterCapacityProviders` leaves an `AWS::ECS::Cluster`'s attachments `UPDATE_IN_PROGRESS` for a few seconds, and cdkd sent `UpdateCluster` for `ClusterSettings` right after it: ECS refused it, cdkd's retry re-sent the Put into the same busy cluster, and the deploy rolled back, intermittently, where CloudFormation deployed the same change. cdkd now sends `PutClusterCapacityProviders` only when `CapacityProviders` or `DefaultCapacityProviderStrategy` changed (before, every update of a cluster declaring either sent it), waits for the attachments to leave `UPDATE_IN_PROGRESS` before the `UpdateCluster` that follows a Put (up to 600s, raised by `--resource-timeout AWS::ECS::Cluster=...`), and re-sends a cluster write ECS refuses while the attachments are still updating. An update that sends no Put makes no extra call.
