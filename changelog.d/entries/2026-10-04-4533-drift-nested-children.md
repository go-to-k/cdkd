- **`cdkd drift <Parent>` checks the parent's nested stacks too, so a resource deleted or changed inside one exits 1 (issue [#4533](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4533))** -- `src/cli/commands/drift.ts`, `docs/_contents/cli-drift.md`, `docs/_contents/design/459-nested-stacks.md`. A nested stack's resources are recorded as their own `<parent>~<child>` stack, which drift compared only when named exactly or under `--all`, so `cdkd drift Parent` printed the parent's `AWS::CloudFormation::Stack` row as `drift unknown` and exited 0 over a deleted child resource. Naming a stack (or the single-stack auto-select) now also checks every nested stack below it in the same region, after the parent, each in its own block and each record once, as `cdkd diff` and `cdkd destroy` act on nested stacks. The auto-select counts a parent with its nested stacks as one stack. The nested row is now `skipped` when its record is checked, and `deleted` (exit 1, printed as `RECORD MISSING` naming the record, `nestedStackRecordMissing: true` in `--json`) when that record is gone from state; a parent holding only nested stacks prints `no drift detected here` rather than `NOTHING was compared`; `--accept` / `--revert` refuse it by name and act on each nested stack's drifted resources under that record's lock. **`--json` change:** the row moves from `notSupported` to `skipped`, or to `deleted`. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift.test.ts`; integ arm in `tests/integration/nested-stack/verify.sh`.
