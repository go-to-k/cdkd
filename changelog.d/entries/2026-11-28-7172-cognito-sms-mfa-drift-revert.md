- **`cdkd drift --revert` now works on a Cognito user pool with an `SmsConfiguration` under `MfaConfiguration: OFF`, and `cdkd drift` reports a leftover software-token factor beside SMS (issues [#7175](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7175), [#7172](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7172))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cognito/{update,mfa-request,read-state}.ts`. A pool with an `SmsConfiguration` reads `EnabledMfas: [SMS_MFA]` back even under OFF. That happens with CDK's phone sign-in, phone auto-verify, or `enableSmsRole: true`, all under the default `mfa: OFF`. The revert sent that list back, and the MFA pre-flight refused `SMS_MFA` beside OFF, so every revert of such a pool failed, whatever had drifted. A bag derived from the readback now leaves `SMS_MFA` out under OFF on both sides, and the pool keeps its SMS block. Second fix: the #7134 drift rule stripped the software token only from a baseline that listed the token alone. A pool that a pre-#7128 release took from OPTIONAL + SMS + token to OFF recorded `[SMS_MFA, SOFTWARE_TOKEN_MFA]` and drift reported it clean. Its baseline now loses the token too, so drift reports it, `--revert` clears it, and the next drift is clean. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift-cognito-mfa-sms-leftover-7172.test.ts`; integ `cognito-custom-attribute-add` Phase 2f.
