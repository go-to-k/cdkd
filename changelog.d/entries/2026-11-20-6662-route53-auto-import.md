- **`cdkd import` finds Route 53 records and record set groups without `--resource` (issue [#6662](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6662))** -- `src/cli/commands/import/sibling-refs.ts` (new), `locked-import.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/route53/import.ts` (`importRecordSetFromTemplate`) and `record-set-group.ts` (`importRecordSetGroupFromTemplate`), `docs/import.md`. Auto mode adopted the hosted zone and reported every record in it as `no matching AWS resource`, and with a `--resource-mapping` each record's `HostedZoneId: {Ref: Zone}` stayed unresolved, so every record was adopted unverified. The import loop now dispatches a `Ref` target before the resources naming it and substitutes its adopted `Ref` value, and a record is found by its zone, `Name`, `Type` and `SetIdentifier`, verified live (a group: every member), and recorded under cdkd's composite id. A mapped record is verified by its own `SetIdentifier`. Integ fixture `route53-drift-import`.
