- **`AWS::CloudTrail::Trail` keeps the SDK route for `AdvancedEventSelectors` and `AggregationConfigurations` (issue [#2762](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2762)).** A trail declaring either used to go through Cloud Control. `AdvancedEventSelectors` now rides on `PutEventSelectors`, the same call as `EventSelectors`, and `AggregationConfigurations` on `PutEventConfiguration`. Both are read back for `cdkd drift`. Removing `AdvancedEventSelectors` from the template resets the trail to the default selector a trail without selectors has, as removing `EventSelectors` already did. Removing `AggregationConfigurations` clears them. Reading the aggregation back needs `cloudtrail:GetEventConfiguration`, and deploying it needs `cloudtrail:PutEventConfiguration`. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/cloudtrail-provider.ts`.
