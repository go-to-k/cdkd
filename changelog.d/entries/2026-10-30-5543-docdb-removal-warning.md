- **cdkd now warns when a DocumentDB property that it keeps on removal is removed from the template (issues [#5543](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5543), [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160)).** On a cluster this covers `EngineVersion`, `PreferredBackupWindow`, `PreferredMaintenanceWindow`, `DBClusterParameterGroupName`, `DeletionProtection`, `BackupRetentionPeriod` and `VpcSecurityGroupIds`; on a DB instance, `PreferredMaintenanceWindow`. A real CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation keeps each one at its live value when it is removed: the cluster stays on its engine version, its custom parameter group and its windows. cdkd already kept them, but did so silently. It now prints one warning per resource after a successful update. On a rollback revert the warning says the value is absent from the state being restored instead. `AWS::DocDB::DBCluster` and `AWS::DocDB::DBInstance` are also registered in `removalHandledInUpdate`. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/docdb-provider.ts`.
