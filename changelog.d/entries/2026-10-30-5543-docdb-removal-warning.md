- **A DocumentDB cluster's removed `EngineVersion`, `PreferredBackupWindow`, `PreferredMaintenanceWindow` or `DBClusterParameterGroupName`, and a DB instance's removed `PreferredMaintenanceWindow`, are now named in a warning (issue [#5543](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5543), [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160)).** A real CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation keeps each of them at its live value when it is removed from the template (the cluster stays on its engine version, its custom parameter group and its windows), which is what cdkd already did; cdkd now says so in one warning per resource instead of keeping them silently. `AWS::DocDB::DBCluster` and `AWS::DocDB::DBInstance` are also registered in `removalHandledInUpdate`, so every handled property's removal is accounted for. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/docdb-provider.ts`.
