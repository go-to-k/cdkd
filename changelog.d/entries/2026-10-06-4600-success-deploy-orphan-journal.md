- **A successful deploy now acts on a resource a failed CREATE made before it failed, instead of silently dropping the rollback journal that is its only record (issue [#4600](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4600))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/{rollback,deploy-flow}.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/journaled-orphans.ts`, `src/deployment/nested-child-journal.ts`, `src/state/s3-state-backend.ts`. After #4584 the automatic rollback, `cdkd rollback` and `cdkd destroy` acted on such a journaled resource (Kinesis so far), but a later successful deploy deleted the journal unconditionally, and a top-level deploy deleted each nested stack's journal the same way, leaving the resource in AWS with nothing in cdkd naming it. Now the deploy deletes it per its journaled `DeletionPolicy` (`Retain` keeps it, `Snapshot` snapshots it) when, after the deploy, no state record sits under its logical id, the deploy completed no operation under it, and no resource record of this stack or of another stack under the same state prefix holds its type and physical id. Otherwise it warns, naming the physical id, drops the entry with the journal, and counts it with the skipped deletes, so the deploy exits `2` (`--allow-unaddressed` exits `0`); a fix-forward that keeps the logical id under another name lands here, and deleting the earlier resource there is follow-up #4606. An entry the deploy could not act on (a failed delete, an interrupt, an unreadable record) keeps the journal, reduced to it where it can, for the next successful deploy. Nested stacks are settled under their own lock against their own record. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/{deploy-engine-rollback-journal,deploy-engine-nested-journal,nested-child-journal,journaled-orphans-success}.test.ts`, `tests/unit/state/{s3-state-backend,malformed-resources-bag}.test.ts`; integ Phase O steps O6-O8 in `tests/integration/rollback-command/`.
