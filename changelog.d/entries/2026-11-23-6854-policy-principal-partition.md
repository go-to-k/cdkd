- **A failed `AWS::SQS::QueuePolicy` / `AWS::SNS::TopicPolicy` create's cleanup compares an account principal in the resource's partition (issue [#6854](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6854))** -- `src/provisioning/policy-document-content.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/{sqs-queue,sns-topic}-policy-provider.ts`. The comparison that decides whether a queue or topic still holds the failed create's policy treated `arn:aws-cn:iam::<id>:root` as `arn:aws:iam::<id>:root`; SQS and SNS refuse a root ARN of another partition, so the two are now different principals. A bare account id still equals its root ARN in the resource's partition, and no principal text can spell an account's comparison key.
