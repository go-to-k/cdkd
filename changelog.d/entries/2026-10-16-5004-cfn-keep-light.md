- **Removing a property from the template no longer resets it for 7 properties CloudFormation keeps (issue [#5004](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5004))** -- A real CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation leaves the live value in place when these are dropped from a template, where cdkd used to reset them. `AWS::SQS::Queue`: `SqsManagedSseEnabled`, `DeduplicationScope`, `FifoThroughputLimit`; `AWS::SecretsManager::Secret`: `Description`, `KmsKeyId`; `AWS::IAM::Role`: `MaxSessionDuration`; `AWS::Lambda::Url`: `InvokeMode`. cdkd now sends nothing for them on removal. The other SQS attributes, IAM Role `Description` and Lambda Url `Cors` are reset by CloudFormation and keep cdkd's reset. Changed: `src/provisioning/providers/{sqs-queue,secretsmanager-secret,iam-role,lambda-url}-provider.ts`, `docs/provider-rules.md`, the matching unit tests and the `fifo-sqs-event-source`, `iam-role-prefixed-name-update`, `sns-sqs-event`, `secrets-dynamic-ref` and `cloudfront-function-url` integ fixtures, which now assert the value is retained.
