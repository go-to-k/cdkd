- **`destroy --remove-protection` on a Cognito User Pool with a `DEVELOPER` (SES) `EmailConfiguration` now says plainly when a write reset it to Cognito's default sender (issue [#4149](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4149))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cognito-provider.ts` and `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/cognito-remove-protection-rmw.test.ts`. Measured in us-east-1: an `UpdateUserPool` that omits a `DEVELOPER` `EmailConfiguration` resets it to `EmailSendingAccount: COGNITO_DEFAULT`, dropping its SES `SourceArn`; echoed back unchanged it is accepted and kept. The `DeletionProtection` flip already echoes it; when AWS refuses that echo and cdkd re-sends without it, the warn used to say a reset was "not measured" -- it now says the email configuration WAS reset, and if the delete then fails and the live pool reads back on the default sender, cdkd reports that at ERROR with how to put it back (a redeploy will not). A re-send that fails with no clear answer (a timeout or 5xx, which may have landed) now warns of the reset on the throttle/5xx retry path and on the re-enable after a failed delete, where it used to say nothing. The ERROR report after a bare flip now lists the `DEVELOPER` sending account among what the pool held. Turning the guard back on after a failed delete, cdkd no longer sends a write without the SES settings to a pool that already reads it on, unless the flip-off's own write without them is known to have landed.
