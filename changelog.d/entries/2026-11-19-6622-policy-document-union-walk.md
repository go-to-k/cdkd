- **`cdkd drift` reports a key added out of band inside an IAM policy document on a resource with no observed baseline, and `--revert` removes it (issue [#6622](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6622))** -- new `src/analyzer/drift-verbatim-paths.ts` (`verbatimEchoDriftKeys`: `PolicyDocument` on `AWS::IAM::Policy` / `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy`, `AssumeRolePolicyDocument` on `AWS::IAM::Role`), `src/analyzer/drift-calculator.ts` (`unionWalkPaths`), `src/cli/commands/drift/compare.ts`, `revert-payload.ts`, `revert-merge.ts`, `revert-outcome.ts`, `plan.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-policy/read-state.ts`, the `drift-revert` integ fixture, and `docs/cli-drift.md` / `cli-drift-accept-revert.md` / `cli-drift-internals.md`. On the template-only `properties` baseline (`--no-capture-observed-state`, or a failed capture) the comparator walked only the template's keys inside an object, so a `Condition` added to a single-object `Statement` -- narrowing a `Deny` -- read clean. Under those keys it now walks the union of keys on either baseline, since IAM returns a document as submitted; the revert sends the template's document whole there instead of keeping the live side's untemplated keys, and the plan no longer lists them as preserved. A list-form `Statement` was already compared whole. Other services' resource policies (S3, SQS, SNS, KMS) are not covered until their readback is measured.
