- **`AWS::Lambda::EventSourceMapping`: a `BatchSize` removed from the template is reset to the source's default, and a removed `LoggingConfig` or `ProvisionedPollerConfig` on a Kafka mapping is cleared, as CloudFormation does (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-eventsource-provider.ts`. `UpdateEventSourceMapping` keeps a field it is not sent, so cdkd used to leave all three at their old AWS value while the deploy succeeded and `cdkd diff` reported no changes. A CloudFormation A/B (us-east-1; CloudTrail read of the update CloudFormation sent) measured the reset on SQS, DynamoDB-stream and self-managed Kafka mappings: `BatchSize` 10 for SQS and 100 for DynamoDB and self-managed Kafka, and `{}` for a self-managed Kafka mapping's `LoggingConfig` / `ProvisionedPollerConfig`. Kinesis, MQ and DocumentDB take the Lambda API reference default of 100, MSK is assumed to match self-managed Kafka, and an unclassifiable source gets nothing. CloudFormation keeps an SQS mapping's `ProvisionedPollerConfig` and a Kafka mapping's `MaximumBatchingWindowInSeconds`, and cdkd still does too. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/lambda-eventsource-provider.test.ts`; the `sqs-esm-max-concurrency` integ fixture now drops `batchSize` in its update phase, and `lambda-esm-self-managed-kafka` gains a Phase 2c dropping `BatchSize`, the poller config and a `LoggingConfig`.
