- **A DynamoDB table's `KinesisStreamSpecification.ApproximateCreationDateTimePrecision` change on the same stream is now sent (issue [#5385](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5385))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`. The change used to be logged as a warning and never reached AWS, while the deploy reported success. It now goes through `UpdateKinesisStreamingDestination`: cdkd waits up to 5 minutes for a destination the previous deploy left `ENABLING` to turn `ACTIVE` (AWS refuses an update otherwise), sends the new precision, and waits up to 10 minutes for the destination to read back `ACTIVE` at it (measured: about 3.5 minutes); both waits are interruptible and end at the per-resource deadline. Drift now also reads an `UPDATING` destination instead of reporting the spec as removed. A precision AWS already holds is success. Removing the member leaves the live precision as it is, which is what CloudFormation does (measured with a stack update in us-east-1).
