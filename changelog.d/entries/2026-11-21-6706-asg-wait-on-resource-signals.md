- **An `--update-policy` ASG rolling update honors `WaitOnResourceSignals` through the signal receiver (issue [#6706](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6706))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/signal-receiver.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/signal-receiver-run.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-in-place.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/asg/instance-refresh.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/asg/update.ts`, `src/types/resource/contexts.ts`. When the group's launch template sends a signed `cfn-signal --resource <group>` (AWS CDK `initOptions.includeRole: true`), the receiver is armed right before the Instance Refresh starts for one signal per instance it replaces (in service, not scale-in protected, at most the desired capacity): a `FAILURE` signal cancels the refresh and fails the update, and the update also fails when the signals do not all arrive within `PauseTime` after the refresh finished; the deploy's rollback reverts the group. Batches still advance after `PauseTime`. Unsigned signals keep the warning, which now names `includeRole: true`.
