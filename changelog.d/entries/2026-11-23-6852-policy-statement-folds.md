- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports phantom drift on a policy document whose service returned an equivalent spelling (issue [#6852](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6852))** -- `src/analyzer/drift-verbatim-paths.ts`, `src/analyzer/drift-calculator.ts`, `tests/unit/analyzer/drift-policy-folds-6852.test.ts`, `docs/design/6852-drift-normalization-rules.md`. On a resource whose drift baseline is the template, inside the `Statement` of an IAM, S3, SQS, SNS, KMS or Secrets Manager policy document, the comparator now reads as one value what those services return for what was sent (measured on real AWS): a one-element `Action` / `Resource` / principal list and its scalar, a bare account id under `Principal.AWS` and its `arn:<partition>:iam::<id>:root`, `Effect` in another case, and a Condition number or boolean and its exact string form (`1.2` and `"1.2"`). Order, `"Sid": ""`, and a Condition value's scalar against a one-element list are still compared as written. The design doc adds a cross-check of each policy rule against hashicorp/awspolicyequivalence and cdk-real-drift.
