- **`cdkd drift --revert` no longer revokes a standalone security-group rule whose protocol, ports or peer were changed out of band, and drift reports that change on the rule itself (issue [#6641](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6641))**. `ModifySecurityGroupRules` keeps a rule's `sgr-` id, but a group matched a sibling `AWS::EC2::SecurityGroupIngress` / `Egress` record's rule only by protocol, ports and peer. The moved rule read as an extra rule of the group, which the group's revert revoked, and the rule's record read as deleted. A rule someone added at the record's old values was taken for the record's rule. Now, on `cdkd drift` and `--revert`, a group with a sibling record that holds an `sgr-` id has its rules read with their ids (`DescribeSecurityGroupRules`, once per such group per read of recorded state, never on the deploy's post-write capture or the rollback replay's refresh), and ownership is by id on both sides: the rule carrying the id is the record's (its change is drift on the rule resource, whose revert refuses, issue [#6638](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6638)); a rule at the record's values under another id, while that id is still live elsewhere, is reported on the group and revoked by its revert. The group's revert reads the ids before any write and refuses rather than revoke a rule a sibling holds, or when the ids cannot be read; a Cloud Control-provisioned group with sibling rule records refuses any change to its rule lists.
