- **An `AWS::LakeFormation::Resource` switching from the service-linked role to its own role now updates in place, as in CloudFormation (issue [#7121](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7121))** -- `src/provisioning/cloud-control/**`. Moving a registered location from `UseServiceLinkedRole: true` to `UseServiceLinkedRole: false` plus a `RoleArn` failed the deploy with "Resource managed by Service Linked Role": Cloud Control's update calls Lake Formation's `UpdateResource`, which refuses that switch. CloudFormation deregisters the location and registers it again with the new role, keeping its ARN, and cdkd now does the same. Lake Formation permissions granted on the location are kept. If the new registration fails, cdkd registers the location back with the service-linked role before it reports the error.
