- **`cdkd local start-service`, `start-alb`, `start-cloudfront`, `start-agentcore`, `start-web-function` and `start-microvm` now emulate an execution role that does not trust the caller, and take `--emulate-role-via <arn>` (issue [#6215](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6215))** -- `package.json` (`cdk-local` `^0.165.1` -> `^0.166.0`). Before this change, a refused `--assume-role` on these commands stopped with the AssumeRole error (the ECS service commands) or ran with the caller's credentials (the Lambda-backed paths). Now the role's policies are applied as session policies, so a permission the role lacks fails locally, as it already did on `local invoke`, `local start-api`, `local run-task` and `local invoke-agentcore`.
