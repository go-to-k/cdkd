- **`cdkd drift` now reports an email-OTP factor left on a Cognito user pool under `MfaConfiguration: OFF`, and `cdkd drift --revert` clears it instead of refusing the pool (issue [#7177](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7177))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cognito/{update,mfa-request,read-state}.ts`. A release before #7135 moved a pool from email-OTP MFA to OFF with `UpdateUserPool` alone, which leaves the pool's `EmailMfaConfiguration` in place (measured). The readback then lists `EMAIL_OTP` with the email message and subject under OFF. Drift compared that leftover against itself and reported the pool clean. `drift --revert` sent the block back beside OFF, and the MFA pre-flight refused every revert of the pool, whatever had drifted. The drift baseline of a template that declares no MFA factor (WebAuthn properties alone included) now loses `EMAIL_OTP` and reads the message and subject as empty, so drift reports the leftover. A bag derived from the readback no longer sends the email block under OFF. The previous side keeps the factor, so the revert sends the bare `SetUserPoolMfaConfig` OFF reset, which clears it (measured), and records the cleared block, so the next drift is clean. Templates, deploys and rollback replays are unchanged. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift-cognito-mfa-email-echo-7177.test.ts`.
