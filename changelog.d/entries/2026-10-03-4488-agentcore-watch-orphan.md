- **`cdkd local invoke-agentcore --watch` no longer leaves a container behind when ^C lands while a rebuild re-synthesizes or starts its new container (issue [#4488](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4488))** -- `src/cli/commands/local-invoke-agentcore.ts` (`bootAgentCoreContainer`, `rebuildAgentCoreContainer`, `ContainerStartGuard`). A rebuild removes the old container, re-synthesizes, then runs `docker run` for the new one. A ^C after the removal reached a cleanup that knew no container id, so it exited while the `docker run` was still starting a container nothing then removed. Once the cleanup begins, a rebuild now starts no container, and a container whose `docker run` was already in flight is removed before the process exits, with a line saying the ^C is waiting for that `docker run`. The wait is bounded at 15 s; past it, cdkd removes the container by its name if it exists, exits, and warns with the `docker rm -f <name>` to run in case a late `docker run` still starts it. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/local-invoke-agentcore-stdout-stream.test.ts`.
