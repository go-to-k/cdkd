- **A destroy, template removal, delete-first replacement or rollback under `DeletionPolicy: Snapshot` no longer reuses an EBS volume's completed final snapshot from an older failed delete; it takes a fresh one, so writes since that attempt are not lost (issue [#6473](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6473))** -- `src/provisioning/final-snapshot/{ebs,shared,pre-delete}.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/delete-resource.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/replacement/delete-context.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/names.ts`, unit tests, `docs/cli-destroy-internals.md`, `docs/cli-destroy-guards.md`. The reuse probe used to take any `pending` or `completed` snapshot tagged `cdkd:final-snapshot-of: <volumeId>`. Now an in-flight snapshot is still resumed whatever its age, so an interrupted destroy re-run resumes its own, while a completed one is reused only when it started at or after this delete attempt's start (`attemptStartedAt`); nothing is persisted. The deletes of a replaced resource's old copy keep their stricter bound (go-to-k/cdkd#6463). The cost is one extra snapshot when a re-run follows an attempt whose snapshot had completed. The re-run hints of the EBS, Redshift and ElastiCache snapshot-wait failures now say an in-progress snapshot is resumed, where they claimed a completed one is reused.
