- **Ctrl-C now stops an `AWS::RDS::DBProxy` or `AWS::RDS::DBProxyEndpoint` deploy that is waiting for the proxy or endpoint to become `available` (issue [#6517](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6517))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/rds-dbproxy/{shared,create,update}.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/rds-dbproxy-endpoint-provider.ts`. The create waits and the waits after `ModifyDBProxy` / `ModifyDBProxyEndpoint` ignored Ctrl-C and could keep calling `DescribeDBProxies` / `DescribeDBProxyEndpoints` for up to 30 minutes. They also kept polling after the per-resource deadline (`--resource-timeout`) had already failed the resource. Each wait now stops within a second of Ctrl-C. It also stops 30 seconds before the resource deadline runs out and fails with its own "Timed out waiting for ..." error, which names the last status it read. A create interrupted or timed out this way still records the proxy or endpoint it created, so `--revert-failed` can remove it.
