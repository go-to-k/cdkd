- **`cdkd deploy` / `cdkd rollback` / `cdkd destroy`: deleting a Lambda event source mapping that is still settling waits for it instead of failing (issue [#7011](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7011))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-eventsource/delete.ts`. A deploy that replaced a mapping (for example after its SQS queue was renamed) and then failed rolled back by deleting the new mapping seconds after it was created; Lambda refused that with `ResourceInUseException` while the mapping was still `Creating`, the rollback left the new mapping in state and the old one out of it, and the next deploy collided with `ResourceConflictException`. On that refusal the delete now polls the mapping until it leaves `Creating` / `Updating` / `Enabling` / `Disabling` (up to 5 minutes) and retries; an ordinary delete makes the same single call as before. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/lambda-eventsource-delete-in-use-7011.test.ts`; integ `esm-replacement-rollback`.
