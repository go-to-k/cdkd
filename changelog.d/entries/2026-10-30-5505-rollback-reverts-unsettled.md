- **A deploy's automatic rollback now also reverts what the failed update left unsettled, as CloudFormation rolls a failed update back (issue [#5505](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5505))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/rollback.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/execute.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/provision.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/options.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/deployment/unsettled-convergence.ts`, `tests/integration/dynamodb-gsi-update/`. Before, a failed UPDATE that left keys unsettled (a GSI still building, an encryption change in flight) stayed that way until the next deploy or `cdkd rollback --revert-failed`. Now the rollback waits (up to 30 min, Ctrl-C ends it) for the update's abandoned call to return and its keys to stop moving, then reverts them through the `--revert-failed` arm with the pre-failure record as the previous side: only the unsettled keys change, and an item is deleted only when the failed update's accepted calls added it and the record does not name it. Only the failed deploy's own marks are reverted: names an earlier failed deploy (e.g. under `--no-rollback`) left stay for the next deploy. A name clears only on the post-update proof. A key still moving, or a revert that fails, keeps its names and its journal entry with a warning. `--no-rollback` is unchanged.
