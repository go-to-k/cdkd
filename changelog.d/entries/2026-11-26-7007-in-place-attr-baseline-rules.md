- **A reader of a Cloud Control resource's update timestamp follows an in-place update of it (issue [#7007](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7007))** -- `scripts/gen-in-place-attr-coverage.ts`, `src/analyzer/diff-calculator/in-place-cc-derived-attrs.generated.ts`, `src/analyzer/diff-calculator/shared.ts`. A resource reading `Fn::GetAtt` of a Cloud-Control-routed type's last-update time (`UpdatedAt`, `LastModifiedTime`, `LastUpdatedTime` and the like, 372 attributes) stayed `NO_CHANGE` after an in-place update of that resource and kept the old value; it is now re-resolved, and an export of such an attribute counts as changed while a consumer imports it, as CloudFormation refuses a moved export. The in-place attribute audit gains that moving rule and two that settle Cloud Control attributes as never moving: an identity-shaped, unversioned CFn primary-identifier member (the handler contract fixes it across an update), and an own ARN whose documented template names only fixed values. The unclassified baseline shrinks from 3204 to 2246 pairs.
