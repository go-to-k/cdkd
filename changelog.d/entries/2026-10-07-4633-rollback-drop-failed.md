- **`cdkd rollback <stack> --drop-failed <logicalId>` drops one journaled failed CREATE whose delete can never succeed (issue [#4633](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4633))** -- `src/cli/commands/rollback-drop-failed.ts`, `src/cli/commands/rollback.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/journaled-orphans.ts`. A failed CREATE whose resource cdkd proved it made is kept in the rollback journal until cdkd deletes that resource; when the delete kept failing for a cause the operator could not fix (a secret or KMS key they cannot read, go-to-k/cdkd#4612), every `cdkd deploy` exited 2 and every `cdkd destroy` ended "State preserved", and the only way out was removing the stack's `rollback-journal.json`, which dropped every other entry too. The new option removes exactly that entry (and the failed replacement UPDATE recorded beside it, go-to-k/cdkd#4604) under the stack lock, after printing the resource it names (masked where the name derives from a secret, with a policy's queue or topic targets) and asking for confirmation (`--force` / `-y` skip it; a non-interactive run without them is refused). It replays nothing and deletes nothing in AWS. It refuses an unknown id, a completed operation, a failed operation of any other kind or one a newer journal entry may own (neither blocks either command), an id with several such entries, a state record that cannot be read, and combination with `--orphan`, `--revert-failed` or `--skip-final-snapshot`. The retry warnings of `cdkd destroy`, a successful `cdkd deploy` and `cdkd rollback` now print the pasteable command for each such entry whose delete they tried and that failed. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/rollback-drop-failed-4633.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/rollback-drop-failed-cli-4633.test.ts`.
