- **`cdkd diff` evaluates `Conditions` per condition, so one secret-fed or unbindable parameter no longer turns every `Fn::If` in the stack FALSE (issue [#4470](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4470))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive.ts`. The diff used to skip the WHOLE `Conditions` section when any template parameter failed to bind or any one condition read a parameter fed a secret `{{resolve:...}}` reference, so every `Fn::If` resolved to its FALSE branch, even on a condition that reads no parameter at all: a condition-true property diffed as a perpetual UPDATE and `cdkd diff --fail` exited 1 on an unchanged stack, at the top level and in nested children alike. Parameters now bind one at a time, and only the conditions that reach an unbound or secret-fed parameter, directly or through a `{Condition: X}` chain, go unevaluated. They still never prune a resource, so no phantom DELETE is reported. Every other condition gets the verdict the deploy computes. An `Fn::If` on a condition that itself depends on an unbound or secret-fed parameter still takes its FALSE branch, unchanged. Residual: go-to-k/cdkd#4479. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/diff-per-condition-4470.test.ts`.
