- **Deleting an `AWS::ECS::Service` on the SDK route now waits for the service to reach `INACTIVE` (issue [#5664](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5664))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ecs-provider.ts`, a new unit file, `docs/cli-deploy-waits.md`, and the `ecs-service-gateway-connectors` integ. `DeleteService` returns while the service is still `DRAINING`, and cdkd reported the service deleted at once, so its dependents were deleted against a live service. A service with a gateway connector left the gateway's cluster association `DISASSOCIATING`, so the gateway's delete was refused with `Cannot delete gateway with active cluster associations` and the destroy stalled. The delete now polls `DescribeServices` until the service is `INACTIVE`, `MISSING`, or its cluster is gone, as CloudFormation waits. The cap is 600 seconds, or the `--resource-timeout` set for `AWS::ECS::Service` if longer. A wait that runs out fails the delete and is not retried. A service that an earlier attempt already deleted, and that is still draining, is waited for too.
