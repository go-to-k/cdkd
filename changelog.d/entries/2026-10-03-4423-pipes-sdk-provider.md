- **`AWS::Pipes::Pipe` has an SDK provider, so a stream or broker source's batch settings change in place (issue [#4423](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4423))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/pipes-provider.ts` (new), `src/provisioning/{register-providers,provider-registry}.ts`, new dependency `@aws-sdk/client-pipes`. A change to a mutable `SourceParameters` member of a Kinesis, DynamoDB Streams, MSK, self-managed Kafka, ActiveMQ or RabbitMQ source (e.g. `KinesisStreamParameters.BatchSize`) failed with "createOnlyProperties [...StartingPosition] cannot be updated": Cloud Control cannot express that update at all. The provider calls `UpdatePipe`, whose source shape has no create-only member, and waits for the pipe to settle after a create or update and to disappear after a delete. A pipe recorded `provisionedBy: cc-api` by an earlier cdkd returns to the SDK provider on its next in-place update under the same name (a `cc-broken` sticky exemption). Removing `TargetParameters` or `EnrichmentParameters` from the template is reported, not applied. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/pipes-provider.test.ts`; integ: `cc-write-only-create-only` (a `BatchSize` change on a cc-api-seeded record), `eventbridge-pipes`.
