- **`cdkd drift` reports an SNS subscription whose topic was deleted, or deleted and re-created under the same name, as deleted outside cdkd (issue [#6711](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6711))**. SNS keeps answering `GetSubscriptionAttributes` for such a subscription with the attributes of a live one, while the topic no longer lists it and nothing published reaches it, so drift read it as clean and could print `no drift detected`. The subscription read now also confirms the topic lists the subscription (`ListSubscriptionsByTopic`, paged until found; a subscription still `PendingConfirmation`, which the topic lists under that literal, matches by protocol and endpoint), and reports it deleted when the topic does not list it or is gone. A membership read that fails (for example a missing `sns:ListSubscriptionsByTopic` permission) reports the read as failed rather than clean. `cdkd import` records no baseline for such a subscription, and `cdkd state refresh-observed` reports it deleted outside cdkd instead of refreshing it. The capture right after cdkd's own create or update makes no extra call, so a deploy is unchanged.
