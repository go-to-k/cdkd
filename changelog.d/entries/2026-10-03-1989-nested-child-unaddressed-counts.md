- **`cdkd deploy` counts what a nested stack's child left unaddressed, and exits 2 for it (issue [#1989](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1989))** -- `src/provisioning/{nested-stack-context,providers/nested-stack-provider}.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/{provision,options}.ts`. The parent discarded the child engine's result, so a skipped DELETE or an orphaned replacement predecessor inside a nested child (or grandchild) printed its own warning and nothing else: the parent's `Skipped (not deleted)` / `of which left an orphaned predecessor` rows stayed at 0, `RunCounts.skipped` was unset, the stack reported `Deployment completed successfully`, and the run exited 0 (the #1960 exit code). The child's `deleteSkipped` / `updatePartial` now reach the parent's counters through a per-row slot on the nested-stack context, recursively, so those rows, the warning banner, the events record and exit 2 (`--allow-unaddressed` opts out) cover nested children too. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-nested-child-unaddressed{,-e2e}.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/nested-stack-provider.test.ts`; integ: `nested-stack-3level`.
