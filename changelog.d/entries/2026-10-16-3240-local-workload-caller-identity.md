- **The four engine `cdkd local start-service` / `start-alb` / `start-cloudfront` / `start-agentcore` commands no longer hand the `--role-arn` role to the emulated workload (issue [#3240](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3240))** -- `src/cli/commands/local-invoke.ts`, `src/local/local-role-arn.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-state-source.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-start-{service,alb,cloudfront,agentcore}.ts`, `local-invoke.ts`, `local-start-api.ts`, `local-run-task.ts`, `local-invoke-agentcore.ts`. With `--role-arn` and no profile, these commands copied the role's credentials into containers, injected ECS task secrets and `{{resolve:...}}` values fetched with the role, resolved `${AWS::AccountId}` to the role's account, read `--from-cfn-stack` as the role and made the `--assume-role` hop as the role. They now resolve all of it as the caller (pass `--profile` to choose the identity), via cdk-local 0.152.0's `workloadIdentity: 'caller'`. A literal layer ARN on `local invoke` / `start-api` is likewise fetched as the caller now, and those four cdkd-owned commands record their assume with cdk-local through `applyRoleArnForLocal`. The startup warning the engine commands printed is gone. Synthesis, the ECR pull and `--from-state` reads still use the role. Tests: `tests/unit/local/local-role-arn.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/local-embed-config.test.ts`.
