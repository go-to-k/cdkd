- **`cdkd state destroy '<parent>~<child>'` resolves a secret principal reference the child's state record places in a region (issue [#4887](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4887))** -- `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`, `src/provisioning/secret-principal-resolution.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/nested-stack-provider.ts`, `iam-policy-provider.ts`, `iam-user-group-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/region-check.ts`. A nested child destroyed on its own skipped every `AWS::IAM::Policy` / `AWS::IAM::UserToGroupAddition` whose principal list holds a `{{resolve:...}}` reference (record kept, exit 2), because the regions its parent reads from are unknown there. It now resolves a region-less reference its state schema v12 record places in a region, in that region, and removes the policy or memberships. A record holding any other region-less reference keeps the skip and the hint to destroy it through the parent, and the child's own nested children inherit that limit instead of reading the child's regions as complete. No new AWS call.
