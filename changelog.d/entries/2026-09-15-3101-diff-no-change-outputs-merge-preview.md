- **`cdkd diff --fail` now reports an Outputs change the next no-change deploy writes beside an output that keeps failing, instead of omitting the whole Outputs section and exiting 0 (issue [#3101](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3101))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive.ts` (`computeStackDiff`), `src/analyzer/outputs-diff.ts` (`failedOutputKeys`, `failuresMirrorDeploy`), `docs/cli-diff.md`, with cases in `tests/unit/cli/diff-recursive.test.ts` and `tests/unit/analyzer/outputs-diff.test.ts`. Since go-to-k/cdkd#2771 a deploy with no resource change persists the outputs that resolved and keeps a failed output's stored value, but the diff still dropped the section whenever one output failed. When the resource diff is empty and every failure is one the deploy records too (the resolver threw, or returned nothing), the diff now previews through the deploy's own `mergeNoChangeOutputs`: an added or changed sibling renders its row, a failed output shows no row and is named in a warning, as compared at its stored value or as having none under its own name (the deploy may still resolve it), and unless every value carried from state (an alias included) is a secret expression the rendered rows withhold their previous values, the warning saying why when a row loses one, and the merge's keep-whole refusals keep the section omitted as before, except the deploy's save-time re-check, which the diff cannot run. The section is still omitted when a resource change is pending, when a condition verdict can reach an output (its own `Condition`, or `Fn::If` / `{Condition}` outside `Resources` and `Conditions`; evaluated best-effort, so a verdict can differ from the deploy's) or its parameters cannot be bound or its conditions evaluated for the diff, when a failure is one only the diff reports (a surviving intrinsic, an `AWS::NoValue` symbol, an unsubstituted `Fn::Sub` placeholder, or a container holding one), and when an `Export.Name` cannot be resolved or decided.
