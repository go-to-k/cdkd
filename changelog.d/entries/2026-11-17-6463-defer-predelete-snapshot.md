- **A create-first replacement of an EBS volume, a Redshift cluster or an ElastiCache replication group under `UpdateReplacePolicy: Snapshot` now deletes the old copy at the end of the deploy, taking its snapshot right before that delete, so a failed deploy's rollback adopts the old copy with its data instead of re-creating it empty (issue [#6463](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6463), finishing [#6445](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6445))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/replacement/{delete-context,delete-replaced,create-first}.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-replace/create-first.ts`, `src/deployment/superseded-resources.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/superseded-retry.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/under-lock.ts`, `src/provisioning/final-snapshot/{pre-delete,ebs,shared}.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/nested-cleanup.ts`, unit tests, a new `replace-predelete-snapshot-failed-rollback` integ fixture, and the rollback and replacement docs. No snapshot is taken before the create: the deferred delete snapshots the old copy, waits, then deletes it, and a failed deploy takes none. The `supersededResources` entry marks the snapshot as owed, so a deploy stopped first leaves the next `cdkd deploy` or `cdkd destroy` to take it before the delete; once taken, a failed delete is retried without another. A copy AWS cannot snapshot (a paused cluster, a modifying group) is kept, not deleted; only a snapshot started since the replacement is reused, and a Ctrl-C during it stops before the delete. An older cdkd refuses to delete such an entry; its `--skip-final-snapshot` advice does not apply, so upgrade cdkd. Both copies stay in AWS, and are billed, until the deploy ends, and writes reaching the old copy meanwhile are in its snapshot.
