- **A deploy that turns off a GlobalTable replica's Kinesis streaming no longer records the destination in the drift baseline (found by the issue [#5399](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5399) integ)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts` (`waitForKinesisDisableVisible`, `kinesisDisableSettleDelaysMs`) and a unit case. `DisableKinesisStreamingDestination` answers `DISABLING`, yet `DescribeKinesisStreamingDestination` went on reporting the destination `ACTIVE` for about a second (measured us-east-1), and the deploy captures its `observedProperties` baseline right after the update. So the baseline kept a destination AWS no longer had, and every later `cdkd drift` reported the replica's `Replicas` entry. The update now re-reads the destination after a disable until it stops reading as active (best-effort, about 60 s at most) before it returns.
