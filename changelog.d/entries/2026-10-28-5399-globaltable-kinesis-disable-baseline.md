- **A deploy that turns off a GlobalTable replica's Kinesis streaming no longer records the destination in the drift baseline (found by the issue [#5399](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5399) integ)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts` (`waitForKinesisDisableSettled`, `kinesisDisableSettleDelaysMs`) and unit cases. `DisableKinesisStreamingDestination` answers `DISABLING`, yet the first `DescribeKinesisStreamingDestination` after it read `ACTIVE` in one measured run and `DISABLING` in another, then `DISABLED` about a second later (us-east-1), and the deploy captures its `observedProperties` baseline right after the update. So the baseline kept a destination AWS no longer had, and every later `cdkd drift` reported the replica's `Replicas` entry. The update now re-reads the destination after a disable until it reads `DISABLED` or is gone (`DISABLING` still settles), within the resource deadline and about 60 s at most; a destination still unsettled after that draws a warning that `cdkd drift` may report it until it settles.
