- **`cdkd drift` no longer reads a propagated or local route as a deleted `AWS::EC2::Route` (issue [#6854](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6854))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ec2/read-state.ts`. The readback found the route by destination alone, so after the route was deleted out of band a virtual private gateway's propagated route (or the table's `local` route) at the same destination read as it. Only a route `CreateRoute` made is matched now; with none, the route reads as gone.
