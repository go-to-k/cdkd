- **A pasteable `aws ...` command is withheld when its identifier would change the command once unquoted (issue [#3950](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3950), partial)** -- `src/provisioning/replacement-protection-advice.ts` (`pasteableArg`, shared by `pasteableAwsCommand` and `renderDisableCommand`, and its new `PASTE_ARG_UNSAFE` set) and `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-globaltable-provider.ts`, with paste cases in `replacement-remedy-preconditions.test.ts`, `logs-loggroup-provider-class-guard.test.ts` and `pasteable-aws-command.test.ts` under `tests/unit/provisioning/`. These commands are printed inside markdown backticks (`protectedReplacementAdvice`, the `LogGroupClass` refusal) and in sentences carrying `cdkd's`. A template- or state-borne id holding a backtick ended the substitution when the clause was pasted with its backticks, and an apostrophe earlier in the line flipped the quote parity, so the shell-quoted id ran. **User-visible change:** a hint that used to show such an identifier shell-quoted now withholds its command and falls back to its console wording. The characters, measured under bash and zsh with their default options: whitespace, a quote, a backtick, `$`, `;`, `&`, `|`, `<`, `>`, `(`, `)`, `\`, `*`, `?`, `[`, `]`, `{`, `}` or `!` anywhere; `#` or `=` starting the name; `~` starting it or right after `=` or `:`. A mid-word `#`, `=`, `,`, `%` or `^` stays admitted, so a log group such as `/app#blue` or an IAM name with `=` or `,` keeps its command. Under zsh's `EXTENDED_GLOB` a mid-word `#`, `^` or `~` becomes a glob operator, but cdkd always shell-quotes those characters, and a `cdkd's` quote flip leaves the word glued to prose, so the worst case is a no-match abort, not a run. The DynamoDB replica-drop command keeps cdkd's own `Delete={RegionName=...}` text in the command, single-quoted around the region, so it is still printed for every table and zsh's `BRACE_CCL` cannot expand it.
