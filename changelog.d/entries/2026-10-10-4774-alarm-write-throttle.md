- **`cdkd deploy` and `cdkd destroy` of a stack with hundreds of CloudWatch alarms no longer fail on `Rate exceeded` (issues [#4774](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4774), [#4781](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4781))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/cloudwatch-alarm-provider.ts` (`create()`, `update()`, `delete()` through one `sendWithThrottleRetry`), a new unit file, and the `throttle-wide-dag` integ fixture (now with 300 alarms). The provider sends one `PutMetricAlarm` or `DeleteAlarms` per alarm, and a deploy or destroy runs them in parallel, so a large stack met CloudWatch's request quota. The engine's own per-resource retry ran out: a deploy failed and rolled back on an alarm create, and a destroy ended partially destroyed with alarms left behind. A throttled alarm write is now retried inside the provider with jittered backoff (8 retries, about 45s to 91s in total) before the engine's retry sees it. Any other failure is not retried there, and on delete `ResourceNotFound` is still treated as already deleted.
