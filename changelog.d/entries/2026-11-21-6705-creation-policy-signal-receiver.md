- **`CreationPolicy` signals are waited on and `cfn-init` finds its metadata, through a transient CloudFormation signal receiver stack (issue [#6705](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6705))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/signal-receiver.ts` (new), `src/deployment/deploy-engine/provision.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-policy.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-in-place.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update/finish.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/wait-condition-provider.ts`. `cfn-signal --resource` and `cfn-init` call the CloudFormation API, which a cdkd deploy had no stack to answer, so a resource declaring a `CreationPolicy` was created without waiting (with a warning) and CDK's `applyCloudFormationInit` could not read its config. A deploy that creates or replaces such a resource now creates a CloudFormation stack under the cdkd stack's name holding a wait condition per resource (its signal count and resolved `Metadata`), waits for each resource's signals (a `FAILURE` or the `Timeout` fails the deploy), and deletes the stack at the end. A deploy without such a resource makes no CloudFormation call; a same-name CloudFormation stack cdkd did not make, a denied `CreateStack`, a nested stack, or metadata not resolvable yet keep the previous warn-and-proceed behavior. A wait condition without a `Handle` is no longer refused.
