- **`CreationPolicy` signals are waited on and `cfn-init` finds its metadata, through a CloudFormation signal receiver stack (issue [#6705](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6705))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/signal-receiver.ts` (new), `src/deployment/deploy-engine/signal-receiver-run.ts` (new), `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/execute/provision-node.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/functions/pseudo-parameter.ts`, `src/types/state/stack-state.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner/state-and-receiver.ts` (new), `src/cli/commands/export/`, `src/cli/commands/state/orphan.ts`. A stack whose resources declare a `CreationPolicy` `ResourceSignal` or `AWS::CloudFormation::Init` metadata, read by signed `cfn-init` / `cfn-signal` calls (`--role`; AWS CDK `initOptions.includeRole: true`), gets a CloudFormation stack with its exact name for its lifetime, recorded in state (`signalReceiverStackId`, no schema bump). `AWS::StackId` resolves to its ARN, so CDK's grant matches; each created or replaced signalling resource completes only on its signals, and a `FAILURE` or the `Timeout` fails the deploy and rolls it back. `cdkd destroy` and `cdkd export` delete the receiver; `cdkd state orphan` keeps it and prints the delete command. Unsigned calls (the AWS CDK default) are answered by CloudFormation only for its own stacks' instances, so they keep the warning, now naming `includeRole: true`. Design: [docs/design/6705-cloudformation-signal-receiver.md](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/blob/main/docs/design/6705-cloudformation-signal-receiver.md).
