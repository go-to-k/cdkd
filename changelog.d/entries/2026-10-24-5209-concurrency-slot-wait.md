- **`CDKD_PHASE_TIMINGS=1` reports how long resources waited for a `--concurrency` slot (issue [#5209](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5209))** -- `src/deployment/dag-executor.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/execute.ts`, `docs/troubleshooting-slow-deploys.md`, the new `tests/integration/wide-independent` fixture, and unit tests in `tests/unit/deployment/dag-executor.test.ts`. Each stack's report gains a `ready, waiting for a --concurrency slot` line: how long each resource sat with every dependency done before a slot freed, added up, with the longest wait. A stack whose resources depend on each other rarely waits (17 resources: 8 ms in all); a stack of 48 independent resources waited 75 s in all at the default of 10, and its resource DAG took 3.78 s, against 2.09 s at `--concurrency 30`. Unset, nothing is recorded and nothing changes.
