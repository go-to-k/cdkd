- **A recorded secret whose own leading or trailing space sits at the edge of an export name or state key is now detected and masked (issue [#2890](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2890))** -- `src/deployment/outputs-export-alias.ts`, plus tests. The secret scan used to read only the key with its edge whitespace trimmed, so a secret such as `' abcd'` at the start of `' abcd-x'` matched nothing: the name printed as `abcd-x`, the secret minus its space, in deploy warnings and `cdkd scrub` output, and the deploy published it as an export alias. The scan now reads the key both trimmed and untrimmed. Masking runs over the untrimmed key and the printed text is its trim, so the name prints as `***-x`, and the deploy refuses the alias. A sub-floor secret that is the whole key, spaces included, is now masked where it was withheld. Names that hold no such secret print exactly as before. Only `\p{Zs}` edges were affected: a tab or newline at a secret's edge was already deleted rather than trimmed.
