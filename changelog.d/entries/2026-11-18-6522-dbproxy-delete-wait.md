- **Ctrl-C now stops an `AWS::RDS::DBProxy` or `AWS::RDS::DBProxyEndpoint` delete that is waiting for the proxy or endpoint to disappear (issue [#6522](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6522))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/rds-dbproxy/{shared,delete}.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/rds-dbproxy-endpoint-provider.ts`. After AWS accepted `DeleteDBProxy` / `DeleteDBProxyEndpoint`, the wait ignored Ctrl-C and the per-resource deadline (`--resource-timeout`), and could keep calling `DescribeDBProxies` / `DescribeDBProxyEndpoints` for up to 30 minutes. Both waits now use the wait every other accepted delete uses (Kinesis, Firehose, DynamoDB tables). A Ctrl-C, the end of the wait's budget (30 seconds before the resource deadline) or a status read that fails for a reason other than throttling now logs a warning and finishes the delete, because AWS completes an accepted delete on its own. Before, the timeout and the failed read failed the resource. The warning masks a secret-derived name, as the timeout error did not. A proxy or endpoint that leaves `deleting` for another status, or that the wait stops on without ever reading `deleting`, fails the delete and keeps its state record. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/rds-dbproxy-delete-wait-6522.test.ts`.
