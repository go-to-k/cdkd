- **`AWS::ServiceDiscovery::Service`: a create right after its namespace no longer fails on Cloud Map's `NamespaceNotFound` lag (issue [#5113](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5113))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/servicediscovery-provider.ts`. Cloud Map can answer `CreateService` with `NamespaceNotFound` just after the namespace's create operation reported SUCCESS (seen live: one of four parallel creates against a fresh namespace), and cdkd sent the call once, so the deploy failed and rolled back. cdkd now retries that error only, for about 30 seconds (27.5s of backoff); every other error still fails at once, and a create that gives up says it retried. The rejection creates no service, so a retry cannot duplicate one. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/servicediscovery-provider-create-namespace-retry-5113.test.ts`; the `servicediscovery` integ fixture exercises the path.
