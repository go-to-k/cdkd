- **An `Export.Name` holding a secret split by a nonspacing mark, or spelled in the other Unicode normalization form, is refused instead of published (issue [#2889](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2889))** -- `src/deployment/outputs-export-alias.ts`, `src/cli/commands/scrub.ts` and `src/state/export-index-store.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/deployment/secret-detection-space-2889.test.ts`. A combining mark (`\p{Mn}`, e.g. `U+09BC`) renders at zero width, so a recorded secret with one inserted still reads as the secret, and a precomposed letter renders the same as its decomposed spelling. The containment test missed both, and the name was written into `state.json` and the exports index. The test now also runs with the text decomposed, nonspacing marks removed and recomposed, on top of the existing checks, so it catches strictly more than before. The printed text still keeps its marks, so non-Latin names print unchanged. When the only match is in that wider space, the name is withheld from warnings, in the deploy and in `cdkd scrub` alike. `cdkd scrub`'s exports-index lines and the deploy's "published by both" exports-index warning now withhold any non-ASCII export name, since the terminal sanitiser blanked such characters after the check, or with no check at all in the exports-index warning. Cost: a name that differs from a recorded secret only by marks is refused, in either direction (`cafe` with or without an accent), when the unmarked form is at least four characters or the whole name.
