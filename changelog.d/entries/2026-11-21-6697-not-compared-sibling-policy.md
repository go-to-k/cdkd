- **`cdkd drift` reports an out-of-band attach of a same-stack IAM managed policy on the role, user or group it was attached to when that policy itself was not compared in the run, and `--revert` detaches it there (issue [#6697](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6697))** -- new `src/cli/commands/drift/not-compared-policies.ts`, `src/cli/commands/drift/detect.ts`, `compare.ts`, `revert-outcome.ts`, `src/provisioning/iam-sibling-managed-policies.ts`, `sibling-owned-members.ts`, a drift / revert arm in the `iam-managed-policy` integ fixture, and `docs/cli-drift.md`. Since go-to-k/cdkd#6627 an attachment of a same-stack `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` whose template sets that principal kind's list (`Roles` / `Users` / `Groups`) is the policy's drift only. When the policy was not compared (its read failed or was aborted, its baseline was refused, or its record was unreadable), the attachment was reported on neither resource and `--revert` left it. Detection now compares the stack's managed policies first, and a principal keeps such a policy's attachment as its own drift for that run; the report keeps the template's order. An attachment the policy's own list names is still neither resource's drift. Also drops the IAM role / user / group rows `UNDECLARED_UNCOMPARED_KEYS` no longer applied (issue [#6647](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6647)).
