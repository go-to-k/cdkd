- **Removing `Monitoring` from an `AWS::ECS::Service` now resets the service's metric resolution, as CloudFormation does (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ecs-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/ecs-service-removal-1160d.test.ts`, `tests/integration/ecs-service-update-props/`. Before this change, cdkd sent nothing to `UpdateService` when a template dropped `Monitoring`, so a service kept its old metric configuration (for example a 20-second resolution) indefinitely. A CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation resets the service revision to `{metricConfigurations: []}`, which cdkd now sends. The same A/B confirmed the existing behavior of three siblings: a removed `VolumeConfigurations` or `VpcLatticeConfigurations` is cleared, and a removed `AvailabilityZoneRebalancing` keeps its live value.
