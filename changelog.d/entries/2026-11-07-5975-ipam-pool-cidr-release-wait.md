- **`cdkd destroy` no longer stops at an IPAM pool CIDR when AWS releases a deleted VPC's or subnet's pool allocation late (issue [#5975](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5975))** -- `src/provisioning/cloud-control/delete.ts`, `src/provisioning/ipam-pool-cidr-release.ts`. An `AWS::EC2::IPAMPoolCidr` delete refused with `The CIDR has one or more allocations.` now waits, for up to 25 minutes, while the pool still lists allocations of VPCs or subnets of your account that are already deleted, then deletes the pool CIDR again. Before, a release slower than the VPC's own 25-minute wait failed the pool CIDR and left the pool and the IPAM undeleted until a second `cdkd destroy`. An allocation held by a live resource, another account or another region still fails the delete at once.
