- **An Auto Scaling group's group metrics and SNS notifications now apply on its first deploy (issue [#3995](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3995))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/asg-provider.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/provisioning/asg-provider-create-metrics-notifications-3995.test.ts` and a first-deploy assertion in the `launchtemplate-asg-inplace` integration fixture. `MetricsCollection` and `NotificationConfigurations` are not `CreateAutoScalingGroup` members, and cdkd sent their dedicated APIs only from the update path, so a CDK group with `groupMetrics` or `notifications` was created without either, silently, until some later change triggered an update. `create()` now sends one `EnableMetricsCollection` per `MetricsCollection` entry and one `PutNotificationConfiguration` per `NotificationConfigurations` entry once the group exists. If one of them fails, cdkd deletes the group it just created, as CloudFormation rolls back a CREATE_FAILED group: it scales the group to zero (clearing a template-set `DeletionProtection`), lifts EC2 termination protection from the instances it launched, force-deletes it and waits, then reports the original error. If that delete cannot finish, the error also names the surviving group and the command to delete it by hand.
