- **`cdkd deploy`: an in-place update no longer erases the attributes create recorded, and `Fn::GetAtt` of a `VPCGatewayAttachment`'s `AttachmentType` or a Runtime's `LastUpdatedAt` resolves (issue [#7009](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7009))** -- An in-place update's returned attribute map replaces the record, and several providers returned a partial or empty one. After a tag or description change, a same-deploy `Fn::GetAtt` read of the dropped attribute hit the physical-id fallback or a refusal. The affected attributes were the `Arn` / `Name` of an `AWS::Events::EventBus`, the `Arn` of an unchanged `AWS::Events::Archive`, the `DefaultSecurityGroup` / `DefaultNetworkAcl` of an `AWS::EC2::VPC`, every attribute of the default `AWS::BedrockAgentCore::Browser` / `CodeInterpreter`, and, when their read-back failed, the `Arn` of an `AWS::DLM::LifecyclePolicy` or `AWS::KinesisFirehose::DeliveryStream`, the `AlarmArn` / `State` of an `AWS::Lightsail::Alarm` and every attribute of an `AWS::Kinesis::StreamConsumer`. An EventBus import now records `Arn` / `Name` too. Each update now keeps the recorded set. `AWS::EC2::VPCGatewayAttachment` records `AttachmentType` (`IGW` / `VGW`, as CloudFormation answers) on create, update and import. A record written before this fix is re-read on demand, and when that re-read fails the read is refused rather than served the composite physical id. `AWS::BedrockAgentCore::Runtime` records `LastUpdatedAt` from the READY read. A VPC update no longer re-reads an IPAM-allocated CIDR.
