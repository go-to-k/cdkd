- **A create replay finishes the IAM role its own throttled attempt left behind (go-to-k/cdkd#5656)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/iam-role-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/created-resource-sink.ts`. When IAM throttled for longer than the SDK retries cover, a failed `AttachRolePolicy` / `PutRolePolicy` was followed by a failed cleanup `DeleteRole`, and the engine's replay then failed `EntityAlreadyExists` against the role the first attempt made. The replay now reads the role and, when its `RoleId` is the one that attempt's `CreateRole` returned, re-runs the wiring on it. A same-named role with another `RoleId` is left untouched and the create fails as before, as does a later deploy, which holds no such proof. Tested in `iam-role-provider-replay-adopt-5656.test.ts`.
