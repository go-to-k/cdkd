- **Changing an `AWS::EC2::NetworkAclEntry`'s rule (for example `RuleAction` from `allow` to `deny`, or its `CidrBlock`, `Protocol`, `PortRange` or `Icmp`) now updates the entry in place with one `ReplaceNetworkAclEntry` call, as CloudFormation does, instead of failing the deploy as not updatable (issue [#5070](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5070))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ec2-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/ec2-nacl-entry-replace-5070.test.ts`, and the `ec2-vpc` integ's Phase 2. The refusal's suggested `cdkd deploy --replace` deleted the entry and created it again, leaving the rule number absent between the two calls so traffic fell through to the next rule. A rollback of the change, and `cdkd drift --revert` of a console-side rule edit, now replace the rule back in place too. A change to `NetworkAclId`, `RuleNumber` or `Egress` is createOnly and still replaces the entry.
