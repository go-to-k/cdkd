- **A successful fix-forward deploy now deletes the Kinesis stream an earlier failed CREATE left under the same logical id, once a live read proves it is not the new stream (issue [#4606](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4606))** -- `src/types/resource.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/{journaled-orphans,plan}.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/kinesis-provider.ts`. After `cdkd deploy --no-rollback` failed with a stream already created (a journaled proven orphan) and the fixed template created a new stream under the same logical id, the successful deploy could not prove the two differ, so it warned, named the earlier stream, exited `2` and left it in AWS. Providers gain an optional `isSameResource`, answering `same`, `different` or `unknown` from a live read; the success-path settle asks it when a record of the orphan's type sits under its logical id, deletes the orphan (per its journaled `DeletionPolicy`, still subject to the other-stack ownership check) only on `different`, without reading or dropping that record, and settles `same` silently as a tracked resource. `AWS::Kinesis::Stream` implements it: both ids must be stream names, the client must be in the stack's region, the record's stream must read back, and the earlier stream is `different` when it reads back under another `StreamARN` or is gone. Equal names are never compared and never deleted. Every other type, an unreadable record, and a read that fails keep the previous warning and exit `2`. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/journaled-orphans-fix-forward-4606.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/kinesis-same-resource-4606.test.ts`; integ `rollback-command` step O8 now asserts the earlier stream deleted, the new one kept, exit `0`.
