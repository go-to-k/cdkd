- **A rollback that removes an inline policy name another record still holds on that role, group or user now puts that record's document back (issue [#4408](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4408))** -- `src/deployment/inline-policy-claims.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/replay-inline-policy-restore.ts`. When a deploy dropped an `AWS::IAM::Policy` and created a new one under the same name on the same principal, then failed before its DELETE phase, the rollback deleted the new policy and the name went with it: the old policy's record still listed it, the principal no longer held it, and the next deploy showed no diff. The same happened when a swap's reversal deleted a copy whose name a sibling still recorded (a partial swap rolled back without `--revert-failed`), and when a re-adopted retained policy's name had been deleted by a sibling's reversal. Every removal the rollback lets proceed is now noted, and at the end of each completed-op replay, a removed name that another record holds on that principal is put back with that record's recorded document (`Put{Role,Group,User}Policy`), logged as `Rollback: put back the inline policy <logicalId> records on its <role|group|user>`. Nothing is put back, and the rollback warns, when the records holding it disagree on the document, or the recorded document is absent or redacted; a failed put warns the same way. In each case the principal lacks the policy until the resource is next updated or `cdkd drift <stack> --revert`. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/rollback-executor-inline-policy-claims.test.ts`; integ arm Phase 2c-rollback in `tests/integration/iam-role-policies-drift-clean/` now includes the delete-beside-create pair.
