- **A later `cdkd deploy` or `cdkd destroy` no longer deletes a replaced S3 bucket's old name when another bucket now holds it (issue [#6668](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6668), part of [#6655](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6655))**. When a replaced bucket's delete outlives its deploy (the deploy was killed, the delete failed, or the holder could not be read), state keeps a superseded entry. The next deploy or `cdkd destroy` deleted that entry by name, without the check the deploy's own cleanup makes ([#6671](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/pull/6671)). The entry now records the old bucket's generation (`identity`, optional; no state schema bump, because an older cdkd ignores it and deletes by name as before). The carried delete compares the holder with it through the same helper as the cleanup. A bucket of another account or region, or one created at another time in us-east-1, is left alone and the entry dropped. A free name drops the entry without a delete. An unreadable holder sends nothing and keeps the entry; `cdkd destroy` then reports it and keeps the state. An entry without `identity` is deleted by name as before, with no extra call.
