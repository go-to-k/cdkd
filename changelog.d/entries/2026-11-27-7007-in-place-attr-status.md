- **A reader of a Cloud Control resource's status or state follows an in-place update that moves it (issue [#7007](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7007))** -- `src/analyzer/diff-calculator/in-place-cc-derived-attrs.generated.ts`, `scripts/gen-in-place-attr-coverage.ts`, `scripts/in-place-attr-classification.ts`. An `AWS::EC2::IPAMPool` `Description`-only update moves its `State` from `create-complete` to `modify-complete`, and a resource reading it through `Fn::GetAtt` kept the old value; a status, state, or the message, reason or error explaining it is now re-resolved after any in-place update of a Cloud Control type that has an in-place property other than `Tags`. The in-place attribute audit now reads create-only properties from the live-registry snapshot instead of the pinned `@aws-cdk/aws-service-spec`, and its unclassified baseline shrinks from 1980 to 1411 pairs.
