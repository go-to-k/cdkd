- **Installing cdkd no longer pulls in `aws-cdk-lib`, and every command starts faster (issue [#4521](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4521))** -- `package.json`. cdk-local moves to `^0.149.8`, which makes its `aws-cdk-lib` / `constructs` peers optional and loads `@aws-cdk/toolkit-lib` on first synth instead of at module load. A clean install of the packed cdkd shrinks from 453 MB to 254 MB of `node_modules`, and `cdkd --help` takes 510 ms of CPU instead of 659 ms (Node 24, median of 9, compile cache off). A CDK app still resolves `aws-cdk-lib` from its own project, as before.
