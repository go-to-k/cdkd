- **`cdkd drift --revert` no longer puts an `AWS::IAM::Policy` or `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` on, or removes it from, a same-named role, group or user someone else made (issue [#6734](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6734), part of [#6655](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6655))**. The revert sent its update with no record of the stack's other resources, so the principal identity checks of [#6703](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/pull/6703) and [#6722](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6722) saw every principal as outside the stack and acted by name: reverting a dropped `Roles` entry granted the policy to whoever held the role's name. A revert of such a policy now reads each in-stack principal with a recorded `RoleId` / `GroupId` / `UserId` (`GetRole` / `GetGroup` / `GetUser`, once per principal per revert run) and, when another id holds the name, refuses to put or attach the policy there (counted failed, nothing put or attached anywhere), or sends no removal or detach there and warns (a managed policy attached out of band to such a principal). A principal outside the stack, or whose record holds no usable id, is handled by name as before; nothing else a revert does changes.
