- **A Neptune DBCluster or DBInstance property removed from the template is named in a warning when cdkd leaves its live value in place (issue [#5544](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5544))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/neptune-provider.ts`. A real CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation keeps the live value of a removed DBCluster `EngineVersion`, `PreferredBackupWindow`, `PreferredMaintenanceWindow` or `DBClusterParameterGroupName`, and of a removed DBInstance `DBParameterGroupName` or `PreferredMaintenanceWindow`: the engine version is not upgraded and neither parameter group moves back to `default.neptune1.4`. cdkd already left them in place, but said nothing. Now one warning per resource names each such removed property, together with the ones CloudFormation was earlier measured to keep (`DeletionProtection`, `IamAuthEnabled`, `BackupRetentionPeriod`, `DBPort`, `AutoMinorVersionUpgrade`).
