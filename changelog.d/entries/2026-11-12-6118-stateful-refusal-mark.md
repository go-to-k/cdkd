- **A stateful-replacement refusal no longer advises `--revert-failed` (issue [#6118](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6118))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/update-replace/preflight.ts`. Before, a deploy refused because its update needed a stateful replacement (`STATEFUL_REPLACE_BLOCKED`, or `RECREATE_TARGETS_INVALID` for a `--recreate-via-cc-api` target) was journaled as a failed update and printed `Revert it with: cdkd rollback <stack> --revert-failed`. Nothing had been applied, so that revert only pushed a no-op to AWS. Both refusals now count as raised before anything was applied: the journal keeps no failed-operation record for them and the deploy prints no `--revert-failed` line.
