- **`cdkd diff --replace-resource <logicalId>` previews the replacement `cdkd deploy --replace-resource` plans (issue [#7089](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7089))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/compute-stack-diff.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/replace-resource-preview.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive/build-tree.ts`, `src/cli/options/provisioning-route.ts`, `src/cli/commands/deploy/dependency-closure.ts`, `src/deployment/replace-resource-targets.ts`. Before, `cdkd diff` had no such option, so its preview of that deploy showed the target as an in-place update and its readers as unchanged. The diff now seeds its forced replacements with the targets and applies the deploy's own `planReplaceResourceTargets`, so `cdkd diff` and `cdkd deploy --dry-run` show the same plan. The deploy's run-level checks refuse an invalid id in the diff too, over the stacks that deploy runs (the selection plus its dependency stacks, through the deploy's own walk, now in `src/cli/commands/deploy/dependency-closure.ts`). A target the plan refuses is a blocking reason (exit 3), and the diff then shows no replacement, its readers' included, since the deploy shows no plan. The flag reaches a top-level stack's diff only, never a nested child's. `cdkd diff` takes no `--force-stateful-recreation`, so the deploy's stateful refusals are shown as warnings.
