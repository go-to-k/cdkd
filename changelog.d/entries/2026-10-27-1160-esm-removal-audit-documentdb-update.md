- **`AWS::Lambda::EventSourceMapping`: an update of a DocumentDB-sourced mapping no longer fails, and every removal cdkd cannot reset is named (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-eventsource-provider.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/provisioning/lambda-eventsource-provider-removal-audit-1160.test.ts` and the new `lambda-esm-documentdb` integ fixture. cdkd sent the whole declared `DocumentDBEventSourceConfig` on every update, and `UpdateEventSourceMapping` refuses its `DatabaseName` and `CollectionName` even unchanged, so any change to such a mapping (a `BatchSize`, say) failed the deploy. A CloudFormation A/B showed CloudFormation sends `{ FullDocument }` alone, leaves a changed `CollectionName` as it was, and keeps a removed `FullDocument` or block; cdkd now does the same, warns about each kept value, and records the kept `DatabaseName` / `CollectionName` so the next diff reports them again. The type is now audited into `removalHandledInUpdate`: a removal cdkd leaves in place on a source kind where CloudFormation's answer was not measured (a Kafka `MaximumRetryAttempts`, an MQ or DocumentDB batching window, any reset on a source cdkd cannot classify) is named in one warning instead of passing silently.
