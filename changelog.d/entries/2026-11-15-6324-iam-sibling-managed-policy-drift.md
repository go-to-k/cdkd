- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports a managed policy that a sibling `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` attaches to an IAM role, user or group as that principal's drift, and `cdkd drift --revert` no longer detaches it (issue [#6324](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6324))** -- `src/provisioning/iam-sibling-managed-policies.ts` (new), `src/provisioning/providers/iam-role/read-state.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-user-group/read-state.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/observed-capture.ts`, `src/cli/commands/drift/{compare,detect,revert-outcome}.ts`, plus a new `iam-sibling-managed-policy-drift` integ. Before, `new iam.ManagedPolicy(s, 'Mp', { roles: [role] })` made every fresh stack report `ManagedPolicyArns` drift on the role, and `cdkd drift --revert` detached the policy while printing success. A policy's ARN that a sibling `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` attaches through `Roles` / `Users` / `Groups` is now left out of the principal's `ManagedPolicyArns` on both sides of the comparison, and out of the bag `--revert` sends, unless the principal's own template declares it too. A baseline recorded before the fix that already holds the ARN compares clean with no redeploy. An attachment made outside the stack is still reported, and `--revert` detaches only that one. The deploy-time baseline also leaves the sibling's ARN out once the policy exists in state.
