Read back now: `Tags` on an EC2 VPC, Subnet, InternetGateway, NatGateway, RouteTable and NetworkAcl and on an IAM user; a zonal NAT gateway's declared secondary addresses; an EC2 instance's declared `SecurityGroups` and `UserData`; an IAM user's declared `LoginProfile.PasswordResetRequired` (one-directional: a required reset IAM cleared at sign-in is not drift); a load balancer's declared `SubnetMappings`; a cache cluster's `LogDeliveryConfigurations` and declared `PreferredAvailabilityZones`; a Lambda permission's `EventSourceToken` and `FunctionUrlAuthType`. A cache cluster's `AZMode`, which no describe call returns, and a `UserData` recorded as `***` are declared not compared. A baseline written before this change lacks the new keys and is not compared on them until the resource is next updated or `cdkd state refresh-observed` re-reads it.
