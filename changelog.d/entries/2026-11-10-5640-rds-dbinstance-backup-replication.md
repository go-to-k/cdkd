- **An `AWS::RDS::DBInstance` declaring `AutomaticBackupReplicationRegion`, `AutomaticBackupReplicationKmsKeyId` or `AutomaticBackupReplicationRetentionPeriod` stays on the SDK provider (issue [#5640](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5640))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/rds-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/rds/instance-members.ts`, `calls.ts`, `instance-create.ts`, `instance-update.ts`, `read-state.ts` and `drift.ts` beside it, the regenerated property-coverage map, `tests/fixtures/cfn-schemas/_todo-backfill.json`, a new unit file, the RDS wire cases, and a new `rds-dbinstance-backup-replication` integ. Before, any of them routed the instance through Cloud Control. As CloudFormation's handler does, once the instance is `available` (under `--no-wait` too) cdkd calls `StartDBInstanceAutomatedBackupsReplication` in the destination region with the instance's ARN, the retention and the KMS key; an update removing the region stops the replication there, and one changing the region, the key, the retention, or `BackupRetentionPeriod` while no replication retention is declared, stops it and starts it again. A KMS key or retention declared without a region, or a region beside `BackupRetentionPeriod: 0`, is refused before any call, as CloudFormation refuses it. `cdkd drift` compares the destination region, and the retention where the template declares it; the KMS key is write-only. A delete makes no replication call, as CloudFormation's delete handler makes none.
