- **`cdkd local *` with a cross-account `--role-arn` now resolves `${AWS::AccountId}` to the deploy account, where the stack lives (issue [#5001](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5001))** -- `src/local/local-role-arn.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-invoke.ts`, `local-start-api.ts`, `local-run-task.ts`, `local-invoke-agentcore.ts`, `cdk-local` bumped to 0.153.0. `${AWS::AccountId}` (and the account filled into a bare `--assume-task-role` ARN) used to be the caller's account under `--from-cfn-stack`, so a pinned ECR image URI and account-derived ARNs pointed at an account the stack is not in. It is now asked as the `--role-arn` role on all `local` commands, for `--from-state` and `--from-cfn-stack` alike; `applyRoleArnForLocal` hands cdk-local the role's credentials with the assume so the engine commands' lookups follow. Container credentials, assumed roles, secrets and `--from-cfn-stack` reads still resolve as you. Same-account use and runs without `--role-arn` are unchanged.
