- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports a bucket's event notifications as drift after a fresh deploy, and `cdkd drift --revert` no longer deletes them (issue [#5736](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5736))** -- `src/cli/commands/drift.ts` and its unit tests. CDK's `bucket.addEventNotification(...)` writes the bucket's `NotificationConfiguration` through a `Custom::S3BucketNotifications` resource that runs after the bucket is created, so the bucket's recorded snapshot did not hold the notifications and drift reported them on every fresh deploy. `--revert` then wrote the empty snapshot back, and S3 stopped delivering events. Drift no longer compares a bucket's `NotificationConfiguration` that the template does not set on the bucket (widened to every writer in [#5741](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5741)), which matches CloudFormation's drift detection. Stacks that are already deployed are fixed without a redeploy.
