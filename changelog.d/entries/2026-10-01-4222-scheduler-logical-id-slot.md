- **The EventBridge Scheduler schedule provider's `getAttribute` errors name the logical id in their `logicalId` field (issue [#4222](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4222))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/scheduler-schedule-provider.ts`. The last two `ProvisioningError`s from #4222 (an unknown attribute, and a failed `GetSchedule` lookup of `Arn` for `cdkd orphan`) carried the schedule's name, which can be built from a secret, in their `logicalId` field. They now carry the logical id (the fourth `getAttribute` argument, #4228), with the schedule name in `physicalId`. The source fence in `tests/unit/provisioning/logical-id-slot-4222.test.ts` now admits no exception. `maskSecretsInError` still masks `logicalId`. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/scheduler-schedule-provider.test.ts`.
