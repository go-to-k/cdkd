- **Converging an unsettled `AWS::DynamoDB::Table` `GlobalSecondaryIndexes` no longer deletes an index that neither the record nor the template names, and an unsettled `AWS::Kinesis::Stream` `DesiredShardLevelMetrics` no longer disables such a metric (issue [#5503](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5503))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/kinesis-provider.ts`, `src/deployment/unsettled-convergence.ts` and its three callers, `src/types/resource.ts`, `tests/integration/dynamodb-gsi-update/`. The convergence step (#5487) made the whole live list the previous side, so the update deleted an index (or disabled a metric) that another tool had added. CloudFormation never does: it diffs the models it recorded. A Kinesis CFn A/B showed an out-of-band metric left enabled. `readUnsettled` now receives the template's bag and projects the live list onto the names in the record or the template. A recorded entry the template dropped is still removed. Each live entry left this way is named in a warning, with the command that removes it. It may be one an earlier failed cdkd update created, which the record cannot tell from another tool's; exact recovery of what cdkd attempted lands with #5498.
