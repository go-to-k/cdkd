- **`AWS::Logs::LogGroup` reads `RetentionInDays` the way CloudFormation does, and refuses the spellings CloudFormation rejects instead of forwarding their `Number()` reading (issue [#2698](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2698))** -- `src/provisioning/dynamodb-warm-throughput.ts` (new `toCfnInteger`, carrying the live A/B table), `src/provisioning/providers/logs-loggroup-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/dynamodb-warm-throughput.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/logs-loggroup-provider-retention-coercion.test.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`. Measured on a live stack: CloudFormation's Integer grammar is an optional sign plus decimal digits, surrounding whitespace trimmed; `'0x1e'`, `'1e3'`, `'30.5'` and `'30.0'` are rejected by the Logs handler. cdkd forwarded `'0x1e'` as 30 and `'30.5'` as 30.5 on templates CloudFormation refuses; it now refuses them by name before any AWS call, while `'+30'` and `' 30 '` deploy as 30 and compare equal to a recorded 30. `toCfnInteger` reuses `config-shape.ts`'s `coerceCfnInteger` digits rule plus a trim (each helper's padding rule is its own measurement). The DynamoDB capacity forwarders stay on `toFiniteNumber` until their own A/B, [#3135](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3135). **Behavior change:** a template spelling the retention in hex / exponent / decimal-point notation now fails at cdkd instead of deploying.
