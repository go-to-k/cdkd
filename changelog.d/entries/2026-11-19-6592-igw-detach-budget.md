- **An internet gateway that cannot be detached or deleted fails after its 10-minute budget, not 30 minutes (issue [#6592](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6592))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ec2/tags-errors.ts`, unit tests. While a VPC still has a mapped public address (an instance that did not terminate), cdkd retries `DetachInternetGateway`, `DeleteInternetGateway` and an Elastic IP's `ReleaseAddress` on `DependencyViolation` for up to 10 minutes. Once that budget ran out, the destroy runner's 3-attempt loop called the provider again, each call starting a fresh 10-minute budget, so `cdkd destroy` took the whole 30-minute per-resource timeout to report the failure. The error rethrown after the budget is now marked non-retryable, so the destroy loop, the deploy engine's retry and a superseded delete's in-use retry stop at it; the failure surfaces about 20 minutes sooner, naming AWS's `DependencyViolation` instead of a per-resource timeout, and the resource and its state record are left as before.
