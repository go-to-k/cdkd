- **A rollback-orphan record no reader can use is now refused where a command can write, and dropped where it cannot (issue [#3500](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3500), PR #3641)** -- `malformed-resources-bag.ts`, `deploy-engine.ts`, `destroy-runner.ts`, `rollback.ts`, `import.ts`, `scrub.ts`, `diff-recursive.ts`. go-to-k/cdkd#3379 validated the CONTAINER; its records were still taken off the cast. `isReadableOrphanRecord` is the one predicate for a usable row -- a WHOLE-record question, including a NON-EMPTY `state.physicalId`, the only handle on a resource cdkd left LIVE and the one field a `resources` entry is not asked for. **Behavior change:** `cdkd deploy`, `cdkd destroy` (both reads), `cdkd rollback`, `cdkd import` and a real `cdkd scrub` refuse such a record, naming the rows; `cdkd scrub --dry-run` DROPS it, warns, and reports it in the audited-record refusal (exit `2`). `cdkd diff` drops by `isPreviewableOrphanRecord`, the NARROW half: a non-string `logicalId` or an empty or non-string `state.physicalId` goes, a torn `properties` / `attributes` map STAYS previewed -- and the preview now WARNS naming the row, at every node it reaches with an adoption preview, that `cdkd deploy` refuses the record over it; the TOP-LEVEL stack also exits `3`, so a clean `--fail` run never precedes a deploy that cannot start. The quietest shape read as `undefined` field by field, so `cdkd destroy` printed a row with a field missing and deleted the resources and record once approved. Two more fixes ride along, both reachable with every row usable: `cdkd scrub` no longer loses one row's secret needles to another row sharing its `logicalId`, which could save a plaintext; and `cdkd destroy`'s pre-confirmation banner is sanitized -- the orphan listing AND the resources list above it -- so a stored id or type cannot forge a row, fake the tally, or redraw the lines the y/N answers. An absent `orphans` field stays absent. Not in scope: a readable `properties` map's CONTENTS.
