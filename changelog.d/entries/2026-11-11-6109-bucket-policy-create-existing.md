- **An `AWS::S3::BucketPolicy` create no longer overwrites a policy the bucket already holds (issue [#6109](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6109))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/s3-bucket-policy-provider.ts`, plus a new `s3-bucket-policy-existing` integ. Before, the create sent `PutBucketPolicy` without looking, and `PutBucketPolicy` replaces the bucket's whole policy, so a policy another owner had attached was silently dropped. In the wild that is the grant a log-delivery service attaches to an `aws-waf-logs-*` or VPC Flow Logs destination bucket, and log delivery then stopped with no error. Like CloudFormation, cdkd now reads the bucket's policy first and refuses the create with `The bucket policy already exists on bucket <name>`, sending nothing, and the message says how to remove the existing policy or adopt it with `cdkd import`. When the policy cannot be read (`AccessDenied` on `s3:GetBucketPolicy`), cdkd warns and creates as before. A rollback replay that restores a policy this deploy removed puts it back with a warning, and a retry whose earlier `PutBucketPolicy` got no definite answer from AWS proceeds when the bucket holds the policy that attempt sent.
