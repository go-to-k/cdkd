- **A Route 53 alias record that also declares `TTL` / `ResourceRecords` is refused as in CloudFormation (issue [#6544](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6544))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/route53/shared.ts` (`withTemplateAliasConflicts`), `route53/record-set.ts`, `route53/record-set-group.ts` and a new unit file. cdkd dropped `TTL` and `ResourceRecords` from any record carrying `AliasTarget`, so an `AWS::Route53::RecordSet` (or a `RecordSetGroup` record) declaring both deployed with cdkd while CloudFormation fails it: Route 53 refuses the combination ("Expected exactly one of [AliasTarget, all of [TTL, and ResourceRecords] ...]", measured). A create or a template-path update now sends the declared values, so Route 53 refuses it the same way. A delete, a rollback replay and `drift --revert` keep dropping them, so a recorded alias stays deletable and restorable. A stack an older cdkd deployed with such a record must drop `TTL` / `ResourceRecords` from it before its next update of that record (or, for a `RecordSetGroup`, of the group); destroying it still works.
