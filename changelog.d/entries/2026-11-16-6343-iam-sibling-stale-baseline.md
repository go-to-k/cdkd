- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports an IAM attachment or group membership that a sibling resource stopped writing, and `cdkd drift --revert` no longer re-adds it (issue [#6343](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6343))** -- `src/provisioning/iam-sibling-managed-policies.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/observed-capture.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/{deploy-flow,execute}.ts`, `docs/cli-drift.md`, plus new arms in the `iam-sibling-managed-policy-drift` integ. A role, user or group's `ManagedPolicyArns`, a user's `Groups`, or a managed policy's `Roles` / `Users` / `Groups` that the template sets could keep, in its recorded snapshot, a member another resource in the stack wrote. This happened when the snapshot was read just after that resource's write, or by a cdkd before #6324 / #6328. When that resource later stopped writing it, `cdkd drift` reported the member as lost while the resource itself had not changed, and `--revert` added it back. That resource is an `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` that dropped the principal, was replaced under a new ARN or was deleted, a `UserToGroupAddition` that dropped the user, or a role, user or group that dropped the policy. After a replaced policy, `--revert` failed with `NoSuchEntity`. Now the deploy that stops the write also removes the member from the other resource's snapshot, unless that resource's own template lists it, or the stopping resource stays in AWS (a `Retain` policy, or an old copy a replacement did not delete). A snapshot that already went stale before this release is re-read with `cdkd state refresh-observed`.
