- **`cdkd local` emulates an execution role that does not trust you (issue [#6201](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6201))** -- `src/local/role-permission-emulation.ts` (new), `src/cli/options/emulate-role-via.ts` (new), the role hops of `local invoke`, `local start-api`, `local run-task` and `local invoke-agentcore`, unit tests and docs. Before, a role CDK creates (trusting only its service principal) refused `--assume-role` / `--assume-task-role` with AccessDenied, and cdkd ran with the caller's own credentials (or `start-api` / `run-task` stopped). Now cdkd reads the role's inline and attached managed policies and gets credentials for the caller with them as session policies: `sts:GetFederationToken` for an IAM user, or `sts:AssumeRole` of the new `--emulate-role-via <arn>` for any other caller. It warns that this is an approximation (resource-based / key policies and `aws:PrincipalArn` conditions naming the role, its permissions boundary and session tags are not reproduced; a GetFederationToken session cannot call IAM or STS). Over the session-policy limits (10 managed ARNs, 2,048 characters, `PackedPolicyTooLarge`), for a role in another account, or for a non-IAM-user caller without the flag, cdkd warns and keeps the old behaviour. The six engine `local start-*` commands do not emulate yet.
