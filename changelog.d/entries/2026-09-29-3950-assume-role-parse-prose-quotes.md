- **The `--assume-role` parse refusals no longer put the operand inside cdkd's own double quotes (issue [#3950](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3950), partial)** -- `src/cli/options.ts` (`parseAssumeRoleToken`), with paste cases in `tests/unit/cli/assume-role-parse-prose-quotes.test.ts`. The three refusals printed the value, its logical id and its ARN as `"${displayIdent(...)}"`, and `displayIdent`'s JSON boundary does not survive a quote around it: a `"` in the value closed cdkd's, and `$( )` or a backtick runs inside double quotes regardless when the sentence is pasted. A plain operand keeps its `"..."` and prints exactly as before, long ARNs included. Any other goes through `shellBoundedDisplay`: shown as its JSON render inside single quotes, as the resolver shows a refused `Fn::GetStackOutput` `RoleArn`, since the operator needs it to correct the flag, or described when that render holds a clause break (a cut value's `[cut: …]` marker included). An empty operand prints `'<unrenderable>'`. `record-value-prose-quotes-fence.test.ts` drops `options.ts` from its allow-list.
