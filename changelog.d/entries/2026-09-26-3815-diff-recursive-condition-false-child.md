- **`cdkd diff --recursive` previews a nested stack whose `Condition` turned false as a delete of its whole subtree (issue [#3815](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3815))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive.ts` (`computeStackDiff` now returns its condition-pruned `effectiveTemplate`; `buildDiffTree` walks nested children and collects the `[via CC API]` annotations from it), with unit tests in `tests/unit/cli/diff-recursive.test.ts`. The walk read the raw template, so a nested row whose `Condition` evaluated false, with its child still in state, showed as a DELETE on the parent while the child itself was diffed against its current template as if it stayed live. That child's template-driven refusals, such as a resource `Type` change into or out of `AWS::CloudFormation::Stack`, then counted toward exit `3` over a deploy that deletes the child and never runs its template. The child now goes through the same state-only DELETE subtree as a nested stack removed from the code, matching what `cdkd deploy` does.
