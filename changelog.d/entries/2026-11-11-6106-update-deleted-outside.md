- **A failed update of a resource deleted outside cdkd now says so, instead of pointing at a rollback that cannot succeed (issue [#6106](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6106))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/rollback/settle.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/execute/rollback-after-failure.ts` and the new `src/deployment/deploy-engine/rollback/deleted-outside.ts`, plus a new `update-deleted-outside` integ. Before, when an update failed because its resource had been deleted outside cdkd (in the console, say), the automatic rollback kept the failed operation and printed `Revert it with: cdkd rollback <stack> --revert-failed`. That command re-applies the previous properties through the same update, fails the same way, and then asks for another re-run, without end. Now, after a failed update, cdkd reads the resource; when AWS reports it gone, the deploy names it as deleted outside cdkd and prints the remedy `cdkd drift` gives for such a resource (remove it from the app and deploy, then restore it and deploy again), and it keeps no `--revert-failed` record for it. Any other read outcome (the resource exists, the read fails, or the type has no read) keeps the previous behavior.
