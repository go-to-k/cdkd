- **`cdkd deploy`: a CloudWatch Logs subscription filter whose Lambda destination was just created retries the permission check instead of failing (issue [#6989](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6989))** -- `src/deployment/retryable-errors/patterns.ts`. Right after a new function and its `logs.amazonaws.com` permission are created, `PutSubscriptionFilter` can be refused for about a second with `Could not execute the lambda function. Make sure you have given CloudWatch Logs permission to execute your function.` cdkd failed the deploy on the first refusal and rolled it back, so creating or replacing a `logs.SubscriptionFilter` with a `LambdaDestination` failed now and then. cdkd now retries that refusal on its IAM-propagation schedule; a function that really lacks the permission still fails after the bounded retries. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/retryable-errors.test.ts`.
