- **An `Export.Name` spelling a secret in compatibility characters (full-width, superscript, ligature, mathematical letters) is refused instead of published (issue [#4001](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4001))** -- `src/deployment/outputs-export-alias.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/deployment/secret-detection-space-4001.test.ts`. A name like `pre-hunter2` followed by a full-width `pass` reads as the recorded secret `hunter2pass`, but no form the containment test used held it, so the name was written into `state.json` and the exports index, and `cdkd scrub --dry-run --fail` called the state clean. The test now also runs on a compatibility-folded form of the name (Unicode NFKD, nonspacing marks removed, NFKC), on top of the existing checks, so it catches strictly more than before. The printed text is unchanged; a name caught only this way is withheld from warnings, in the deploy, `cdkd diff` and `cdkd scrub` alike. Cost: a name whose folded spelling holds a recorded secret of at least four characters, in both its mark-stripped and folded forms, or equals it whole, is refused, as its plain spelling would be. Cross-script look-alikes (a Cyrillic letter for a Latin one) are not folded, nor is a secret with a Hangul jamo at either edge, or ending in an open Hangul syllable, whose other letters are spelled in compatibility characters (the edge can recompose with its neighbour in the name).
