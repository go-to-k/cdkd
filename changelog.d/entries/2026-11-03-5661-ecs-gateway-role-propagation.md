- **An `AWS::ECS::Gateway` created in the same deploy as its infrastructure role no longer fails while the role propagates (go-to-k/cdkd#5661)** -- `src/deployment/retryable-errors/patterns.ts`. cdkd creates the gateway through Cloud Control seconds after the role, and ECS rejected it with `Could not assume the infrastructure role ...` and then, once the role was assumable, `Access denied: AWS was not able to validate the provided access credentials (Service: Ecs, ...)`. Neither was retried, so the deploy failed where CloudFormation succeeds; redeploying once the role existed was the workaround. Both wordings are now on the IAM-propagation retry schedule (dense backoff, 47.75s over 26 retries, each retry a new Cloud Control create). The second is matched only as ECS relays it (`(Service: Ecs,`), so cdkd's own invalid credentials (EC2's bare `AuthFailure` sentence) still fail at once; a role that never becomes assumable fails after the retries. Tested in `retryable-errors.test.ts`; new integ fixture `ecs-gateway-role-propagation` creates the role and the gateway in one deploy.
