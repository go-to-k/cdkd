- **`cdkd destroy` now also destroys the stacks that depend on a selected stack, and destroys a dependent first (issue [#6115](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6115))** -- `src/cli/commands/destroy/select-targets.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy/command.ts`, plus a new `destroy-manifest-deps` integ. Before, destroy read only the `Fn::ImportValue` / `Fn::GetStackOutput` references in the templates and never the cloud assembly's stack dependencies. A `crossRegionReferences` reference and an explicit `stack.addDependency()` leave only a manifest dependency, so `cdkd destroy Producer` destroyed the producer alone and left the dependent deployed with references to deleted resources, and `cdkd destroy Producer Consumer` destroyed the producer first. Like `cdk destroy`, the selection now grows to every deployed stack that depends on a selected one, directly or through other stacks, and the new `-e` / `--exclusively` flag destroys only the named stacks. The order puts each dependent before the stacks it depends on, by the manifest dependencies together with the template references. The added stacks are named in the selection lines and at their own confirmation prompt. When a dependent is not destroyed (declined at its prompt, termination-protected, or failed), the stacks it depends on are kept in that run with a warning. Without a synthesized app there is no dependency graph to read, so destroy keeps to the named stacks and warns.
