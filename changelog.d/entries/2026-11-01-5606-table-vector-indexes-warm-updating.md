- **A malformed `AWS::DynamoDB::Table` `VectorIndexes` no longer deletes the recorded indexes, and a warm throughput raise no longer reports phantom drift (issue [#5606](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5606))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/dynamodb-table-provider.ts`: a changed `VectorIndexes` that is no list, or whose entry has no string `IndexName`, read as "not declared" and every recorded index got a Delete. It is now refused before any call (`vectorIndexesShapeDetail`, as `AWS::DynamoDB::GlobalTable` does); a state replay or `drift --revert` warns and leaves the indexes as they are. After a raise AWS keeps reporting the OLD `WarmThroughput` until its `Status` leaves `UPDATING` (over 4 minutes, measured on a single-region table and on a GSI), and the post-deploy baseline was read in that window, so the next `cdkd drift` reported the raise. The readback now takes the larger of the live and the declared value per member while `UPDATING`, at the table level and per index.
