- **`AWS::ServiceDiscovery::Service`: a `Description`, `DnsConfig` or `HealthCheckConfig` removed from the template is now kept on the service, as CloudFormation keeps it (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/servicediscovery-provider.ts`. `UpdateService` replaces its `ServiceChange` body rather than merging it, so the update cdkd sent for the properties still declared cleared a removed `Description` or `HealthCheckConfig`, and a removed `DnsConfig` failed the deploy (`Service dns config cannot be null`). A CloudFormation A/B showed all three are kept on removal, so the update now fills every one the template does not declare from `GetService` before the call. The type is also audited into `removalHandledInUpdate`. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/providers/servicediscovery-provider.test.ts`; the `servicediscovery` integ fixture gains two removal phases.
