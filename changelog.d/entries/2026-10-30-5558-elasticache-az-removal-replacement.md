- **`AWS::ElastiCache::CacheCluster` is replaced when `AZMode` or `PreferredAvailabilityZones` is removed from a `single-az` cluster's template, as CloudFormation does (issue [#5558](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5558))** -- `src/analyzer/replacement-rules.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/elasticache-provider.ts`. Real CloudFormation stack updates replaced a cluster whose previous template said `AZMode: single-az` for either removal, on 1 and 2 nodes, and updated a `cross-az` cluster in place. Before this change cdkd updated every such cluster in place and warned that CloudFormation would replace it; now a `single-az` cluster is replaced (a stateful type, so the deploy needs `--force-stateful-recreation`), and a `cross-az` removal stays in place and is named in the warning for properties that keep their current value.
