- **`AWS::ElastiCache::CacheCluster` is replaced when removing `AZMode` or `PreferredAvailabilityZones` leaves a `single-az` cluster with no zone list, as CloudFormation does (issue [#5558](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5558))** -- `src/analyzer/replacement-rules.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/elasticache-provider.ts`. Real CloudFormation stack updates replaced a cluster whose previous template said `AZMode: single-az` when the zone list was removed, or when `AZMode` was removed with no zone list, on 1 and 2 nodes. They updated in place a `cross-az` cluster for either removal, and a `single-az` one whose `AZMode` was removed while its zone list stayed. Before this change cdkd updated every such cluster in place and warned that CloudFormation would replace it. Now the replacing cases are replaced (a stateful type, so the deploy needs `--force-stateful-recreation`), and the in-place ones are named in the warning for properties that keep their current value.
