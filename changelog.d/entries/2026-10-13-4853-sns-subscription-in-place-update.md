- **Editing an SNS subscription's attributes updates it in place instead of replacing it ([#4853](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4853))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/sns-subscription-provider.ts`. A change to `RawMessageDelivery`, `FilterPolicy`, `FilterPolicyScope`, `RedrivePolicy`, `DeliveryPolicy`, `ReplayPolicy` or `SubscriptionRoleArn` on an `AWS::SNS::Subscription` made `cdkd deploy` unsubscribe and subscribe again. The subscription got a new ARN, messages published in between were not delivered, and an email or http(s) endpoint went back to pending confirmation. cdkd now reads the subscription and applies only the attributes that differ with `SetSubscriptionAttributes`, keeping the ARN, as CloudFormation does. An attribute removed from the template is reset to its default (`RawMessageDelivery` off, no `FilterPolicy` or `RedrivePolicy`, `FilterPolicyScope` back to `MessageAttributes`). A change to the topic, protocol or endpoint, the removal of `DeliveryPolicy`, `ReplayPolicy` or `SubscriptionRoleArn`, and a new `FilterPolicy` together with a change of its `FilterPolicyScope` (declared or dropped) still replace the subscription.
