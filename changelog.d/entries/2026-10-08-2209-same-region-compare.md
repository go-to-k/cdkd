- **A mis-cased deploy region no longer records one cross-stack producer twice, and the CloudFormation fallback client takes the canonical region (issue [#2209](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2209))** -- `src/utils/aws-partition.ts` (new `sameRegion`), `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/{cross-stack,stack-output,cfn-fallback,clients}.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-function-resolver.ts`, and a new fence `tests/unit/deployment/resolver-region-compare-fence.test.ts`. `IntrinsicFunctionResolver.resolverRegion` stays RAW (it keys the stack's own state), while values derived from it are folded, so a bare `===` between two region values disagreed whenever the operands arrived by different paths. Under `--region US-EAST-1`, a producer read once without a `Region` and once with `Region: us-east-1` was written twice into `state.outputReads`, and likewise into `state.imports` across the exports-index and state-scan arms. The CloudFormation fallback built `ListExports` with the raw region and `DescribeStacks` with the folded one, so one region held two cached clients and a mis-cased spelling reached the SDK's case-sensitive endpoint resolution. Every region equality in the resolver family now goes through `sameRegion`, which folds both operands at the comparison and moves no state key. The CFn client memo and the `DescribeStacks` cache fold their region key. The fence refuses a bare region comparison in the resolver family, `!==` and either operand order included. A comparison that genuinely means "this exact string" carries `// allow-raw-region-compare: <reason>`.
