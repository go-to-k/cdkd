- **A reader of a resource named from a secret is no longer updated again on the deploy after that resource is updated (issue [#4802](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4802))** -- `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/getatt.ts` (`recordProducerSecretPairs`), `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/context.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/resolver-context.ts`. When a deploy updated an SQS queue named from a secret, a reader of its `Fn::GetAtt Queue.Arn` was served the provider's fresh ARN in plaintext and stored it that way, while a reader of an unchanged queue stores the reference form (go-to-k/cdkd#4800). The next deploy's diff reads the queue's stored, reference-form ARN, so every such reader was updated once more. A reader served an attribute of a resource resolved in the same deploy now records that resource's own secret pairs its value carries, and stores the reference form too, when the resource keeps its physical ID. A deploy that creates or replaces it still stores the plaintext in its readers and in a stack output reading it (an export alias carrying it is published on that deploy and withheld on the next), as before.
