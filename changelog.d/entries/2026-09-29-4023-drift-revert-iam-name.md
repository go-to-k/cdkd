- **`cdkd drift --revert` no longer replaces a named IAM Role or ManagedPolicy under a different name (issue [#4023](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4023))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/iam-role-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-managed-policy-provider.ts`, `src/types/resource.ts` (`desiredFromAwsReadback`), `docs/cli-drift.md`. Both providers derive the physical name inside `update()` and replace the resource when it differs from the recorded one. The revert runs outside the deploy's stack-name and prefix scope, and without an observed baseline its desired bag carries the template name. So on a stack deployed with `--prefix-user-supplied-names` (live `MyStack-my-role`, template `my-role`) a revert created a new role `my-role` and deleted the live one. On a revert (`desiredFromAwsReadback`) both providers now keep the recorded name, since a role or policy name cannot change in place. The drifted properties are still reverted on the live resource, and a mismatching name is reported in a warning rather than applied; a drifted `Path` (or policy `Description`), which would need a replacement, now fails that resource's revert instead. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift-revert-iam-name-scope.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/iam-role-provider.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/iam-managed-policy-provider.test.ts`; integ `drift-revert` step 6e.
