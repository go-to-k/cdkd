- **A nested stack's replaced resources are now deleted only after the whole stack tree's deploy succeeds (issue [#6398](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6398))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine/nested-cleanup.ts`, `src/deployment/nested-cleanup-scope.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/execute.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow/no-change.ts`. A nested child ran its cleanup phase (#6374) as soon as the CHILD finished, still inside its parent's deploy, while a sibling child or the parent could still reference the old resource through a parameter. Renaming a `ScheduleGroup` in one child deleted the schedules another child had not moved yet; when the parent then failed, the rollback came back to an empty group. The root stack now runs every nested child's cleanup after its own, on success only, as CloudFormation runs a nested stack's cleanup at the end of the root's update; the line naming the nested stack precedes each. Each child's pass runs under that child's lock against a fresh read of its state, and drops from it exactly the superseded entries it deleted. A root deploy that fails or is interrupted sends none of them: the children's old resources stay recorded, and the parent's rollback takes them back. Integ: `tests/integration/nested-cleanup-at-root`.
