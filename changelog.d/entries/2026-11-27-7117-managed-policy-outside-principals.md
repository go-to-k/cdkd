- **Deleting an `AWS::IAM::ManagedPolicy` no longer detaches it from roles, users and groups outside the stack (issue [#7117](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/7117))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/iam-managed-policy/**`. A removal or `cdkd destroy` detached the policy from every principal IAM reported, so a role that another stack or an operator had attached it to lost its permissions without a warning. As in CloudFormation, the delete now detaches only the roles, users and groups the policy declares, and when another principal still holds it, detaches nothing and refuses with a message naming each one and how to detach it. The record is kept, and a re-run after the detach deletes the policy. A record whose principal lists cdkd cannot read (a secret reference) still detaches every principal, as before.
