- **Renaming an `AWS::S3Tables::Namespace` no longer demands `--force-stateful-recreation` (issue [#2539](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2539))** -- `src/provisioning/stateful-types.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`, `tests/unit/provisioning/stateful-types.test.ts`.
  - The type was on the stateful-replacement guard list on the fail-safe side of an unmeasured question: whether AWS's `DeleteNamespace` cascades to the tables inside.
  - A live probe on 2026-09-25 settled it: AWS refuses to delete a namespace that still holds a table (`BadRequestException: The namespace that you tried to delete is not empty.`), and the Cloud Control delete fails the same way. cdkd's delete enumerates no tables, so a namespace replacement cannot destroy one. The replacement creates the new namespace first; if the old one still holds tables, its delete is refused and cdkd warns and continues, leaving the old namespace and its tables in AWS, untracked by state.
  - The entry, its row in the always-stateful table, and the matching doc paragraph are removed; a unit case pins the exclusion. `AWS::S3Tables::TableBucket` and `AWS::S3Tables::Table` stay guarded.
