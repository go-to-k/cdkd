- **A Firehose delivery stream now applies a changed `DeliveryStreamEncryptionConfigurationInput` on update (issue [#5357](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5357))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/firehose-provider.ts`. `update()` never read the property, so turning server-side encryption on, off, or onto another key deployed green while the stream kept its old setting. It now calls `StartDeliveryStreamEncryption` for a new or changed block and `StopDeliveryStreamEncryption` for a removed one, as CloudFormation does (measured), and waits for `ENABLED` with the requested key, or `DISABLED`; an `ENABLING_FAILED` / `DISABLING_FAILED` report fails the update, non-retryable, with AWS's failure description, masked. A transition already in flight is waited out first, and the call is skipped when the stream already holds the requested state. No call is sent once the resource timeout has expired or after Ctrl-C, and the wait ends with that timeout; when the wait after a sent call fails, a warning says the stream may no longer match the recorded block. `readCurrentState` now reports the key only while encryption is in effect or being enabled. A template block that is not an object with a string `KeyType` is refused before any call, and a rollback replaying an unreadable recorded block leaves the encryption alone with a warning. A stack that added the block on update before this fix keeps a stream recorded as encrypted that is not: `cdkd drift` now shows it, and `cdkd drift --revert` applies the recorded block (or `cdkd drift --accept`, then a deploy).
