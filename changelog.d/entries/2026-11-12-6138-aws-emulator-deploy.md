- **`--aws-emulator [url]` (env `CDKD_AWS_EMULATOR`) on every command that talks to AWS: `bootstrap`, `deploy`, `diff`, `destroy`, `rollback`, `gc`, the `state` commands and the rest (issue [#6138](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6138))** -- `src/cli/aws-emulator.ts`, `src/cli/program.ts`, `src/provisioning/cloud-control-provider.ts` + `cloud-control/wait.ts`, `src/assets/docker-asset-publisher.ts`. Under the flag every AWS call goes to AWS Emulator with its keys and CA only: `--profile`, `--role-arn` / `CDKD_ROLE_ARN`, `AWS_PROFILE` and the credential environment are ignored with one notice, and the process environment (`AWS_ENDPOINT_URL`, `AWS_CONFIG_FILE`, `AWS_SHARED_CREDENTIALS_FILE`) names the emulator, so no request reaches real AWS. Cloud Control status is polled every 100 ms under the flag (the real-AWS backoff is unchanged). Docker image assets are refused under the flag before anything is built, since the emulator's ECR names the real `amazonaws.com` registry. **Behavior change:** on `cdkd local` commands, `--aws-emulator` now also drops `--profile` and the host credential variables, and its malformed-value refusal runs from the program-level hook.
