- **A throttled IAM call is retried for longer, so a role is left behind less often (go-to-k/cdkd#5652)** -- `src/utils/aws-clients.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-create-client.ts`. Deploying a large app whose parallel stacks create many IAM roles, or several apps to one account at once, throttles IAM. At the SDK's 3 attempts a throttled `AttachRolePolicy` / `PutRolePolicy` failed the role's create, the cleanup `DeleteRole` was throttled too ("Failed to clean up partially-created IAM role"), and the create's replay then failed `EntityAlreadyExists` against the role the first attempt left. The shared IAM client and the create client now take `IAM_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (8), as API Gateway's client does. Tested in `aws-clients.test.ts`.
