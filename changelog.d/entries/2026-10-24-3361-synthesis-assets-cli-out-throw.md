- **A failure value that cannot be converted to text no longer replaces the failure being reported in the macro-expansion, asset-publishing and retry-mark handlers** (`src/synthesis/{macro-expander,failed-stages,assembly-reader}.ts`, `src/assets/{asset-manifest-loader,file-asset-publisher}.ts`, `src/analyzer/security-diff.ts`, `src/cli/{yaml-cfn,options}.ts`, `src/cli/commands/retire-cfn-stack.ts`, `src/deployment/retryable-errors/marks.ts`, go-to-k/cdkd#3361). `String()` throws for a null-prototype object, and these handlers built their message with it, so a `TypeError: Cannot convert object to primitive value` took the place of the handler's own error when an SDK call rejected with such a value: a rejected macro-expansion `CreateChangeSet`, a failed S3 `HeadObject` asset check (which also threw on a `null` rejection), and a provider failure that `wrapMaskedAwsError` wraps. The Stage-scoped synthesis error, the cloud-assembly and asset-manifest read failures, the security-diff failure (`SECURITY_DIFF_FAILED`), the template-parse failures and the `--resource-timeout` per-type parse failure are converted as defence in depth: nothing known today throws them a non-`Error`. Each now goes through `describeAwsFailure(x).detail` or `safeStringify(x)`, byte-identical for every value `String()` converts and a placeholder otherwise. Test: `tests/unit/string-outthrow-3361.test.ts`.
