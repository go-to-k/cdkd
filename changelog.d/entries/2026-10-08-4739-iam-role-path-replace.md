- **Changing an IAM role's `Path` is a replacement through the engine's guards, not a deploy failing with `EntityAlreadyExists` (issue [#4739](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4739))** -- `src/analyzer/replacement-rules.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-role-provider.ts`. `Path` is create-only, but the replacement registry listed it as updatable, so the deploy sent a `path` edit on an `iam.Role` to the provider's in-place update, whose re-create asked for the name the live role still holds (IAM role names are unique whatever the path) and failed with `EntityAlreadyExists`, with no guidance. A `Path` change now replaces the role: the create-first attempt's name collision is refused with the replacement-collision guidance (the name's origin, and `cdkd deploy --replace`), and under `--replace` the role is deleted and re-created on the new path, and resources reading its ARN are updated to the new one. `/` and an absent `Path` are the same path and replace nothing. The provider's `update()` refuses a `Path`-only change as not updatable in place instead of attempting the colliding create. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-iam-role-path-4739.test.ts`, `tests/unit/analyzer/replacement-rules-iam-role-path.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/iam-role-provider.test.ts`.
