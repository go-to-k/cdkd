- **The `cdkd local` `--assume-role` messages, two intrinsic-resolver refusals, the bootstrap-marker and asset-storage refusals and the Scheduler degraded-delete warning no longer put a record or template value inside cdkd's own quotes (issue [#3950](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3950), partial)** -- `src/cli/commands/local-invoke.ts`, `local-invoke-agentcore.ts`, `local-start-api.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-function-resolver.ts`, `src/assets/asset-storage.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/scheduler-schedule-provider.ts`, with paste cases in `tests/unit/cli/local-assume-role-prose-quotes.test.ts`, `tests/unit/assets/asset-storage.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/scheduler-schedule-provider.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/intrinsic-functions.test.ts` and `intrinsic-getstackoutput-cross-account.test.ts`.
