- **`AWS::SQS::QueuePolicy` naming several queues is now cleared from every queue it wrote on destroy, and from a queue an update drops from `Queues` (issue [#4594](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4594))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/sqs-queue-policy-provider.ts`. `cdkd destroy` cleared only the first queue (the physical id) and an update ignored the previous list, so the other queues kept granting what the policy granted. The physical id stays the first queue URL; create and update now also record every queue they wrote in a `cdkd:WrittenQueues` attribute, which `delete()` clears and `update()` uses to clear the queues the new list drops (a gone queue is tolerated). A record without the attribute (written before this change, or imported) needs no migration: its other listed queues are cleared only while they carry the recorded policy document, compared by content, because a failed create's revert (issue [#4583](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4583)) or a failed update's revert lists queues it may never have written; one that cannot be read is named in a warning. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/sqs-queue-policy-provider-multi-queue-4594.test.ts`; integ: `sns-subscription-filter` (two-queue policy shrink, grow, destroy).
