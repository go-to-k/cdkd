- **Removing `AvailabilityZoneImpairmentPolicy` from an `AWS::AutoScaling::AutoScalingGroup` now turns zonal shift off, as CloudFormation does (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/asg-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/asg-provider-update-removal.test.ts`, `tests/integration/launchtemplate-asg-inplace/`. Before this change, cdkd sent nothing for a removed policy, so the group kept zonal shift enabled and `cdkd diff` reported no changes afterwards. A real CloudFormation A/B showed that CloudFormation sends `ZonalShiftEnabled: false` with the previous `ImpairedZoneHealthCheckBehavior`, after which AWS reports no policy; cdkd now sends the same. The same A/B confirmed that CloudFormation keeps a removed `DesiredCapacity` and `ServiceLinkedRoleARN`, and sends only the newly declared member on a `LaunchTemplate` / `MixedInstancesPolicy` or `VPCZoneIdentifier` / `AvailabilityZones` switch, which is what cdkd already did.
