- **`cdkd diff` and `cdkd deploy --verbose` mask a secret-derived name that only a property's OLD value holds (issue [#3869](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3869))** -- `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/masking.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/deploy-flow.ts`, `src/deployment/secret-name-needles.ts`. Consider a metric filter whose create-only `LogGroupName` moves from a log group named from a secret to an ordinary one. Its row is a replacement, and its old side is the state record's copy of the secret group's name. Nothing in the new template reads that group, so no resolution of the diff recorded the name. The `requires replacement (from <old> to <new>)` line of `cdkd diff --verbose` and of `cdkd deploy --verbose` printed it, and so did `cdkd diff`'s row. A new helper, `storedSecretNameNeedles`, takes the name spellings of each record named from a secret, judged from state and the stack's `NoEcho` values, wherever another record stores one. Both diffs now mask with them: `cdkd diff` on every line and row of the stack, including an orphan record the preview adopts, and the deploy on the replacement line. A live nested stack's stored values are masked with its parent's secret-named records too, as a deleted one's already were. Masking only: nothing persisted, compared or sent changes. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/diff-secret-name-needles-3869.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/deploy-engine-replacement-line-secret-name-3869.test.ts`. Integ `secret-derived-immutable-names` step 5c moves a metric filter off a secret-named log group under `cdkd diff --verbose` and `cdkd deploy --dry-run --verbose`, and fails when either log names the group.
