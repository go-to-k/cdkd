- **Deleting a failed `AWS::SQS::QueuePolicy` or `AWS::SNS::TopicPolicy` journal entry no longer clears a queue or topic whose policy was replaced by a different one (issue [#4612](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4612))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/{sqs-queue-policy,sns-topic-policy}-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/{region-check,policy-document-content}.ts`, `src/types/resource.ts`, `src/deployment/{rollback-executor,delete-outcome}.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/{types,journaled-orphans}.ts`. A policy that wrote some targets and then failed is journaled under exactly those (#4583). The automatic rollback, `cdkd rollback`, `cdkd destroy` and a later successful deploy cleared (or reset) each by name, so a target another policy or an outside write had since replaced lost that policy. The failed-CREATE delete now marks the entry a proven orphan and hands over a lazy re-resolution of its redacted attempted bag; the provider clears a queue, or resets a topic to its default, only while its live policy matches the attempted document by content, IAM-equivalent spellings folded (SQS stores a bare account id as its root ARN). Anything else is left with a warning and reported (`leftInPlace`), so the rollback and the deploy exit `2` (`cdkd destroy` warns only); so is a target the settling deploy itself just wrote. A missing or masked document, or a secret reference known to fail (not found, or refused for good: a refusal cdkd marks non-retryable) settles the entry, every target named, nothing cleared; any other failure (an unreadable policy, credentials, throttling) keeps it as a failed delete. A gone target is region-checked first; a one-target entry no longer widens; a record's own delete and update are as before.
