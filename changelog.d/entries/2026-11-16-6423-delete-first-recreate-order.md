- **A rollback now re-creates a delete-first replacement only after the resources it depends on are reverted (issue [#6423](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6423))** -- `src/deployment/rollback-executor/plan.ts`. A rollback reverses completed changes newest first. When a deploy updated a resource's dependency and then replaced the resource delete-first under the same name, the rollback re-created the old resource before reverting that dependency. AWS then refused the re-create against the dependency's new configuration. Renaming a `ScheduleGroup` under `--replace` hit this: the schedule's target role already trusted the new group, so re-creating the schedule in the old group failed, and the schedule was left absent. The rollback now moves such a re-create after the reverts of the resources its old record depends on.
