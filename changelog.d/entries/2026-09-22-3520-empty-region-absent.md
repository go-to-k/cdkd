- **An empty stack name or region in a malformed-`resources` message is now treated as ABSENT rather than rendered as `<unrenderable>` (issue [#3520](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3520), PR #3525)** -- `malformed-resources-bag.ts`. `safeStackName('')` and `safeRegion('')` both yield the `<unrenderable>` stand-in, so a builder handed `''` rendered `State for 'S' ('<unrenderable>')` in the clause and `--stack-region '<unrenderable>'` in the command it tells the reader to paste -- a flag that selects no record at all, in a message whose whole job is to send them to the damaged one. `undefined` was always handled correctly: the region clause and the flag are both dropped, which is right for a legacy `version: 1` record predating the region-prefixed key layout. **No caller can reach this today** (`cdkd state` throws on a falsy region, `cdkd export` normalises at its call), so this states a contract rather than fixing an output. **Behavior change:** five exported builders now normalise `'' -> undefined` at their boundary, so `''` renders identically to `undefined` there. Sixteen others spell their own identity clause and are NOT covered; they are named in the fence rather than filtered out of it, and swept by go-to-k/cdkd#3526. At the boundary, not in the shared helpers: `malformedOrphanResourcePropertiesRefusalMessage` measured a helper-only fix as a half-fix, and a floor in `stackClause` / `inspectCommand` was tried here and removed after measuring that no unfixed builder reaches them for its identity clause, so it reddened nothing. The fence is per builder for the same reason -- an aggregate case stays green for a fix applied to only some -- and the builder list is derived from the source, so one added later is classified as fenced or as known-unfixed instead of joining the ungated side silently.
