- **An empty stack name or region in a malformed-`resources` message is now treated as ABSENT rather than rendered as `<unrenderable>` (issue [#3520](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3520), PR #3525)** -- `malformed-resources-bag.ts`. `safeStackName('')` and `safeRegion('')` both yield the `<unrenderable>` stand-in, so a builder handed `''` rendered `('<unrenderable>')` in its clause and `--stack-region '<unrenderable>'` in the command it tells the reader to paste -- a flag that selects no record at all. `undefined` was always right: the clause and the flag are both dropped, which is what a legacy `version: 1` record needs. **No caller can reach this today** (`cdkd state` throws on a falsy region, `cdkd export` normalises at its call), so this states a contract rather than fixing an output. **Behavior change:** five exported builders now normalise `'' -> undefined` at their boundary, so `''` renders identically to `undefined` there. Three more are already correct by their exactness gate, which rejects `''`, and are fenced for that property rather than trusted; the remaining thirteen spell their own identity clause and are NOT covered — they are named in the fence rather than filtered out of it, and swept by go-to-k/cdkd#3526. At the boundary, not in the shared helpers: `malformedOrphanResourcePropertiesRefusalMessage` measured a helper-only fix as a half-fix, and a floor in `stackClause` / `inspectCommand` was tried here and removed: three unboundaried builders do reach them, but each passes `exact ? x : undefined` and `''` is never exact, so they already receive `undefined` and the floor reddened nothing. The fence is per builder for the same reason -- an aggregate case stays green for a fix applied to only some -- and the builder list is derived from the source, so one added later is classified as fenced or as known-unfixed instead of joining the ungated side silently.
