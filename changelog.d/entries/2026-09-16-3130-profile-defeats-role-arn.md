- **`--role-arn` is now the identity every AWS call cdkd makes runs as, even when `--profile` is also passed (issue [#3130](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3130))** -- `src/utils/aws-client-defaults.ts`, `src/utils/role-arn.ts`, `src/utils/aws-clients.ts`, `src/local/ecs-secrets-resolver.ts`, `src/cli/commands/local-*.ts`, `src/cli/options.ts`, `docs/cli-reference.md`, `docs/cross-stack-internals.md`, `docs/getting-started.md`, `docs/troubleshooting.md`. `applyRoleArnIfSet` published the assumed credentials through the `AWS_*` env vars alone, and `@aws-sdk/credential-provider-node` skips the env provider whenever a profile is selected -- so `--profile ci --role-arn <r>` assumed the role, reported it assumed, then ran every call as `ci`, in whatever account that was; an ambient `AWS_PROFILE` did it with no flag. Clearing `AWS_PROFILE` would not have fixed it (cdkd also threads `profile` as an explicit client-config key, which outranks the environment), so the credentials go to `awsClientDefaults()` instead -- spread FIRST at all 157 client sites under `src/**`, so each takes the role unless it opts out or carries its OWN credentials -- 18 sites on the `cdkd local` surface opt out deliberately, to keep the emulated workload on the caller's identity. Published once only: a second publish is refused and the `AssumeRole` hop itself opts out, so no run chains role onto role. What `cdkd local` resolves FOR the emulated workload keeps the caller's identity, now fenced at every client site on that surface including `new AwsClients(...)` bags.
