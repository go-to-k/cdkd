- **Replacing a nested stack now requires `--force-stateful-recreation` (issue [#2548](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2548))** -- `src/provisioning/stateful-types.ts`, `docs/cli-deploy-safety.md`, plus `stateful-types.test.ts` and `diff-calculator-createonly.test.ts`.
  - `AWS::CloudFormation::Stack` joins the stateful guard. Its `StackName` is immutable in the registry schema, so a `StackName` edit deployed with `--prefer-sdk-route AWS::CloudFormation::Stack:StackName` diffed as a replacement. The refusal that followed recommended `--replace`, and the delete-first path it takes destroyed every resource in the child stack with no consent flag. The replacement is now refused before anything is created, until `--force-stateful-recreation` is passed.
  - `AWS::EC2::Instance` and `AWS::SQS::Queue` deliberately stay off the guard: the instance's root volume is ephemeral by design (persistent data belongs on the guarded `AWS::EC2::Volume`) and a queue's backlog is transient.
