- **`cdkd destroy` no longer writes control characters from a stack name or a state record to the terminal in its spinner and interrupt notice (issue [#3811](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3811))** -- `src/utils/live-renderer.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/utils/live-renderer.test.ts` and `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-empty-state-lock.test.ts`. The live area's task labels and multi-stack `[stack]` prefix, and the first-Ctrl-C notice of the empty-state cleanup, interpolated S3-derived values raw, so a crafted state key or record could move the cursor, clear lines or reorder text while resources were being deleted. The live renderer now passes every label and stack prefix through `displaySafe` at draw time, covering the deploy-side spinner as well, and the notice renders the stack name through `displayStackName`. Ordinary values render unchanged.
