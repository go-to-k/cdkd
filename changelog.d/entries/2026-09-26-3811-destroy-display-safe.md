- **`cdkd destroy` no longer writes control characters from a stack name or a state record to the terminal in its spinner, confirmation prompt and interrupt notice (issue [#3811](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3811))** -- `src/utils/live-renderer.ts`, `src/cli/commands/destroy-runner.ts`, `docs/cli-destroy.md`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/utils/live-renderer.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-empty-state-lock.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-sigint.test.ts` and `tests/unit/cli/destroy-runner-events.test.ts`. The live area's task labels and multi-stack `[stack]` prefix, the per-stack confirmation prompt (both wordings) and its non-interactive refusal, the first-Ctrl-C notice and the no-longer-empty refusal of the empty-state cleanup, the `Acquiring lock` line, and the retained-resource and slow-delete lines printed above the live area interpolated S3-derived values raw, so a crafted state key or record could move the cursor, clear lines or reorder text, including on the line where the operator approves the delete. The live renderer now passes every label through `displaySafe` at draw time, covering the deploy-side spinner as well; the logical ids and types in those lines take `displaySafe`, and every stack name above renders through `displayStackName`. As a result the prompt and the refusals show a plain stack name without the surrounding double quotes (`destroy stack MyStack`), and a name that is not a plain identifier JSON-quoted. Labels are otherwise unchanged.
