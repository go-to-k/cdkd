- **`cdkd deploy --update-policy` honors `AutoScalingRollingUpdate`, `AutoScalingInstanceRefresh` and `AutoScalingReplacingUpdate`, and a rollback reverts the same rollout (issues [#6437](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6437), [#6457](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6457), [#6472](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6472))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/asg/*.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/**`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/{types,replay-revert,replay-failed}.ts`, unit tests, `src/analyzer/diff-calculator/update-policy-replacement.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff.ts`, `docs/supported-features.md`, `docs/cli-deploy*.md`, `docs/cli-diff.md`, `docs/wait-modes.md`. Still opt-in; without the flag every deploy is unchanged. With it, a group update changing `LaunchTemplate`, `LaunchConfigurationName`, `VPCZoneIdentifier` or `PlacementGroup` runs the declared rollout: a rolling update becomes an instance refresh the deploy waits for (cancelled and failed on a failed or timed-out refresh); an `AutoScalingInstanceRefresh` starts the refresh it declares on a `LaunchTemplate`, `MixedInstancesPolicy`, `VPCZoneIdentifier`, `AvailabilityZones`, `AvailabilityZoneIds` or `PlacementGroup` change; and `AutoScalingReplacingUpdate` (`WillReplace: true`, which wins) replaces the group create-first at plan time, so `cdkd diff --update-policy` (new flag pair) and `--require-approval` show it and the group's readers move to the new group, deleting the old group once the new one's instances are in service. A named group, a stack without an instance id, `AutoScalingInstanceRefresh` beside `AutoScalingRollingUpdate`, or a Cloud Control-routed new group is refused before anything changes. A rollback revert runs the same rollout back (journaled on the operation, no journal version bump) and re-sends every recorded size, as CloudFormation does. Integ: `tests/integration/asg-update-policy/`.
