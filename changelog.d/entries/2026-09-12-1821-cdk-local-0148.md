- **`cdk-local` 0.147.7 -> 0.148.4 closes a wrong-region-host bug and stops a resolver error's text reaching the user** (issue [#1821](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1821)). Two upstream user-visible deltas, both previously pinned by cdkd tests asserting the OLD behaviour on purpose:

  **Partition table.** cdk-local's table predated the three rows [#1764](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1764) added, so every `cdkd local` path reaching `derivePseudoParametersFromRegion` — `Fn::Join` `Code.ImageUri` on `local-start-api`, `local-invoke-agentcore` (x3), `lambda-resolver` — resolved `us-isof-` / `eu-isoe-` / `eusc-` regions as COMMERCIAL even spelled canonically, synthesizing hosts like `<acct>.dkr.ecr.us-isof-south-1.amazonaws.com` that do not exist. They now resolve `aws-iso-f` / `csp.hci.ic.gov`, `aws-iso-e` / `cloud.adc-e.uk`, `aws-eusc` / `amazonaws.eu` — identical to cdkd's own table. `tests/unit/local/intrinsic-image.test.ts` stopped asserting the commercial answer and now asserts the correct literals AND agreement with `derivePartitionAndUrlSuffix`, the invariant #1821 was really about. Commercial regions unaffected.

  **Resolver error text.** A `--from-state` / `--from-cfn-stack` cross-stack lookup that THROWS no longer puts the error's `message` into the per-key unresolved reason; the reason names the export and the stage, reports a character count, and points at `--verbose`. Same hazard [#2803](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2803) closed on the import path — a thrown message can carry an assembled secret and the audit entry is rendered to the user. `tests/unit/cli/local-invoke-cross-stack.test.ts` now drives a canary and asserts it is ABSENT.

  Changed: `package.json`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, those two tests, `.claude/rules/layout-local.md`. Supersedes [#2725](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/pull/2725), whose CI failed on exactly these four cases.
