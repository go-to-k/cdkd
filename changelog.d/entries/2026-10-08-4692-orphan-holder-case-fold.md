- **A successful deploy and `cdkd rollback` no longer delete a journaled failed-CREATE resource that a state record holds under its identifier spelled in another case (issue [#4692](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4692))** -- `src/deployment/replacement-name-holder/name-keys.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/{journaled-orphans,plan,replay-reverse-replacement}.ts`. RDS, DocumentDB, Neptune and ElastiCache identifiers are case-insensitive, and a provider records the identifier as the template spells it. Every check that keeps a journaled orphan because a record holds it compared physical ids exactly, so a `cdkd import` adopting the cluster journaled as `MyCluster` under `mycluster` (in the same stack under another logical id, or in another stack under the same state prefix) left it unheld: the settle proved the identifier still named the journaled cluster and deleted it. Those checks (this stack's records, other stacks' records and rollback-orphan records, the supersede pass, the rollback classifier, a failed rollback re-create's cleanup, and the `--remove-protection` ownership proof) now compare under the type's case rule (`physicalIdKey`): case-folded for the types whose names already fold for replacement-name decisions, exact for every other type. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/journaled-orphans-case-fold-4692.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/replacement-create-orphan.test.ts`; integ `rds-fix-forward-orphan`.
