- **A physical name derived from a secret no longer prints in the lines of the resources that read it, or in the engine's own lines about it (issue [#3869](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3869))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/{masking,resolver-context,provision,create,update,outputs,update-in-place}.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/{context,getatt,refs}.ts`, `src/deployment/resource-secrets-scope.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/names.ts`. When a resource's name came from a secret or a `NoEcho` parameter (resolved this deploy, or still recorded as a `{{resolve:` reference after a rotation), a `Ref` / `Fn::GetAtt` to it resolves to a name or ARN no masker recognised. `cdkd deploy --verbose` printed it on the resolver's `resolved to` line, and the reading resource's provider lines, errors and events printed it too, a deleted reader's included. Examples: an SQS queue named from a secret, read by a Scheduler target or a queue policy. The read value and the name's spellings (the rollback's `secretDerivedNamePairs`) are now masked in those lines. The resource's own lines mask them too, from its create onward, including `Deleting old <id>` and `was replaced: <old> -> <new>`. No persisted value, sent value or diff changes: the needles are print-only. A nested-stack row keeps them out of its child's bag, and the outputs and masked-input fingerprint passes do not take them. An event's `physicalId` field stays exact. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/{derived-name-needle-3869,deploy-engine-derived-name-needle-3869,deploy-engine-delete-guard-events}.test.ts`. Integ: `secret-derived-immutable-names` gains a queue policy and a role that read the secret-named queue and policy.
