- **A physical name derived from a secret no longer prints in the lines of the resources that read it, or in the engine's own lines about it (issue [#3869](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3869))** -- `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/{masking,resolver-context,provision,outputs,update-in-place}.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/{context,getatt,refs}.ts`, `src/deployment/resource-secrets-scope.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/names.ts`. When a resource's name came from a secret (resolved this deploy, or still recorded as a `{{resolve:` reference after a rotation), a `Ref` / `Fn::GetAtt` to it resolves to a name or ARN no masker recognised: `cdkd deploy --verbose` printed it on the resolver's `resolved to` line, and the reading resource's provider lines, errors and events printed it too (an SQS queue named from a secret, read by a Scheduler target or a queue policy). The read value and the resource's name spellings (the rollback's own, `secretDerivedNamePairs`) are now masked in the reader's lines. The resource's own lines mask them as well, including `Deleting old <id>` and `was replaced: <old> -> <new>`, and so do its error and event text. Nothing persisted, diffed or sent changes; the outputs pass and the masked-input fingerprint pass are unchanged, and an event's `physicalId` field stays exact. Tests: `tests/unit/deployment/{derived-name-needle-3869,deploy-engine-derived-name-needle-3869}.test.ts`; integ `secret-derived-immutable-names` gains a queue policy and a role reading the secret-named queue and policy.
