- **A rollback of a log group whose state record spells `RetentionInDays` falsy now completes instead of being refused (issue [#3141](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3141))** -- `src/types/resource.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/logs-loggroup-provider.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/logs-loggroup-provider-retention-coercion.test.ts`, `tests/unit/deployment/{rollback-executor,rollback-executor-secret-redaction,deploy-engine-provider-secret-masker}.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/commands/rollback.test.ts`, `docs/provider-development.md`, `docs/provider-rules.md`, `.claude/rules/{provider-diff-record-folds,provider-replay-and-refusals,layout-deployment}.md`. Since issue [#2699] `applyUpdate` refuses a `RetentionInDays` of `0` / `'0'` / `false` / `null` on a template bag, admitting `0` only under `UpdateContext.desiredFromAwsReadback`. The rollback executor's two revert arms hand `update()` the journaled `previousState.properties` and set no such flag -- correctly, since that bag is a template recorded earlier, not an AWS readback -- and `UpdateContext` had no `replayingState` counterpart to `CreateContext`'s. So a record spelling the retention falsy (a pre-#2699 template cdkd accepted, or `drift --accept` writing a readback `0` onto a record with no `observedProperties`) was REFUSED where it used to take the delete arm: the rollback op failed and the resource stayed at the failed deploy's retention. `UpdateContext` now carries `replayingState`, set by both revert arms, and under it the provider reproduces what the record CERTIFIES -- a spelling the writing binary read as a positive integer through `Number()` (`'0x1e'`, `'30.0'`) is RESTORED as that integer, everything else takes the delete arm, both with a warning naming the spelling. This is the update twin of the create-path downgrade #3137 shipped. Template bags and `drift --revert` keep the refusal unchanged; the flag licenses the downgrade and nothing else.
