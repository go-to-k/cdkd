- Removing `DeletionProtection` or `BackupRetentionPeriod` from an `AWS::DocDB::DBCluster`, `DeletionProtection`, `IamAuthEnabled` or `BackupRetentionPeriod` from an `AWS::Neptune::DBCluster`, or `AutoMinorVersionUpgrade` from an `AWS::Neptune::DBInstance` no longer resets the live value: cdkd now leaves it untouched, as CloudFormation does (measured in a real CloudFormation A/B). A removed `DeletionProtection` previously switched protection off. Changed `docdb-provider.ts`, `neptune-provider.ts`. Issue [#5004](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5004).
