- **`cdkd destroy --remove-protection` now waits out a busy DocumentDB DBCluster, Neptune DBCluster or Neptune DBInstance before flipping its DeletionProtection (issue [#5995](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5995))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/{docdb,neptune}/cluster-delete.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/neptune/instance.ts`. The `ModifyDBCluster` / `ModifyDBInstance` that turns DeletionProtection off before the delete, and the one that turns it back on when the delete then fails, were sent once, so a cluster that was `backing-up` (its first automated backup after create) refused the flip with `InvalidDBClusterStateFault: DB cluster isn't available for modification with status backing-up.`: the delete that followed was refused as protected, and a refused re-enable left the guard off. cdkd now waits for the resource to read `available` and resends, within 10 minutes or what the resource timeout leaves, as the RDS DBCluster / DBInstance flips already do. A terminal status, and any other refusal, still fails at once.
