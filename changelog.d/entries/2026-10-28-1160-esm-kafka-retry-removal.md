- **`AWS::Lambda::EventSourceMapping`: a `MaximumRetryAttempts` / `MaximumRecordAgeInSeconds` removed from a provisioned-mode Kafka mapping is now named as kept the way CloudFormation keeps it (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-eventsource-provider.ts`, with unit tests in `tests/unit/provisioning/lambda-eventsource-provider-removal-audit-1160.test.ts` and a new Phase 2b assertion in the `lambda-esm-self-managed-kafka` integ fixture. A CloudFormation A/B on a self-managed Kafka mapping in provisioned mode showed that CloudFormation sends neither key when the template drops them, and the mapping keeps its values. cdkd already kept them, but its warning said CloudFormation's answer was not measured. The warning now says the mapping keeps its current values, as CloudFormation keeps them, and that `-1` means no limit. When the same update also leaves provisioned mode, CloudFormation's answer is still not measured, and the earlier warning stays.
