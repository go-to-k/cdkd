- **A DocDB or Neptune DBCluster that breaks while its security groups change now fails the deploy at once (issue [#5419](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5419))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/docdb-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/neptune-provider.ts`. After a `VpcSecurityGroupIds` change cdkd waits for the new groups to apply; when the cluster entered a terminal status (`incompatible-network`, `inaccessible-encryption-credentials`, ...) or `DescribeDBClusters` returned no cluster, that wait kept polling for its full 30 minutes before failing. It now fails on the first such read, naming the cluster and its status. A member instance that never leaves `modifying` after the change now times out naming that instance and the cluster.
