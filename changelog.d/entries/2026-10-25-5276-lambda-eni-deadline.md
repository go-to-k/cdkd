- **Destroying a VPC-attached Lambda function no longer fails after 30 minutes when AWS is slow to release its network interface (issue [#5276](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5276)).** The wait for the function's hyperplane ENIs had a 30-minute budget, the same as the default per-resource deadline, which also has to cover the VPC detach and the delete. A slow release therefore hit the deadline and failed the delete (`timed out after 30m during DELETE`) before cdkd could give up on the ENI and leave it to the Subnet and SecurityGroup deletes as intended. The ENI wait now ends 2 minutes before the per-resource deadline, including one set with `--resource-timeout`. Source: `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-function-provider.ts`.
