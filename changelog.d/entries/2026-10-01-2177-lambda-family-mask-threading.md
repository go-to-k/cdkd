- **The Lambda Url, LayerVersion, Permission, EventInvokeConfig and EventSourceMapping providers mask secret-resolved values in their own log lines and failure messages (issue [#2177](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2177), Lambda family slice)** -- `src/provisioning/providers/lambda-{url,layer,permission,event-invoke-config,eventsource}-provider.ts`, `src/utils/lambda-function-name.ts`, `scripts/check-provider-update-context.ts`; tests in `tests/unit/provisioning/lambda-family-providers-masked-log.test.ts`. Their `create()` / `update()` logged through a logger no deploy-engine mask reaches, and wrapped AWS error text unmasked. Every line and wrapped AWS text now goes through the deploy's masker, extended so a function, layer or qualifier name taken from a secret is also masked where it sits inside a physical id or ARN or AWS quotes it back, at any length and by its bare name as well as its ARN, including a name recorded from a rotated `{{resolve:...}}` secret. A wrapped failure the mask changed still retries on AWS's wording (the unmasked AWS error stays its cause). The EventInvokeConfig re-spelling refusal masks the function ARNs it quotes. `AWS::Lambda::Permission` and `AWS::Lambda::EventInvokeConfig` `update()` now take the update context, and the Permission re-add passes on the masker only. Not caught: a secret under four characters inside AWS error text when it is not a name these providers derive. `delete()` still has no masker (issue [#2007](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/2007)).
