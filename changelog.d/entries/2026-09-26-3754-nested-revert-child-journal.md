- **A rollback now reverts a nested stack it reports restored (issue [#3754](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/3754))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/nested-stack-provider.ts`, `src/deployment/nested-child-journal.ts` (new), `src/deployment/deploy-engine.ts`, `src/cli/commands/rollback.ts`, `src/state/s3-state-backend.ts`, `src/types/rollback-journal.ts`, with unit tests and the `nested-stack-rollback` integ fixture. When a parent deploy failed after a nested child had deployed, the rollback reverted the `AWS::CloudFormation::Stack` row by re-deploying the child's CURRENT template, which changed nothing, and printed "restored successfully" while the child kept the failed deploy's configuration. A nested child's successful deploy now keeps its completed operations and pre-deploy outputs in its rollback journal until the top-level deploy succeeds; the parent's revert replays the child's segments for the same deploy run (recursing into grandchildren) and fails the row when no such record exists or an operation fails. Standalone `cdkd rollback` of a nested row now works instead of throwing. The top-level stack's successful deploy deletes every nested child's journal, and a settled rollback drops the segments it consumed.
