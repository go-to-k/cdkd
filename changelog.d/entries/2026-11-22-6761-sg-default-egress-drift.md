- **`cdkd drift` reports a security group's default allow-all egress rule revoked out of band (issue [#6761](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6761))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ec2/read-state.ts` (`sgRuleLists`), `tests/unit/provisioning/ec2-sg-default-egress-drift-6761.test.ts`, `tests/unit/provisioning/ec2-provider-readcurrentstate.test.ts`, `tests/integration/drift-sg-sibling-rules/`, `docs/cli-drift.md`. A group whose template declares no `SecurityGroupEgress` keeps the allow-all rule AWS creates with it, as CloudFormation's group does, but the readback filtered that rule out, so a group whose rule was revoked (all outbound traffic blocked) read the same as one that still had it, and an egress rule added out of band to such a group was not reported either. The rule is now read back and recorded in the drift baseline: revoking it, or adding a rule, is drift, and `--revert` restores the rule. A baseline recorded by an earlier cdkd holds no egress rule for such a group and reports nothing until `cdkd state refresh-observed --yes` re-reads it. Groups that declare egress (CDK's `allowAllOutbound` either way) are unchanged.
