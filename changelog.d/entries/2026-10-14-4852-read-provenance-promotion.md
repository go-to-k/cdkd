- **A reader left on the value of a resource replaced in an earlier deploy is updated (issue [#4852](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4852), part 3 of 3)** -- `src/analyzer/read-provenance-promotion.ts`, `src/analyzer/diff-calculator.ts`, `src/deployment/deploy-engine/read-provenance-moved.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/cross-stack.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/replay-props.ts`, `src/cli/commands/diff-recursive.ts`. A queue named from a secret and replaced after the secret rotated stores its `Arn` as `arn:...:{{resolve:...}}`, so a reader of it compared equal to its record and kept the deleted queue's ARN whenever the replacement and the reader's update did not happen in the same deploy: an interrupted deploy, a nested stack's child reading it through a parameter, a cross-stack consumer, or a producer updated in place earlier. With the state schema v12 record, `cdkd deploy` and `cdkd diff` now compare the secret versions a reader recorded with the ones its upstream records, promote it (`cdkd diff` labels it `[read value moved upstream]`), and send it, naming why. A rotation alone still sends nothing. A cross-stack consumer resolves a reference at the version the producer's output records (one read of the producer's state per deploy, only where needed), and a rollback restores a reference at its record's version. Records without the v12 field behave as before. Integ: `secret-derived-immutable-names` (5h-5j), `cross-stack-secret-import`.
