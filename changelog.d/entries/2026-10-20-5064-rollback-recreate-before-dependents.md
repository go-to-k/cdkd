- **A rollback re-creates a replaced resource's old copy before reverting what references it (issue [#5064](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5064))** -- `src/deployment/rollback-executor.ts`, `src/deployment/rollback-executor/plan.ts`, `replay-reverse-replacement.ts`, `replay-scope.ts`. When a replacement gave the resource a new physical id (a generated name at its next generation, or a renamed resource), `cdkd rollback` and the automatic rollback reverted the resources that moved onto it (an IAM managed policy's `Roles`, an inline policy's role) BEFORE re-creating the old resource, so they named one AWS no longer had ("The role with name ... cannot be found") and the rollback exited with a partial failure. The old resource is now re-created before the first revert that may reference it (and before a replaced resource its own old properties reference), and the new one is deleted after those reverts, as CloudFormation does. A same-physical-id re-create and a delete-first reversal keep their order.
