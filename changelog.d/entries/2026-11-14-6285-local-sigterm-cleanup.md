- **`cdkd local run-task`, `cdkd local invoke` and `cdkd local invoke-agentcore` clean up on SIGTERM as they do on Ctrl-C, and exit `143` (issue [#6285](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6285))** -- `src/utils/shutdown-signals.ts`, `src/cli/commands/{local-run-task,local-invoke,local-invoke-agentcore}/command.ts`, `docs/cli-reference-exit-codes.md`, `docs/local-*.md`. The teardown was registered on SIGINT only, so a SIGTERM (`kill`, `timeout`, an IDE stop button, a CI cancellation) skipped it: `run-task` left its containers and per-task docker network behind, and the next run failed on an overlapping subnet; `invoke` and `invoke-agentcore` left their container running. SIGTERM now runs the same teardown and exits `143`; on `run-task` a second signal of either kind is the force-exit, as a second Ctrl-C was. The exit-code pages also now match the shipped cdk-local engine: `start-service`, `start-alb`, `start-cloudfront`, `start-agentcore`, `start-web-function` and `start-microvm` exit `143` on SIGTERM. Tests: `tests/unit/utils/shutdown-signals.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/local-run-task-command-body.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/local-invoke-stdout-stream.test.ts`, `tests/unit/cli/local-invoke-agentcore-stdout-stream.test.ts`, and the `local-run-task` integ fixture's SIGTERM step.
