- **`cdkd drift` reports a managed policy, inline policy, permissions boundary or group membership added out of band to an IAM role, user or group whose template declares none, and `--revert` removes that one member (issue [#6627](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6627))** -- new `src/analyzer/drift-undeclared-grants.ts`, `src/cli/commands/drift/compare.ts`, `revert-outcome.ts`, `plan.ts`, the `drift-verbatim-readback` integ fixture, and `docs/cli-drift.md`. On the template-only baseline the comparator walked only the template's keys, and on the observed baseline an undeclared `ManagedPolicyArns` / `Groups` (or any of these keys captured empty) was skipped, so an `AdministratorAccess` attached to such a role read clean and `--revert` kept it. `ManagedPolicyArns`, `Policies`, `PermissionsBoundary` (role, user) and `Groups` (user) are now compared on both baselines when the template omits them: a live member nothing recorded is drift, `--revert` removes it per member (one detach / inline-policy delete / group removal / boundary delete each; none of these keys replaces the principal), and the revert plan lists each removed member. A member a sibling record of the same stack writes is still neither drift nor removed; one whose live document was edited out of band is reported as a change and `--revert` restores the sibling's recorded document. An attachment of a same-stack managed policy whose template sets that principal kind's list is that policy's drift, not the principal's. A member granted on purpose from another stack or tool is adopted with `cdkd drift --accept`. Under these keys a recorded member AWS no longer holds is not reported and never re-granted (go-to-k/cdkd#6328's class); a recorded permissions boundary removed out of band is reported and put back.
