- **`cdkd drift --accept` and `--revert` write a NoEcho parameter's position as `***` in every baseline they save (issue [#4043](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/4043), Phase C)** -- `src/cli/commands/drift.ts`, `docs/cli-drift.md`. Both writers clone the record's existing baseline (`observedProperties`, or `properties` when there is none) and merge into it what was accepted or what the provider reported after a revert. A baseline that still held a `NoEcho` value in the clear at a position the record names in `noEchoLeaves`, or a provider echo of the live value a revert kept there, was written back as it came, and a value the redaction's value arm cannot match (under 4 characters, a number) stayed in the clear. Every such position is now masked in what either writer saves: by its list's identity field in an observed baseline (the whole list where none pairs it), and by index in `properties`. What `--accept` reports as recorded is unchanged. Tests: `tests/unit/cli/drift-noecho-v11.test.ts`.
