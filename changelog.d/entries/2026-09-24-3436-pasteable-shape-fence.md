The fence keys on the SHAPE: a `cdkd` verb and an interpolation inside a prose `'...'` span, a span assembled from two or more holes, or a bare `<placeholder>` with words after it. Against the merge base it reports 12; all are fixed, so the tree reports ZERO and the test asserts that -- a new one fails by name. It is a SOURCE-shape check and its header enumerates the bounds: a lone re-wrapped `'${built.command}'` and a value quoted at RUNTIME are outside it. What changes for users: a bare `<stack>` followed by a flag is two shell REDIRECTIONS, so pasting `cdkd events prune <stack> --all` where a file named `stack` exists exited 0, did nothing, and created a file called `--all`; those placeholders are quoted now. `cdkd export`'s second redaction-mask refusal, the twin of one gated three PRs ago, moves its `cdkd import` remedy onto a labelled line too. `cdkd drift`'s three remaining prose remedies and its untracked-properties plan note give the stack AND the region on a labelled line, gated by one predicate shared with the rest -- closing #3307's last three sites and its `--stack-region` requirement. `state.ts`'s three `Usage:` lines, `cdkd destroy --help` and `cdkd drift`'s revert summary now spell arguments the way Commander does.
