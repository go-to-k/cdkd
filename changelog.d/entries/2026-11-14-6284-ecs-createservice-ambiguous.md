- **An ECS service that `CreateService` made before the call itself failed is no longer left unrecorded, wedging its cluster's rollback and destroy (issue [#6284](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6284))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/service-create.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/service-create-ambiguous.ts` (new). When `CreateService` throws with no definite answer -- a 2xx whose body the AWS SDK cannot read, a 5xx, or a reset or timeout after the request was sent -- cdkd now looks the service up by the name it sent in that cluster. A service there, when the pre-create lookup measured the name free, is recorded on the failed create, so the automatic rollback, `cdkd rollback` and `cdkd destroy` delete it, and the cluster's deletion no longer fails with `ClusterContainsServicesException`. A service that held the name before the create is never touched; without that measurement (`--no-wait`, a rollback replay, a failed pre-create lookup) a service created during the attempt is only reported with commands. When the lookup cannot answer, a `CreateService` that answered 2xx with the name measured free is deleted by name; its delete command is printed first. Anything else is reported with `aws ecs describe-services` and `aws ecs delete-service` commands naming the region. A 4xx refusal or a throttle runs no lookup. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/ecs-service-create-ambiguous-6284.test.ts`.
