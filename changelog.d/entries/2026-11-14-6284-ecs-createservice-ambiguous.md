- **An ECS service that `CreateService` made before the call itself failed is no longer left unrecorded, wedging its cluster's rollback and destroy (issue [#6284](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6284))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/service-create.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/ecs/service-create-ambiguous.ts` (new). When `CreateService` throws with no definite answer -- a 2xx whose body the AWS SDK cannot read, a 5xx, or a reset or timeout after the request was sent -- cdkd now looks the service up by the name it sent in that cluster. A service there that did not hold the name before the create (the pre-create lookup measured it free, or its `createdAt` falls inside the attempt) is recorded on the failed create, so the automatic rollback, `cdkd rollback` and `cdkd destroy` delete it, and the cluster's deletion no longer fails with `ClusterContainsServicesException`. A service that held the name before the create is never touched. When the lookup cannot answer, a `CreateService` that answered 2xx with the name measured free is deleted by name; anything else is reported with the `aws ecs describe-services` and `aws ecs delete-service` commands. A 4xx refusal or a throttle runs no lookup. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/ecs-service-create-ambiguous-6284.test.ts`.
