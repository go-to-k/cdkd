- **`CDKD_SDK_CALL_LOG` now counts S3 calls (issue [#5219](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/5219))** -- `src/utils/sdk-call-log.ts` (`quietS3Logger`), `src/utils/aws-clients.ts`, `src/utils/bucket-region-client.ts`, `src/deployment/intrinsic-resolver/stack-state.ts`, two new unit files. The S3 clients replaced the call log's counting logger with a silent one (to hide the SDK's "Stream of unknown length" warning), so every state, lock, exports-index and refactor-marker call was missing from the log. They now keep the counting logger while the log is on; the warning stays hidden either way.
