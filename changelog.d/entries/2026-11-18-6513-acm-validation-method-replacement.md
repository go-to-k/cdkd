- **A certificate's `ValidationMethod` change is reported and made as a replacement (issue [#6513](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6513))** -- `src/analyzer/replacement-rules/rules.ts`. ACM cannot change an `AWS::CertificateManager::Certificate`'s validation method, and CloudFormation and the AWS CDK CLI's diff treat the change as a replacement, but the registry schema leaves `ValidationMethod` out of `createOnlyProperties`. `cdkd diff` showed it as an update, `--fail-on destructive` and `--require-approval destructive` did not stop on it, and the provider replaced the certificate inside its own update, deleting the old one before resources that read its ARN (a CloudFront distribution, a load balancer listener) moved to the new one. The change is now a replacement the deploy engine makes, as for the certificate's other immutable properties: the diff marks it `[requires replacement]`, and the old certificate is deleted after its readers point at the new one.
