- **An `AWS::IAM::Policy` delete or update no longer strips its inline policy from a same-named role, group or user someone else made (issue [#6655](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6655))**. The policy is removed from each principal by name, and a destroy deletes it before the role it names, so the role's own `RoleId` compare came too late. Each principal the policy names that is a record of the same stack with a recorded identity id (`RoleId` / `GroupId` / `UserId`) is now read first (`GetRole` / `GetGroup` / `GetUser`, one per such principal per policy delete, at most 5 at a time) and compared; a holder with another id is sent nothing, the policy is still removed from every other principal, and the delete then refuses non-retryably, naming the principal's logical id and the attribute (no value), record kept, with the `cdkd state orphan` remedy. An update that removes a principal from the policy's `Roles` / `Groups` / `Users` reads that leaving principal the same way, before any write; a holder with another id is sent nothing and warned about (the one cdkd created is gone with its inline policy), and the update completes. A principal outside the stack, or one whose record holds no usable id (written by an older cdkd, or masked), is not read and is handled by name as before. Files: `src/provisioning/providers/iam-policy/{delete,update,principal-identity}.ts`, `src/deployment/stack-records-scope.ts` (comment).
