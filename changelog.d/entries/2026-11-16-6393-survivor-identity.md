- **A rollback no longer re-adopts someone else's same-named IAM role as the stack's own (issue [#6393](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6393))** -- `src/deployment/rollback-executor/replaced-survivor.ts`. When reversing a replacement, the rollback checks whether the old resource still exists, and re-adopts it if so. That check asked only whether something answered the old name. If the old role had been deleted out of band and another party created a role with the same name, the rollback adopted that role into the stack and deleted the stack's new one, and a later `cdkd destroy` would have deleted the stranger's role. The rollback now compares the role's AWS-minted `RoleId`, recorded at create, with the live one. When they differ it refuses with `REPLACED_SURVIVOR_UNPROVEN`, sends nothing, and keeps the journal.
