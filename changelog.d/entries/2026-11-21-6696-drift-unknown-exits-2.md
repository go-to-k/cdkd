- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports "no drift" for a resource it could not compare (part of issue [#6696](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6696))**. **Behavior change for CI:** a detection run (no `--accept` / `--revert`) over a stack holding a drift-unknown resource (`? <id>`, counted as `unsupported`: a type no provider can read back, such as `AWS::EC2::LaunchTemplate` or `AWS::CloudWatch::AnomalyDetector`) now exits `2` ("the comparison was incomplete") when nothing drifted, where it exited `0`. A run that also found drift still exits `1`. A job that accepts a known gap can treat `2` as a pass and fail on `1`. Custom resources (`Custom::*`, `AWS::CloudFormation::CustomResource`), a nested stack's row its own block compares, and the `unresolvedToken` / `noEchoParameter` causes keep exit `0`. The summary line no longer prints `✓ ... no drift detected` over a drift-unknown resource: it reads `⚠ <stack> (<region>): no drift detected in N resources checked, but M resources could not be compared (M unsupported, drift unknown)`. A `✓` line over a stack holding custom resources now ends with ` — excludes K custom resources, which cdkd does not drift-check`, after the unchanged `(N resources checked, M unsupported)` counts. `--accept` / `--revert` keep their exit codes; over a drift-unknown gap they print `Comparison INCOMPLETE ...` instead of `No drift detected — nothing to accept/revert.` `--json` is unchanged (drift-unknown resources stay in `notSupported`).
