- **`cdkd drift` no longer reports an IAM attachment or group membership that another resource in the stack writes, and `cdkd drift --revert` no longer re-grants or removes it (issue [#6328](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/6328))** -- `src/cli/commands/drift/baseline-keys.ts`, `src/provisioning/iam-sibling-managed-policies.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-managed-policy/read-state.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-managed-policy-provider.ts`, `src/provisioning/providers/iam-user-group/read-state.ts`, `src/cli/commands/drift/{compare,revert-outcome}.ts`, `docs/cli-drift.md`, plus new arms in the `iam-sibling-managed-policy-drift` integ. Before, a user's group membership written by an `AWS::IAM::UserToGroupAddition` could land in the user's recorded baseline, because the baseline read raced the addition. When the addition later dropped the user, `cdkd drift` reported it and `--revert` added the user back to the group. A managed policy also reported the roles, users and groups that attach it through their own `ManagedPolicyArns` (`role.addManagedPolicy(mp)`), and `--revert` detached them. Now, as in CloudFormation drift, a user's `Groups`, the `ManagedPolicyArns` of a role, user or group, and a managed policy's `Roles` / `Users` / `Groups` are compared only when the template sets them on that resource. When it does, the members a sibling resource currently writes are left out on both sides and from `--revert`, unless the template lists them too; a member a sibling has since stopped writing, on a declared list, is tracked in #6343. Existing stacks are fixed without a redeploy. An out-of-band change to a list the template does not set is no longer reported.
