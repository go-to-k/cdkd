- **`AWS::CertificateManager::Certificate`: removing `CertificateTransparencyLoggingPreference` from the template now resets it to `ENABLED` (issue [#1160](https://github.com/go-to-k/cdkd/issues/1160))** -- `src/provisioning/providers/acm-certificate-provider.ts`. Before, the removal was silently dropped: `UpdateCertificateOptions` keeps an absent option, so a certificate opted out of transparency logging stayed opted out while `cdkd diff` reported no changes. CloudFormation documents the removal as the same as declaring `ENABLED`, so the provider now declares that reset through `removalDefaults`. `CertificateExport` cannot be updated after a certificate is created; the deploy already replaced the certificate on its change or removal (a createOnly property), and the provider's own `update()` now does the same instead of sending an `Export` ACM rejects or dropping the removal. The type is also audited into `removalHandledInUpdate`, so a removed property cdkd does not handle is warned about. Tests: `tests/unit/provisioning/acm-certificate-provider.test.ts`.
